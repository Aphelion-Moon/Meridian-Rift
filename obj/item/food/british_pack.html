british_pack
/obj/item/food/british_pack

Grab-and-go food from Mrs. Luvvit's. You can eat it straight from its container, or use it in hand to take the food out, which keeps the bites already taken and leaves the empty container.

Vendor food is junky: less nutritious than the home-cooked dish, salted or sugared, and never handmade.

Vars

served_hotWhether it comes out of the vendor piping hot
unwrapped_typeThe bare food inside, or null when it is only eaten from its container

Procs
