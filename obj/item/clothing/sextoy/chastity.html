chastity
/obj/item/clothing/sextoy/chastity

A lockable device worn in a lewd slot that closes genitals off: a chastity cage, belt or plate.

It draws in place of the genital it sits over (see device_state on the genital's bodypart overlay), so clothing, underwear and the genital's own visibility settings hide it exactly as they hide the genital. Genitals it closes off refuse interactions and other toys until it comes off, and it only comes off unlocked: with the key that comes tucked inside it, or with its lock picked. Fitting one needs the wearer's chastity preference.
