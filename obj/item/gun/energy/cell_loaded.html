proc
select_via_radial
(/mob/living/user)

Shows a radial of the installed cells and switches to the picked cell's firing mode.

Installing a cell appends its casing to ammo_type, and click_alt() removes the newest of each, so the last length(installed_cells) entries of ammo_type line up 1:1 with installed_cells.

Arguments:

  • user: The mob picking a cell.
