/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types — Var Details - Proc Details

remote
/obj/item/mod/remote

Controls its module's suit from within control_range tiles: activation, parts, modules, lock and forced emotes. Needs the wearer's sex toy preference and the holder's ERP preference.

Vars

control_rangeHow many tiles away, on the same z-level, the wearer can be controlled from.
moduleThe module whose suit this controls.

Procs

force_emoteMakes the wearer emote message for user. user's mutes, emote ban and the IC filters apply, as for the me verb.
in_reachWhether user is within control_range of the wearer, or nobody wears the suit.
on_module_deletedDrops the module when it's deleted.
takes_partWhether holder has the ERP preference on.
toy_wearerReturns the suit's wearer if they have the sex toy preference on.
