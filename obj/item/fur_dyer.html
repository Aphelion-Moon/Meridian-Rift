proc
finish_marking_dye
(/mob/living/carbon/human/target_human, zone, marking_name, color)

Recolours one marking on a body once its painting has finished.

The marking is looked up among the body's markings as they are now, which may have changed while it was being painted, and nothing is written when it is gone. The body's markings are its own, so the colour never reaches its player's saved character.

Arguments:

  • target_human: the painted body.
  • zone: the marking zone painted.
  • marking_name: the name of the marking painted.
  • color: the new colour.

Returns:

  • TRUE if the marking was recoloured. A locked marking takes no dye.
