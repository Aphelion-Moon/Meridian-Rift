proc
hidden_by_clothing
(/mob/living/carbon/human/human, drawing)

Whether clothing hides this genital: whatever covers it where it is drawn (covered_by_clothing()). A genital that shapes the body (shapes_body()) is the exception while drawn: it draws under the underclothes covering it, and only a uniform, suit or gown hides it. Examine still reads it as covered by them.

Arguments:

  • human: The genital's owner.
  • drawing: Whether its overlay asks, to draw it.
