use_onPaints with an armed can. A click on an adjacent wall or floor, a full-tile window fastened down, or graffiti on one, on the static station (graffiti_static_station(), else "can't paint here!" and the can stays armed), opens a freehand session there, continues a freehand draft on its own tile, or starts a paint job of the armed piece. A session that starts uses the arming up, and a job keeps it. An Unfinished piece whose paint still shows goes on only there: a click on another surface is refused ("wrong wall!") and starts no cooldown. Each attempt waits out GRAFFITI_START_COOLDOWN from the last, refused or not, and is refused before any costly work: by an empty can, a paint job already running, or a two-tile piece without a surface like this one to the right. A two-tile piece that paints nothing on its right tile paints one tile. A click on a poster that isn't ripped paints over the poster itself, one painter at a time, and only on its own pixels; it takes no two-tile piece ("too wide!"), and nothing resumes there. A click on anything else clears the arming and sprays as usual, as does an unarmed can, which tints a window or a poster.
