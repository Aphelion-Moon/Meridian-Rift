custom
/obj/effect/decal/cleanable/crayon/custom

One tile's custom graffiti: a stack of placed pieces, oldest at the bottom, shown as one composite with its glow.

SSgraffiti owns the placements and their art; the decal keeps the packed placements it shows and what it drew from them. It lasts while the surface it was made on does, a wall, a floor with ground or a full-tile window's glass, and it can't be painted over. A window's graffiti goes with the window, and a floor's stays under one fastened later.

Vars

kindgraffiti_surface_kind() of the tile the decal was made on, which the tile must stay: GRAFFITI_KIND_WALL, GRAFFITI_KIND_FLOOR or GRAFFITI_KIND_WINDOW.
render_cachelist("base_art" = every piece but the top one merged, "art" = the whole stack merged, "kind" = its graffiti_art_glow_kind(), "mask" = its glow mask or the sheet holding it), plus "older", whether older paint shows beneath the top piece, once examined. The merges are kept only while someone paints the tile: a stack shown from its sheet, or left by its painters, keeps neither (release_merges()), and stack_merge() and base_merge() make them again.
stackThe placements shown here, bottom to top, packed as SSgraffiti keeps them.
stack_keygraffiti_art_hash() of the merged stack, which names its sheet state and its drawn icon.

(/list/cached)

Shows the stack under the key and glow kind the map file cached for it, as the load does, without merging it: the merges come once a sheet must be drawn or someone paints the tile (stack_merge(), base_merge()).

Arguments:

  • cached: list(the stack's key, its graffiti_art_glow_kind()), from SSgraffiti's stack_cache.

Returns:

  • TRUE/FALSE: Whether it took them. A malformed entry is left for refresh() to merge instead.

proc
refresh
(full, deferred, /list/top_record)

Merges the stack again after it changed, then shows it.

Only the top piece is merged over the cached merge of the ones beneath, unless full asks for the whole stack, as a change below the top needs, or no merge of them is kept. The merge comes decoded, so its glow and icons never decode it again.

While the map loads, the arts come decoded from SSgraffiti's decoded as far as it keeps them, and it keeps the merge decoded for its sheet while its budget allows. Nobody paints during the load, so only the whole stack's merge is kept, for the sheet.

Arguments:

  • full: Whether the pieces beneath the top one changed.
  • deferred: Whether to leave the icon for SSgraffiti.build_sheets() to set.
  • top_record: The top piece's art decoded, when the caller has it, as a painter's composite does.
