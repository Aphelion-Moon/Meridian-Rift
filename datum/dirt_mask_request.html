dirt_mask_request
/datum/dirt_mask_request

What a surface's masks are made from: the body they're drawn on and the splats on it. Requests with the same mask_key() share their masks, so bodies drawn from the same sprites with the same splats draw the same icons. A request is made once and never changed. It keeps copies of the splats. So a splat that levels up or lands while its masks wait in the queue changes neither the key nor what's drawn under it.

Vars

bloodFor blood below a taur's waist, what it's drawn from (dirt_taur_blood_data()): on skin, the taur body's share, and on cloth, a taur-fitted garment's. Null for other masks.
clipFor cloth on underclothes, the sprites its dirt is kept to, each list(icon, icon state).
coverFor skin, the sprites worn over it, from dirt_cover_sprites(). Its dirt fades out beside them.
cutThe sprites drawn over clip's, in the same form, whose pixels the dirt is kept off.
deposit_partsFor skin holding general deposits, the parts drawn over it in some direction, lowest first (dirt_parts_over()). Each draws the deposits on its own pixels there (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/deposit_part_runs()). Null if none.
deposit_surfaceWhat general deposits are rasterised on, where that isn't surface_kind: DIRT_SURFACE_SKIN for underwear, which takes the skin's general dirt and takes mess as cloth. Null when they're rasterised on surface_kind.
facingsFor a part's masks, the directions it shows over what it lies on and draws its share in, or null for all four.
kindWhat it's drawn on: DIRT_MASKS_BODY, DIRT_MASKS_PART, DIRT_MASKS_TAUR, DIRT_MASKS_SHAPE or DIRT_MASKS_HAIR, or DIRT_MASKS_WET for sweat's multiplier (/datum/dirt_mask_request/wet).
paintedWhether the body is bare, so general deposits on its skin draw their painted designs. Only ever TRUE where they're rasterised on skin.
part_sourceFor a part's masks, the part they're drawn on. Their mess keeps to its pixels.
partsThe parts the splats reach, each a /datum/dirt_part_source, lowest first. Below the top level, the surface's mess drops a level or two where they take a share (dirt_part_body_level()), and it always leaves out the rounded parts' pixels. In each direction, a part drawn under the surface (/datum/dirt_part_source/proc/under()) has its own mess drawn in these masks, and a part drawn over it has its mess drawn on itself.
rowsFor a taur body's own dirt, the row of the rider's surface that each canvas row shows at the rider's height (dirt_height_rows()). The rider stretches and the taur body stays put. Null at a height that stretches nothing.
shape_sourceFor a taur body's, a drawn part's or the hair's dirt, the shape it's drawn on.
sideThe dirtier side, 1 (the body's right) or -1, which general deposits draw on.
splatsCopies of the surface's /datum/dirt_splat as they were when it was made, oldest first. They merge in this order.
surface_kindDIRT_SURFACE_SKIN or DIRT_SURFACE_CLOTH, or DIRT_SURFACE_FUR for a drawn part, DIRT_SURFACE_HAIR for the hair. On skin, a taur body's dirt is drawn per layer, on the taur body's own images. On cloth it's drawn over the whole canvas, inset on a taur suit.
surface_layerThe layer of the surface the masks are drawn on, which the parts are drawn under or over.
template_keyThe key of the template its zone sources build (dirt_template_key()), or null without zone sources. It's worked out once, for the masks' key and for finding the template.
zone_sourcesThe sprites the body's template is built from, as dirt_zone_sources() returns them. On a taur, the rider's.

Procs

NewArguments:
mask_keyReturns the masks' key: an MD5 of what they're drawn on and each splat's zone, level and variant, in order. A general deposit gives its cause, material, level, variant and limbs, so two materials never share masks. It adds everything else that changes what they draw, each only when there is any. Serials stay out of it, so splats that landed apart draw alike. A part's splats count at the level they draw at. A taur body's count at their own level, because the rider's mess that its pools keep clear of is drawn at that level.
read_partsReturns every part the request reads: the parts its splats reach, or the part its masks are drawn on.
read_spritesReturns the sprites a request reads besides its template, each list(icon, icon state, what for, shift). What for is "cover", "clip" or "cut". Shift is where a cover sprite's wrapper has the body's tile (dirt_cover_sprites()), or null.
