dirt_mask_request
/datum/dirt_mask_request

What a surface's masks are made from: the body they're drawn on and the splats on it. Requests with the same mask_key() share their masks, so bodies drawn from the same sprites with the same splats draw the same icons. A request is made once and never changed: it keeps copies of the splats, so a splat that levels up or lands while its masks wait in the queue changes neither the key nor what's drawn under it.

surface_kindDIRT_SURFACE_SKIN or DIRT_SURFACE_CLOTH. A taur body's pools are drawn per layer on skin (on the taur body's own images), on cloth over the whole canvas (inset on a taur suit).

taur_sourceFor a taur body's pools, the taur body they're drawn on.
zone_sourcesThe body's sprites, as dirt_zone_sources() returns them: its template's. On a taur, the rider's.

Procs

NewArguments:
mask_keyReturns the masks' key: an MD5 of what they're drawn on, each splat's zone, level and variant in order, the sprites that fade or keep them, and the parts that shape them, each with where it draws against the surface in each direction, or the part or taur body they're drawn on and the directions it draws in. Serials stay out of it, so splats that landed apart draw alike. A part's splats count at the level they draw at. A taur body's count at their own: the rider's mess its pools keep clear of is drawn at it.

Proc Details

New
(kind, /list/zone_sources, surface_kind, /list/splats, /list/parts, /datum/dirt_part_source/part_source, /datum/dirt_taur_source/taur_source, /list/cover, /list/clip, /list/cut, surface_layer, /list/facings)

Arguments:

  • kind: DIRT_MASKS_BODY, DIRT_MASKS_PART or DIRT_MASKS_TAUR.
  • zone_sources: The body's sprites, as dirt_zone_sources() returns them. A part's masks need them only for the vulva on skin, which takes the ring of the body round it.
  • surface_kind: DIRT_SURFACE_SKIN or DIRT_SURFACE_CLOTH. For a part, what it lies on.
  • splats: The surface's /datum/dirt_splat, oldest first. Copied: the request never reads them again.
  • parts: The parts the splats reach, lowest first, from dirt_surface_parts(), or null.
  • part_source: For a part's masks, the part.
  • taur_source: For a taur body's pools, the taur body.
  • cover: The sprites worn over skin, from dirt_cover_sprites(), or null.
  • clip: The sprites cloth's mess is kept to, each list(icon, icon state), or null for the whole canvas.
  • cut: The sprites drawn over clip's, in the same form, or null.
  • surface_layer: The surface's layer: a garment's, DIRT_UNDERCLOTHES_LAYER or DIRT_SKIN_LAYER.
  • facings: For a part's masks, the directions it draws its share in, or null for all four.
