dirt_skin
/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_skin
inherits /datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt

A limb's skin dirt: one container image at DIRT_SKIN_LAYER holding the limb's baked image, with that image's emissive blocker beside it. So a limb adds one of each to its person whatever the splats, and an arm adds one more of each for its hand (/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_skin/arm). It lies under the underclothes, the genitals and everything worn. The masks are its carrier's, clipped to the pixels the limb shows, fading out beside what's worn over them.
