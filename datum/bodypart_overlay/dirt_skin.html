dirt_skin
/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_skin

A limb's skin mess: one container image at DIRT_SKIN_LAYER holding the limb's baked image, so a limb adds one overlay to its person whatever the splats. It lies under the underclothes, the genitals and everything worn. The masks are its carrier's, clipped to the pixels the limb shows, fading out beside what's worn over them.
