dirt_underclothes
/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_underclothes

A person's mess on their underclothes: one container image on the chest at DIRT_UNDERCLOTHES_LAYER, over the underclothes and under the uniform, holding the baked image of each piece worn and shown, already kept to the pixels it shows.
