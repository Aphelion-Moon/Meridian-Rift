dirt_underclothes
/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_underclothes
inherits /datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt

A person's dirt on their underclothes: one container image on the chest at DIRT_UNDERCLOTHES_LAYER, over the underclothes and under the uniform. It holds the baked image of each piece worn and shown, already kept to the pixels the piece shows. Each baked image's emissive blocker draws beside the container. A piece holding sweat also draws its multiplier there (get_all_overlays()), and no blocker for it.
