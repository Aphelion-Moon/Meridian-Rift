proc
pubic_hair_clip
(/obj/item/bodypart/limb)

Returns what this pubic hair is clipped to on limb's owner, while a baring underwear (GLOB.body_hair_baring_clothing) is worn and drawn over it, its only cover. The hair is drawn past the crotch's outline to frame the genitals: over a bare groin that's as drawn, and under such underwear it would float in the air or show through the underwear's gaps.

Arguments:

  • limb: The chest the hair is drawn on.

Returns:

  • A list: the clip's key first; the underwear, then its state, female cut and whether the GAGS digitigrade mask draws its legs, all as make_appearance() draws it before any fitting; then the sheet and state the chest, right leg and left leg each draw their own sprite from (/obj/item/bodypart/proc/drawn_sheet()), both null for a part that draws none, like a taur's legs.
  • null: The hair isn't clipped. Nothing it's drawn under covers the groin, or the hair is on no one.
