dirt_read_sourceReturns the taur body as mess reads it: each of its layers' images, as get_images() draws them, their states taken together, or null when they draw nothing on the 64x32 taur canvas or its kind takes no pools: Big Legs, legs under the rider with no taur back. Read again when the accessory, its base state or the limb's gender changes.
get_overlayA taur body carrying pools (its owner's part_mess_splats in ORGAN_SLOT_EXTERNAL_TAUR) draws them with each of its layers: one container just over the layer's images, at their layer and offsets (centred on the 64x32 taur canvas as they are), holding the pools' baked image kept to the pixels that layer shows. So the container joins the layer's own holder and adds no overlay to the mob, and takes the height filter with it (dirt_container_over()).
icon_render_keyAdds the key of the pools' masks, or "pending" while they're made, after DIRT_PRIVATE_KEY, so the chest's images follow them, kept on its own mob (limb_icons_for()).
