proc
bulge_redraw_cuts
(/mob/living/carbon/human/wearer, /datum/uniform_cut, /datum/body_cut)

Draws a cover cut away for a look again when its cut is not the one the look wants (uniform_cut and body_cut, for the two slots a cover can be cut in). TRUE when it drew one again: the body update that drawing ends with has resolved the look already. A slot not drawn holds no cut.

No try here: a runtime in a render below (an accessory's worn overlay, a limb's) ends only the proc it happens in, and the render around it carries on, so the guard is always reset below. A try would abandon every frame in between and leave the cover undrawn. bulge_clear() resets the guard too, which every full resolve calls (one nested in these draws included, so a nested resolve can draw a cover once more) and so does removing the penis.

Resolves the penis's bulge look for a body update; TRUE when it draws one. proc
bulge_swap_cut
(/mutable_appearance/standing, /datum/cut, slot, /obj/item/clothing/under/uniform)

Swaps the drawn sprite of a worn appearance for its cut: the appearance itself, or the first child of the KEEP_TOGETHER root build_worn_icon() nests separate overlays under. The sprite's own children are cut with it (bulge_cut_children()), and so is what the garment draws over its sprite beside it in that root (its blood), which would otherwise draw over what the cut reveals (bulge_cut_siblings()). A uniform's root as built, uncut, is kept for the look's cloth, which carries the blood as the garment had it (bulge_over_overlays()).

Arguments:

  • standing - the appearance build_worn_icon() or make_appearance() built.
  • cut - the cut: a flap (/datum/bulge_print_template) or a loincloth's derived cut (/datum/bulge_sheet_state).
  • slot - UNIFORM_LAYER or BODY_LAYER.
  • uniform - the uniform drawn, for a uniform: its accessories keep their pixels.
