proc
dirt_share_key
(/obj/item/bodypart/limb, own_key)

Returns what the genital's own mess draws as, for keys, after DIRT_PRIVATE_KEY: its masks' key, "pending" while they're made, or null while it has none or doesn't draw as itself. Asking queues masks not made yet. Given its own render key, the part source is read again only when that changes: the key covers the genital's sprites, colours, layering and device, which are what the part source is read from.

Arguments:

  • limb: The limb it's drawn on.
  • own_key: The genital's own render key, without its mess, joined, or null to read the part source now.

proc
dirt_shown
(/obj/item/bodypart/limb, /mob/living/carbon/human/owner)

Returns whether the genital draws on a body now, from the checks can_draw_on_bodypart() makes of it: not on a husk, not closed off by a device, not hidden by its accessory (is_hidden(), which reads clothing and custom layering), and not covered by a look drawn in its place. They're read rather than asked through can_draw_on_bodypart(), which a garment's render reading the parts mustn't call: a penis's resolves its bulge look, which can draw that garment again.

Arguments:

  • limb: The limb it's drawn on.
  • owner: Its owner.
