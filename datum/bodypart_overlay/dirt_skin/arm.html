arm
/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_skin/arm

An arm's skin dirt. It draws the arm's baked image at DIRT_SKIN_LAYER, as every limb does, and the hand's at DIRT_HAND_SKIN_LAYER. That layer lies over the hand and under gloves, and the hand draws over the uniform. Each layer draws only while its baked image has something to draw.
