custom_marking
/datum/bodypart_overlay/custom_marking

A drawing painted on a bodypart, clipped to the limb's own geometry. Subtypes keep a limb's zone, hand and taur paint apart.

Vars

cache_iconsWhether built icons go into the shared caches. Throwaway overlays, such as region-map scratch, skip them.
drawingPrivate drawing snapshot owned by this limb overlay.
drawing_hashFull drawing identity used to skip unchanged overlay updates.
drawing_pixel_hashPixel identity shared by cached masks with different appearance settings.

Procs

get_layer_postfixesCustom markings supply their own paint: their layer keys pick a limb silhouette, not an accessory icon state postfix.
pixel_render_keyPixel caches depend on paint and mask geometry, not tint, opacity, emission or layer.
