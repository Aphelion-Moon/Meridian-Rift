dirt_canvas_view
/datum/dirt_canvas_view
inherits /datum/dirt_surface

Every pixel of one canvas in one direction, mapped to mob space, as a surface that dirt_halo() reads. Each pixel lies on it, flagged as the torso's, a limb nothing on a canvas reads. No pixel is its rim, and no face guards any.

Vars

pixel_wHow far the canvas's left edge sits from the tile's, in pixels: negative to its left.
pixel_zHow far its bottom edge sits over the tile's floor.
