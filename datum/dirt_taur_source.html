dirt_taur_source
/datum/dirt_taur_source

A taur body's sprite as mess reads it: each of its layers' states, its colour layers' together, as the taur body draws them on the 64x32 taur canvas. A mess aimed at the back puts pools along the back these pixels show (dirt_taur_pools()). Made once and never changed.
