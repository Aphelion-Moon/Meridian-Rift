body_marking_test_lock
/datum/body_marking_test_lock

Locks a marking while the unit test that allocates this lives, and gives it its own colour mode back when the test ends. No marking ships locked: the mode stays for content that wants it, and its tests lock a tattoo, which has a colour of its own.
