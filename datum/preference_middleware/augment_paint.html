augment_paint
/datum/preference_middleware/augment_paint

The recolourable finishes' colours in character setup: their finishes and presets sent once with the rest of the constant data, with the brand styles the finish picker tries on, each slot's colours, and the editor's two actions, each answered with the colours alone. The preview's skin follows the preview mob as it is rebuilt (add_preview_answer()). The brand styles' try-on sprites are sent only when the window asks for them (send_augment_brand_sprites()).

Vars

send_augment_colors
(/mob/user)

Sends the open character setup window the augment colours.

Returns:

  • FALSE: Nothing else needs sending.

proc
set_augment_colors
(/list/params, /mob/user)

Sets one slot's colours, or with no colours its finish's default. The slot's finish must be recolourable.

Arguments:

  • params: "slot", an augment slot, and "colors", a colour string or null.
  • user: The player acting.

Returns:

  • FALSE: The window is sent the colours alone, or nothing for a refused action.
