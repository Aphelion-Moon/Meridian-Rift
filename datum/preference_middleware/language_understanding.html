language_understanding
/datum/preference_middleware/language_understanding

A language a character only understands can be followed in part, from LANGUAGE_UNDERSTANDING_MIN to all of it; the level is granted as the language holder's partial understanding, which catches that share of words, more of the common ones. See /datum/language_holder/proc/adjust_languages_to_prefs(). Speaking a language means following it.
