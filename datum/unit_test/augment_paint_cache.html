augment_paint_cache
/datum/unit_test/augment_paint_cache

Two bodies wearing one finish in different colours, or one of them without glow, draw their own: the limb icon cache is shared by every body, so a key without the colours or the glow would serve one body's arm to the other. A colour change redraws the arm in the new colours.

Procs

paint_drawnReturns the paint colour body's right arm draws, as "#rrggbb": the red row of its _paint copy's matrix, which BYOND reads back as 20 numbers. Read from the images the body's last draw was served, by its render key.
put_on_finishGives body a cyborg right arm wearing finish in colors, drawn, and returns it. The arm it replaces is deleted.
