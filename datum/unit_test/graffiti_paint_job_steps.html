graffiti_paint_job_steps
/datum/unit_test/graffiti_paint_job_steps

Procs

DestroyStops the test's paint jobs and puts the registry and the library back.
RunA paint job places nothing while its steps reveal no paint, then shows layer 1 from the left a few strips a step. A step waits in SScustom_sprite_work's queue while its overrun budget is spent, or every painter's composite share, and steps that came meanwhile land together. The first step to show paint charges the can GRAFFITI_COST, half that for a tagger, and the last strip waits for the finish, which charges nothing more. A slowed painter's steps spread over their slower do_after. The job is driven step by step: a test human's do_after ends at once, so start() would finish it.
