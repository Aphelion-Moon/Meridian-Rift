dirt_mask_queue
/datum/unit_test/dirt_mask_queue

SSdirt makes mess masks in the background and caches them: the first ask queues them, finish_all() makes them, and the body that asked is drawn again. They're made from the splats as they were when asked for, whatever happens to them meanwhile. Skin's masks are clipped to each limb and cloth's cover the canvas, and both match the parity manifest's cases, one splat or two merged, the second drawn over a first cached from an earlier ask. A request whose limb names a missing state is dropped, cached as failed, and never queued again, and the queue goes on past it. Masks asked for on more body types than the template cache holds are all made, though templates drop while they wait.
