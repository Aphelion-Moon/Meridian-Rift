dirt_mask_queue
/datum/unit_test/dirt_mask_queue
inherits /datum/unit_test/dirt_test

SSdirt makes mess masks in the background and caches them: the first ask queues them, finish_all() makes them, and the body that asked is drawn again. The masks are made from the splats as they were when asked for, whatever happens to the splats meanwhile. Skin's masks are clipped to each limb, and cloth's are made too. If a request's limb names a missing state, the request is dropped, cached as failed and never queued again. The queue goes on past it. Masks asked for on more body types than the template cache holds are all made, though templates drop while they wait.
