A person carries DIRT_MAX_SPLATS splats at most, counted across their skin, their parts and what they wear, and past it the oldest go first. A human in a jumpsuit and a labcoat, breasts drawn above all clothing, takes DIRT_MAX_SPLATS

  • 1 huge shots: on the face's skin, on the labcoat with the breasts' share, and on the jumpsuit. At the cap a landing takes the oldest splat off before anything is drawn. So a face landing that evicts a face splat asks for one mask at most. A heavy shot that tops the face's newest splat and starts another asks for exactly one. Drawn, it all adds four overlays at most. It adds nothing to tg's shared limb icon cache, as a dirty limb's images are kept on its mob.
