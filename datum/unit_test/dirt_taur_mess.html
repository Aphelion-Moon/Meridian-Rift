dirt_taur_mess
/datum/unit_test/dirt_taur_mess
inherits /datum/unit_test/dirt_test

A back shot on a taur lands on the rider's back and along the taur body's: the rider's skin and the taur body's slot each take a splat of the same variant. The game reads the rider and the taur body as the parity corpus names them. Once the masks are made, the taur body's images carry the pools beside them. A taur suit takes the pools itself, inset over its 64x32 sprite. The rider's mess on the suit lands on the body's tile, not 16 px off. Removing the taur body takes its pools with it. A taur's penis drawn only behind the body takes no share.

proc
check_rider_height
(/mob/living/carbon/human/rider, kind_name, reaches_waist, deposit_under)

Fails unless a taur body's dirt at Tall, Tallest and Short height is its Average dirt moved by the rider's height alone. The rider owns the pixels its sprites are drawn on once its chest's height filters stretch them. It doesn't own those where the taur body's layers are drawn over it, or any others. Each pixel the taur body shows at both heights keeps its Average dirt. Each pixel the rider uncovers keeps the dirt a deposit drew there under the Average rider. Else it takes the dirt of the pixel just below its run of uncovered pixels, and a glint there goes on as the body tone. It takes none if the taur body doesn't show that pixel at Average height. Each layer shows its own pixels alone, so nothing the rider covers is drawn.

Arguments:

  • rider: The taur, at Average height, holding back shots.
  • kind_name: The taur's kind, as the failures name it.
  • reaches_waist: Whether its dirt reaches its rider's waist, so a taller rider uncovers some of it.
  • deposit_under: Whether it holds a deposit drawn under its Average rider, which a taller rider uncovers in places where the dirt just below differs.

proc
