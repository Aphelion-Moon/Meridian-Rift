dirt_taur_mess
/datum/unit_test/dirt_taur_mess
inherits /datum/unit_test/dirt_genital_test

A back shot on a taur lands on the rider's back and along the taur body's: the rider's skin and the taur body's slot each take a splat of the same variant. The game reads the rider and the taur body as the parity corpus names them. Once the masks are made the taur body's images carry the pools beside them. A Big Legs body, its legs under the rider and no taur back, takes the shot on the rider alone. A taur suit takes the pools itself, inset over its 64x32 sprite, and the rider's mess on it lands on the body's tile, not 16 px off. Removing the taur body takes its pools with it. A taur's penis drawn only behind the body takes no share.
