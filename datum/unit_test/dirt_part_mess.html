dirt_part_mess
/datum/unit_test/dirt_part_mess
inherits /datum/unit_test/dirt_test

Parts take their own share of the mess aimed at them and draw it with their own images. The checks below follow a share through portals, relays, state and layer changes, bulges, underclothes put on, washes and the part's removal. A part hidden while underclothes carry mess, or drawn by a gloryhole's relay alone, doesn't shape the skin's mess.

Procs

check_bulgeA bulge over boxers covers their mess with its cloth: the mess is drawn again over the cloth.
check_bulge_jumpsuitA bulge in a jumpsuit draws the jumpsuit's mess over its cloth, and draws it again once a second shot raises it. The chest's images stay off tg's shared limb icon cache, because each mess state draws a key that never comes back.
check_bulge_washTesticles drawn behind a hard loincloth by its bulge look draw their mess there, the chest's images kept off tg's shared limb icon cache meanwhile, and lose it to a wash.
check_buttA butt shot on a bare butt: its own share facing north, none in profile, where its back image lies under the skin.
check_butt_layeringA bare butt with mess of its own, hidden under boxers, then set to draw above them: its share is read at its new layer.
check_butt_under_boxersA butt drawn below its underwear, with mess of its own facing north over the bare skin, then boxers put on: its front image draws under them now, so its share draws nothing.
check_hidden_breastsBreasts never shown while socks carry mess: the chest's mess covers where they drew.
check_hidden_buttA butt shot and a stained bra, then the butt never shown: the skin's mess is left nowhere past the body.
check_over_clothingBreasts above all clothing over a jumpsuit: each takes its share, and the breasts draw theirs.
check_part_portalA one-part portal: a shot on the penis alone, which the relay draws, and nothing else through it.
check_relayed_penisA gloryhole's occupant, whose penis the relay alone draws, takes a groin shot on their own side. The penis takes no share of it and doesn't shape the skin's mess. Once the session ends, a second groin shot gives the penis its share, and the penis shapes the skin's mess.
check_state_changesAn erection draws the penis's share again for the erect sprite, and removing the breasts takes their splats off.
check_vulva_over_boxersA vulva drawn above its underwear, with mess of its own on the bare skin, then boxers put on: it lies on them now, so its share is made again for cloth, without the ring of skin.
check_wallA wall-stuck relay shows the groin and butt only, and draws no leg, nor the mess on one. It draws the mess on what it shows: on the bare skin, the chest's included through the torso it shows, and on a jumpsuit, which takes the shot under a winter coat the relay doesn't draw. Two relays each clip what's worn to a body of their own.
check_wall_skirtA wall relay leaves out a skirt, so a shot through the wall lands where the relay shows it: on the underwear covering the zone, else the skin. A wall-stuck body draws no bulge (portal_hides_bulge()), on either.
draws_body_targetReturns whether a wall relay draws its body to its own render target (body_render_target()), as the filters that clip what's worn over the body read it.
images_atReturns how many images lie at a layer, such as a relay's copies of the uniform at -UNIFORM_LAYER.
leg_mess_masksReturns the baked icons of the legs' skin dirt containers (/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_skin), as their limbs draw them now. A relay's copy of a container is matched by these, so a filter on the copy doesn't hide it.
relay_insetsReturns how many inset images a portal's relay draws in the wrappers it copies: a garment's mess.
share_keyReturns what the chest's render key carries for the penis's share: its masks' key, "pending", or null for none.
skin_past_bodyReturns the pixels the chest's skin mask draws off the body, in any direction, each as "direction:index".
skin_shaped_byReturns whether the skin masks a carrier asks for are shaped by the part in an organ slot (dirt_surface_parts()).
