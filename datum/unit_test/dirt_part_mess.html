dirt_part_mess
/datum/unit_test/dirt_part_mess
inherits /datum/unit_test/dirt_genital_test

Parts take their own share of the mess aimed at them. Breasts drawn above all clothing take theirs over a jumpsuit, which takes the rest, and draw it with their own front layer. Through a one-part portal a shot lands on the penis alone, and the relay draws it; a wall-stuck relay draws no legs, and draws the mess on the hips and butt, on the skin and on a jumpsuit, under a winter coat too, but not a skirt, nor ever a bulge: a shot lands on what the relay shows under the skirt. A share follows the part: an erection draws it again for the erect sprite, a butt set to draw above its underwear is read again at its new layer, and a part removed takes its splats with it. A bulge's cloth over boxers draws their mess over itself, a bulge's in a jumpsuit draws the jumpsuit's again once another splat lands, and testicles a bulge look draws lose theirs to a wash. A butt shot takes the butt's share facing north, where its front image draws over the skin, and in profile, where its back image draws under it, shapes the skin's mess past the body's edge. Boxers put on over a butt drawn below them leave its share nothing to draw; put on under a vulva drawn above them, they have its share made again for cloth. A part hidden while underclothes carry mess no longer shapes the skin's.
