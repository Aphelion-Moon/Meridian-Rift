graffiti_registry_caps
/datum/unit_test/graffiti_registry_caps

An account at its placement cap loses its oldest placement to its next one, a sixth piece on a tile buries the oldest for good, and a map at its placement cap or out of art bytes loses the lowest-ranked placement of an owner who isn't connected. New art for a placement meets the same caps, its own old art going with it, and with nothing else to go it is refused and the placement keeps its old art.
