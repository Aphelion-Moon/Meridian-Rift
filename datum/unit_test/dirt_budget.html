dirt_budget
/datum/unit_test/dirt_budget
inherits /datum/unit_test/dirt_test

The heaviest body. It's dressed in every slot that takes dirt but the gloves, so a hand shows. It has a tail, wings, horns, braided hair (its braid drawn over hats too) and a butt drawn on two layers above its clothes. It takes mess to the cap, and then every general cause but the seven slime causes aimed at one zone. Each cause comes in a material of its own. Then it takes soot and sweat on its tank top, sweat on its bra, and a shot on the butt. It holds DIRT_MAX_SPLATS, and the mess, which landed first, goes first. It draws every surface's dirt within MAX_ATOM_OVERLAYS less 40. Where its mud and its oil meet on a garment, each pixel takes the thicker one's colour.

The tail, the wings and the horns draw it in a container at each of their layers. The hair and its braid draw it in a container each, and the butt's share draws in a container over its front image.
