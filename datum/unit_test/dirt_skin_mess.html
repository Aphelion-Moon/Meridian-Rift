dirt_skin_mess
/datum/unit_test/dirt_skin_mess
inherits /datum/unit_test/dirt_test

The mess on bare skin draws through the limbs' own images. A landing gives each limb it reaches one container image at DIRT_SKIN_LAYER, holding the baked image SSdirt makes, and gives the other limbs nothing. Washing takes the mess off, even while its masks are still being made. Mess on every zone at once keeps the chest's splats in its one container. The body's images share one holder per layer, and their blockers one group (prepare_bodypart_overlays()). So skin dirt adds a holder and a group, and underclothes dirt the same, four at most. Past the top level, a shot starts another splat, of a variant the zone doesn't have yet. A steady draw only compares what each is drawn from. Even so, if a limb is drawn from other sprites with no limb swapped, the masks are made again for what the body draws. The cases are the chest at another physique, a restyled arm, which the chest checks, and eyes drawn from another state.
