character_preview_height
/datum/unit_test/character_preview_height

Character setup's drawn preview shows a tall or short body where the game draws it. A pixel on the head that height stretches into place, as it does the head, and one that height lifts by an offset, as it does hair, eyes and hats, land on the same row as in a round, for a plain body, a Big Legs taur and a taur whose body keeps its size.
