bulge_manifest

Procs

manifest_accessoryWhat the renderer reads from an underclothes accessory's own definition: its sprite and state, whether its colour is static, its layer, its GAGS config, its own digitigrade sprite, for an undershirt whether it covers the groin, its GAGS colours (colours=, also the colours its digitigrade legs are drawn in), its digitigrade state (digi_state=) and how it is drawn on a female body (female=, its female_sprite_flags).
manifest_familyA garment's family as the manifest writes it: "family=" and its name under /datum/bulge_family/ ("jumpsuits", "skirt/short_reveal/hem_24"), or its whole typepath when it is not under it.
manifest_linesThe manifest's lines in order: families, sheets, garments. The base family's line lists every var a family has, and each other family the vars it gives another value. A garment lists its family (by its name under /datum/bulge_family/) and, where they are not the defaults, its suit rule, its print and its cloth layer.
manifest_wornWhat the renderer reads from a garment type's own definition, as the manifest writes it: its worn sprite and state (worn=FILE:STATE, "default" for the slot's default file; for a GAGS garment the state it takes once made, not its map preview's), its digitigrade sprite when it has one (digi=), its GAGS worn configs and colours, whether it covers the groin (groin=), the layer it is worn at when not its slot's own (layer=); for a suit with a rule, its toggled state (toggled=), worn offsets (offset=), worn dimensions (dimension=), alpha and colour (color=), which decide whether it may show a bulge (bulge_suit_verdict()); the sprites it declares for a species' body (Teshari, Vox, Vox Primalis), as files or as GAGS configs (teshari=, vox=, better_vox=, gags_teshari=, gags_vox=, gags_better_vox=); and for a uniform how it is drawn on a female body (female=, its female_sprite_flags).
