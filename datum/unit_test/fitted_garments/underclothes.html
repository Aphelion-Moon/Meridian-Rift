underclothes
/datum/unit_test/fitted_garments/underclothes

Underclothes are fitted to their wearer's fitness limbs as they're drawn, cached apart from a Normal body's, and fitted again when a limb changes: a Muscular arm refits a long-sleeved shirt, and an augment in its place draws it as it is again. A digitigrade body's Muscular leg refits the digitigrade state its underwear draws.
