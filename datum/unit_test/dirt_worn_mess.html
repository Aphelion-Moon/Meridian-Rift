dirt_worn_mess
/datum/unit_test/dirt_worn_mess

The mess on clothes. A shot at a covered region lands on the outermost garment over it, which draws it inset in its own worn wrapper, where the bulge can copy it, from its wearer's cloth masks: moved to a digitigrade lizard, from the lizard's. Washing the garment takes it off. A region covered only by underclothes takes it on the piece showing the most of it, drawn over that piece and kept to its pixels, and kept with it while it's hidden or swapped. Skin mess lies under the underclothes, and fades out beside them.
