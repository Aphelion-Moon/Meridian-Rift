dirt_drawing
/datum/dirt_drawing

What one surface's masks are made from and drawn with: a garment's cloth, one of a person's underclothes, or a part. A request is made when the surface's splats or what it's drawn on change, never on a draw.

Vars

absentWhether the surface isn't drawn now (underclothes off or hidden), until the next forget(), so draws meanwhile don't look for it again.
keyThe request's mask_key().
masksThe masks drawn: the request's once made, and until then the ones before them, if any.
parts_keyWhat the request's parts were made from, as its owner writes it, where its owner checks it.
requestWhat the masks are made from now.

Procs

currentReturns the masks to draw: the request's once made, else the ones before them, or null. Until they're made SSdirt draws body again once they are, whoever queued them.
forgetDrops the request, so the next draw makes one.
startStarts drawing from a new request. The masks before it are drawn until its own are made.

proc
current
(/mob/living/carbon/human/body, redraw_flags) as /datum/dirt_masks

Returns the masks to draw: the request's once made, else the ones before them, or null. Until they're made SSdirt draws body again once they are, whoever queued them.

Arguments:

  • body: The body they're drawn on.
  • redraw_flags: DIRT_REDRAW_* flags, what to draw again on it.

proc
forget
(drop_masks)

Drops the request, so the next draw makes one.

Arguments:

  • drop_masks: Whether to stop drawing the masks made before, which no longer fit what's drawn.

proc

(/datum/dirt_mask_request/new_request, parts_key)

Starts drawing from a new request. The masks before it are drawn until its own are made.

Arguments:

  • new_request: The request.
  • parts_key: What its parts were made from, for its owner to check, or null.
