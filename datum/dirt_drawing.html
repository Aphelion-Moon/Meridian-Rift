dirt_drawing
/datum/dirt_drawing

What one surface's masks are made from and drawn with: a garment's cloth, one of a person's underclothes, or a part. A request is made when the surface's splats or what it's drawn on change, never on a draw.

Vars

Proc Details

proc
current
(/mob/living/carbon/human/body, redraw_flags) as /datum/dirt_masks

Returns the masks to draw: the request's once made, else the ones before them, or null. If they aren't made yet, SSdirt draws body again once they are, whoever queued them. A skin's job that this queues holds the masks drawn now, so each limb whose runs are unchanged keeps its icon (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/limb_masks()).

Arguments:

  • body: The body they're drawn on.
  • redraw_flags: DIRT_REDRAW_* flags, what to draw again on it.

proc
current_wet
(/mob/living/carbon/human/body, redraw_flags, /list/garment)

Returns sweat's multiplier to draw with the masks drawn on a garment, or null for none. Once SSdirt has made the multiplier for those masks and the garment's look, this drawing holds it (wet, wet_key). One not made yet is asked for, and body is drawn again once it's made. Masks without sweat drop it, and so does forget() with the masks. So a garment washed or taken off while its multiplier is made draws none once it is. The key is worked out again only when the masks' key or the garment changes (wet_read).

Arguments:

  • body: The body it's drawn on.
  • redraw_flags: DIRT_REDRAW_* flags, what to draw again on it.
  • garment: As dirt_wet_look() takes it, or null where the masks drawn hold no sweat.

proc
drop_jobs
(/mob/living/carbon/human/body)

Drops the queued jobs of the masks this drawing asked for, as it's let go, while no other body waits for them (/datum/controller/subsystem/dirt/proc/drop_mask_job()). Those are the jobs of its request, of the request a newer one replaced (superseded_key), and of the sweat multiplier it last asked for (wet_read).

Arguments:

  • body: The body that waits for them.

proc
drop_wag
(/mob/living/carbon/human/body)

Stops drawing the wag's masks (wag). Drops their job, and the job of the request before them, if no other body waits for it (/datum/controller/subsystem/dirt/proc/drop_mask_job()). So no frames are made that nothing would draw.

Arguments:

  • body: The body drawing them.

proc
forget
(drop_masks)

Drops the request, so the next draw makes one. Its job is dropped once the next request has another key (current()).

Arguments:

  • drop_masks: Whether to stop drawing the masks made before, which no longer fit what's drawn, and their sweat's multiplier.

proc
start
(/datum/dirt_mask_request/new_request, parts_key)

Starts drawing from a new request. The masks before it are drawn until its own are made.

Arguments:

  • parts_key: What its parts were made from, for its owner to check, or null.

proc
wag_shown
(/datum/dirt_wag_source/wag_source) as /datum/dirt_masks

Returns the masks a wagging tail draws, from this, the part's drawing. Where its one frame shows the still sprite's pixels, and while its job finds out whether it does, that's the still masks. Otherwise it's the wag's own once they're made (wag), or none.

Arguments:

  • wag_source: The part read wagging, or null when it can't be.
