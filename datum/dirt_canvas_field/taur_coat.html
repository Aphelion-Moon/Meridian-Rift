taur_coat
/datum/dirt_canvas_field/taur_coat

A taur body's contact coat in one direction, as dirt_taur_coat() draws it. It's drawn some of its body's pixels at a time (draw_rows()). A mask job draws it a band a piece (dirt_canvas_field_band()) and keeps it among its fields (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/field_for()).

It draws the same pixels however it's banded. dirt_taur_coat() draws a whole coat through it too, its bands back to back, so the job's draw and the whole one are one code path. Each coat pixel is worked out from that pixel alone, and each level picks its tongues as its first band begins. Each finish runs once, on the whole level.

draw_rows
(rows)

Draws the coat's next piece. The first piece makes its view and random draws. Each piece after that draws a level's coat on as many of the body's next pixels as rows of the canvas hold. The level's first piece picks its tongues. Once the level's last pixel is drawn, the next piece is its finish, stacked on the levels before. Once the last level's finish is drawn, the view, random draws and samples go. Call it while drafting.

Arguments:

  • rows: How many pixels to draw, counted in rows of the canvas, at least one.

Returns whether every level is drawn.
