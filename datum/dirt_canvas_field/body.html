body
/datum/dirt_canvas_field/body

A plunge, a blast or a douse on one direction of a body, as dirt_rasterise_deposit() draws it, drawn some rows at a time (draw_rows()). A mask job draws it a band a piece (dirt_canvas_field_band()) and keeps it among its fields (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/field_for()). Each level's whole-body coat is bound a band of rows at a time (/datum/dirt_feature/coverage/proc/bind_rows()).

It draws the same pixels however it's banded. Each coat pixel is worked out from that pixel alone. A douse reads its tops from the whole surface on its first band. Each finish runs once, on the whole level and the whole deposit. dirt_rasterise_deposit() draws a whole deposit through it too, its bands back to back, so the job's draw and the whole one are one code path.

Binds the level's coat on its next rows. As a level begins, it makes the level's features (dirt_level_features()).
