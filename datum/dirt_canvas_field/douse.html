douse
/datum/dirt_canvas_field/douse

A douse on a shape in one direction, as dirt_douse_rasterise() draws it. It's drawn some of the shape's pixels at a time (draw_rows()), level after level. A mask job draws it a band a piece (dirt_canvas_field_band()), as many pixels as a field's band draws of the canvas. The job keeps it among its fields (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/field_for()).

draw_rows
(rows)

Draws the douse's next pixels, as many as rows of the canvas hold, level by level. It makes its random draws as it begins. Once a level's last pixel is drawn, it cleans the level up (dirt_cleanup()) and stacks it on the levels before. The next level goes on with what's left. Once the last level is stacked, hair's core turns to the body tone. Call it while drafting.

Arguments:

  • rows: How many pixels to draw, as a count of the canvas's rows, at least one.

Returns whether every level is drawn.
