/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types — Var Details - Proc Details

dirt_mask_job
/datum/dirt_mask_job
inherits /datum/dirt_job

Makes a request's masks a piece at a time (next_piece()). While it waits, SSdirt.mask_jobs holds it under its key, so a body asking meanwhile waits for it (wait()) rather than queueing it again.

Vars

deposit_featuresalist(level = its features, from dirt_cause_features()) of the general deposit being made on a body's template. The first direction to ask makes them, and the others get them unbound (dirt_level_features()). They're dropped with the deposit's fields.
deposit_samplesalist(seed = alist(scale = alist(sample_y = alist(sample_x = value)))): the noise deposit_features have sampled in the deposit's levels and directions (/datum/dirt_feature/proc/noise()), each value under its exact arguments. It's handed out and dropped with the features.

Its fields are of one type and share one table in every direction (field_for()), and it's dropped with them. A term samples by its position and the deposit's random draws, whichever way the field faces, and the canvas or template surface is the same size every way. So a value one direction kept is what sampling it again returns.

Procs

bake_frameBakes the frame being made into each layer of the wag that has it, in every direction, at the layer's own delay (/datum/dirt_wag_source/var/layer_frames). Each direction gets the carried dirt's runs on the pixels the layer shows at the frame, or a blank if there are none. Where an earlier frame shows the same pixels, it takes that frame's bake. A layer's animated icon (frame_icons) starts at its first frame with dirt, with blanks for the frames before it. Nothing is registered here, because only frame_masks() registers what's shown.
bakes_framesA wag's job (/datum/dirt_wag_source) bakes its frames if it has more than one. A one-frame wag's job is about as quick as a still tail's.
canvas_masksReturns the masks for cloth or a part. They're baked from its runs on the pixels canvas_shown() gives. They also keep the wet pixels for sweat's multiplier, which take no colour in the bake.
canvas_shownReturns the pixels cloth's or a part's masks draw, once its pieces are made. On underclothes it's only the pixels they show, alist(direction = indexes in row-major order). It's null for the whole canvas, which the garment's own pixels clip.
carry_frameReturns the dirt of a wag's frame being made in one direction, carried from its still sprite's along the tail and across it (/datum/dirt_tail_carry). So the dirt swings with the tail and draws as it does when the tail is still. Where the frame shows the still sprite's pixels (frame_from), it's the still's dirt as it is. Each piece carries DIRT_CARRY_PIXELS of the frame's pixels. It returns null until the last piece, which notes the frame's pixels and carry in sprite_frames. A kept wag's dirt is carried whole from sprite_frames (dirt_wag_carried()).
deposit_part_runsReturns the runs of skin's general deposits on each part taking them (/datum/dirt_mask_request/var/deposit_parts) that they reach: alist(its placed_key() against the skin = dirt_shown_runs()). A part takes them on its own pixels in each direction it draws over the skin. They lie where their raster lies on the body's template, as far past the body as that reaches. That's how the reference generator composites the body's dirt over the parts drawn on it. Mess stays off them, because a part takes its share of mess on itself. It takes no share of general deposits, and draws them at their own level.
direction_rastersReturns each splat's raster in one direction, in the splats' order, each as its rasteriser left it: text on a body, a /datum/dirt_pixels on a part or a drawn shape.
drop_depositDrops what the job holds for a deposit it has made in every direction: its fields with their samples, its features with their noise, and its lattice. The deposit's rasters stay in SSdirt.rasters.
field_forReturns the field a general deposit is drawn from. Each field is made once and drawn a band a piece. On the job's shape, it's the canvas field, drawn facing one way (dirt_shape_field_facing()) and made once for every direction it serves. For a douse, it's a field on the shape's pixels in one direction (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/douse). For a taur body's contact coat, on its fur or a taur suit's cloth, it's a field in one direction (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/taur_coat). On a body's template, it's a plunge, a blast or a douse in one direction (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/body). The deposit's fields in every direction share one table of its noise (field_samples). A body's fields also share the deposit's features (deposit_features).
finishedCaches the masks, or FALSE if they failed, and notes the bodies that waited for them, to be drawn again. Sweat's multipliers go in SSdirt.wet_icons. A wag's sprites, as its frames were read, go whole in SSdirt.wag_sprites.
frame_masksReturns a wag's masks once every frame is baked: each layer's animated icon with dirt, registered in the resource cache. An icon built at run time can't hold the rewind flag. So a layer that rewinds gets its frames again in reverse after the last, at their delays. Its dirt then keeps step with the state it's drawn over. A layer without dirt in any frame draws none.
frame_ownersReturns what each pixel of a wag's canvas shows in one direction at the frame being made (dirt_frame_owners()). It notes in frame_from whether the still sprite or an earlier frame shows the same there, comparing an MD5 of each. If one does, the frame takes the still's dirt as it is (carry_frame()), or that frame's bake (bake_frame()). The match is noted in sprite_frames too, and the still's pixels are noted there with the first frame. A kept wag's pixels are unpacked from sprite_frames, read from no sprite, and empty where an earlier frame shows the same.
frame_shows_stillReturns whether a wag's frame being made shows the still sprite's pixels in every direction (frame_from).
hair_mergeMerges one direction of the hair's deposits into its dirt (dirt_hair_dirt()). It reads the rasters of the deposits that reach the hair at their level (rasterise_shape_deposit()), in the request's order. It merges them on the hair and its pieces drawn over hats together (dirt_hair_layers_brightness()).
held_templateReturns the template it's drawn on once that's built, or null.
layers_shownReturns, for each layer of the stack but the rider, the pixels of a taur body's, a drawn part's or the hair's merge that the layer shows: alist(layer key = alist(direction = indexes in row-major order)), with every direction listed. In each direction one walk over the merge's drawn span (/datum/dirt_pixels/proc/drawn_span()) hands each drawn pixel to the layer that shows it. That's by height_owners at a height that stretches a taur's rider, else by taur_owners. A pixel the rider shows goes to no layer. The walk goes in row-major order, as dirt_role_runs() needs the pixels.
limb_masksReturns the masks for skin: each limb's baked from its runs (limb_runs()), and each part's that takes its general deposits from its own runs (part_runs). A limb or part whose runs key matches the one in previous takes that icon rather than a new bake. Equal runs bake the same icon (dirt_baked_icon()).
limb_runsReturns skin's runs, once its pieces are made: alist(limb zone = dirt_shown_runs()), for each limb they draw on. Each is clipped to the pixels its image shows. So nothing floats in the margin past the body, and each limb's overlay draws only on its own limb. The chest's also takes the pixels past the body of the parts drawn under the skin there, such as a butt in profile. They carry their share in the skin's mess, so they're drawn with the chest. The parts taking its general deposits get theirs in part_runs, faded as the limbs' runs are (deposit_part_runs()).
mergeMerges one direction's splats into one mask, the thickest winning each pixel, with the parts drawn under and over the surface (dirt_merge_with_parts()). Each splat's packed raster is read as it merges, a general deposit's on its limbs alone. Mess's limbs are 0, so it's read whole.
most_piecesReturns the most pieces the job can take. A body's or a part's masks take a piece for each sprite direction of its parts that it reads, then a piece for each part read. Each splat then takes a piece a direction, but on a part one piece draws all four. A plunge, a blast or a douse takes a band a piece instead, as a canvas field on the tile does (dirt_field_pieces()). Then come a piece that finds only cached splats left, four merges, a piece for each sprite read, and the icons. A taur body's, a drawn part's or the hair's masks take their reads, then four canvas reads and up to three pieces for each splat in each direction. For mess those are the rider's mess on cloth and on skin, then the pools. Then come a piece that finds only cached splats left, four merges and the icons. At a height that stretches a taur body's rider, its masks read the canvas again in four more pieces. A wag takes a piece for each state of its sprite loaded, and its still sprite's dirt takes the same pieces as above, once. Then each frame takes four reads, four carries and a bake, and its icons come last. A carry takes a piece for each DIRT_CARRY_PIXELS of its canvas at most. Each deposit's canvas fields, douses or taur coats take a band a piece. Sweat's multiplier takes a piece for each direction, and one for the icon.
next_frameStarts a wag's next frame (/datum/dirt_wag_source), which is read and carried afresh. Past the last frame, only the icons are left (frame_masks()).
next_pieceMakes the next piece of the masks: one sprite direction of its parts or shape read, one part's pixels read, one splat rasterised in one direction (on a part, in every direction), one direction's splats merged, one sprite worn over or under them read, or the icons. A taur body's, a drawn part's or the hair's dirt goes by next_taur_piece(), and sweat's multiplier goes by next_wet_piece().
next_taur_pieceMakes the next piece of a taur body's, a drawn part's or the hair's dirt, once its states are read (read_wanted_sprite()). A piece is what one direction of its canvas shows (dirt_taur_owners()), one splat in one direction (rasterise_taur()), one direction of blood below a taur's waist (rasterise_blood()), one direction's splats merged (the hair's by hair_merge()), or the icons (taur_masks()). At a height that stretches a taur body's rider, the canvas is read again with the rider stretched (height_owners). A wag (/datum/dirt_wag_source) loads its sprite a state a piece, then goes a frame at a time. Each frame is read (frame_owners()), carried from the still sprite's dirt (carry_frame()) and baked into its layers' animated icons (bake_frame()). The still's dirt is made once, before the first frame is carried. Once the last frame is baked, frame_masks() puts the icons together. A one-frame wag that shows the still sprite's pixels in every direction ends once its frame is read. Its masks are then noted as showing the still's (/datum/dirt_masks/var/shows_still).
next_wet_pieceMakes the next piece of sweat's multiplier on a garment (/datum/dirt_mask_request/wet), which needs no template. A piece is one direction's factors, or the icon (dirt_wet_icon()), stored as baked. The factors come from the garment's sprite near the masks' wet pixels (dirt_garment_pixels(), dirt_wet_factors()), with none under the sprites drawn over it that stay dry. The first piece makes the garment's sprite and each sprite kept dry (wet_sprites), which every direction reads.
parts_past_bodyReturns the chest's pixels in each direction with the pixels past the body of the parts drawn under the skin there added: alist(direction = indexes in row-major order).
rasterise_bloodMakes blood below a taur's waist in the next direction it isn't made in yet (dirt_blood_rasterise()). It makes every stain's at once, each in its own splat_rasters entry, on the taur body's own pixels or the taur-fitted garment's cloth.
rasterise_nextRasterises the next splat in the next direction it lacks (dirt_rasterise()) and keeps the raster in SSdirt.rasters. First it takes the directions already rasterised on this template from there, which cost no piece. Mess is rasterised at its level in that direction (dirt_part_body_level()). A general deposit is kept whole, on every limb, so every carrier holding it shares it. Each carrier keeps to its own limbs as it merges (merge()). Its key has its material's shape class, so materials alike share it and take their colours in the bake. On a bare body, one that wears a painted design (dirt_painted_design()) is keyed by its material instead, as each material's design differs. One without a design shares a dressed body's rasters, because bareness (painted) changes only the design. A plunge, a blast or a douse is drawn a band a piece (field_for()). It's kept once its last band is drawn, as dirt_rasterise() draws it whole.
rasterise_partRasterises one splat on the part the masks are drawn on, in every direction (dirt_part_field()).
rasterise_shape_depositMakes the next direction of a general deposit on a taur body or a drawn part (dirt_shape_deposit()), or on the hair as hair (dirt_shape_rasterise()). Directions already made on this shape (and rider) come from SSdirt.rasters. Their key has the material's shape class rather than the material, so materials alike share them. A taur body's are made as an Average rider shows its canvas (taur_owners), so every height shares them. A deposit under its cause's first hair level (dirt_hair_levels()) makes none on the hair. A deposit the shape takes from the canvas field (dirt_shape_field_facing()) is drawn from it a band a piece (field_for()), once for every direction it serves. It's then clipped to the shape's pixels, but a taur body's and a taur suit's stay whole, as dirt_shape_deposit() draws them. A douse on any shape, and a taur body's contact coat (dirt_taur_coat_cause()), are drawn a band a piece in each direction. A wag's rasters are its still sprite's, under the still's keys.
rasterise_taurMakes the next splat on a taur body or a drawn part in the next direction it lacks. First it takes the directions already made on this shape (and rider) from SSdirt.rasters, which cost no piece. A general deposit lands by the shape's own rules (rasterise_shape_deposit()). Mess makes pools, which keep clear of the rider's mess, the splat at its own level on the rider's cloth and skin. That rider's mess comes from SSdirt.rasters too, as the rider's own masks may have made it. Each one made here is a piece of its own. The pools are made with the rider at Average height (taur_owners), so every height shares their rasters. What's made goes in SSdirt.rasters.
read_partReads the pixels a part takes its share on in every direction (dirt_part_own()).
read_spriteAdds a sprite's pixels in every direction to what its kind covers. A sprite with a shift is read where its wrapper puts it over the body's tile, whatever its size (dirt_sprite_zone_pixels()).
read_wanted_spriteReads the next sprite direction of the job's parts or shape that SSdirt.sprite_reads doesn't hold, so the pieces after find them all read.
shape_facesReturns the directions where a wag's frame being made is carried and baked anew. That's all four but those whose pixels an earlier frame showed, which take that frame's bake (bake_frame()).
taur_masksReturns a taur body's, a drawn part's or the hair's dirt as masks on its canvas. On skin or fur, each layer's mask keeps to the pixels it shows. On cloth, the mask is the whole canvas, which a taur suit's own pixels clip. The hair's is the whole canvas too, as its merge already keeps to its pixels (hair_merge()). A hairstyle with pieces drawn over hats is the exception: each of its layers keeps to the pixels it shows.
waitNotes a body that drew without the masks while this waits, to draw it again once they're made. A body being deleted isn't drawn again.
wanted_readsReturns the sprite directions the request's parts or shape are read from, each list(sheet, state, direction, width, height). That's every state of each in every direction, including the parts taking its general deposits. A wag's are its still sprite's, which its frames are compared with. It has none if SSdirt.wag_sprites holds its sprites (sprite_frames_kept). Blood below a taur's waist draws nothing facing north (dirt_blood_continuation()), so its shape isn't read that way.

For sweat's multiplier: the garment's sprite, then each sprite kept dry, as icon() makes them from the request. They're made at the job's first piece, for every direction to read.

proc
bake_frame
()

Bakes the frame being made into each layer of the wag that has it, in every direction, at the layer's own delay (/datum/dirt_wag_source/var/layer_frames). Each direction gets the carried dirt's runs on the pixels the layer shows at the frame, or a blank if there are none. Where an earlier frame shows the same pixels, it takes that frame's bake. A layer's animated icon (frame_icons) starts at its first frame with dirt, with blanks for the frames before it. Nothing is registered here, because only frame_masks() registers what's shown.

proc
carry_frame
(direction) as /datum/dirt_pixels

Returns the dirt of a wag's frame being made in one direction, carried from its still sprite's along the tail and across it (/datum/dirt_tail_carry). So the dirt swings with the tail and draws as it does when the tail is still. Where the frame shows the still sprite's pixels (frame_from), it's the still's dirt as it is. Each piece carries DIRT_CARRY_PIXELS of the frame's pixels. It returns null until the last piece, which notes the frame's pixels and carry in sprite_frames. A kept wag's dirt is carried whole from sprite_frames (dirt_wag_carried()).

proc
deposit_part_runs
(/alist/fades) as /alist

Returns the runs of skin's general deposits on each part taking them (/datum/dirt_mask_request/var/deposit_parts) that they reach: alist(its placed_key() against the skin = dirt_shown_runs()). A part takes them on its own pixels in each direction it draws over the skin. They lie where their raster lies on the body's template, as far past the body as that reaches. That's how the reference generator composites the body's dirt over the parts drawn on it. Mess stays off them, because a part takes its share of mess on itself. It takes no share of general deposits, and draws them at their own level.

Arguments:

  • fades: alist(direction = dirt_cover_fades()), as the limbs' runs fade beside what's worn, or null.

proc
field_for
(/datum/dirt_splat/splat, /datum/dirt_material/material, facing, surface_kind) as /datum/dirt_canvas_field

Returns the field a general deposit is drawn from. Each field is made once and drawn a band a piece. On the job's shape, it's the canvas field, drawn facing one way (dirt_shape_field_facing()) and made once for every direction it serves. For a douse, it's a field on the shape's pixels in one direction (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/douse). For a taur body's contact coat, on its fur or a taur suit's cloth, it's a field in one direction (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/taur_coat). On a body's template, it's a plunge, a blast or a douse in one direction (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/body). The deposit's fields in every direction share one table of its noise (field_samples). A body's fields also share the deposit's features (deposit_features).

Arguments:

  • facing: The direction the field is drawn facing. For a douse, a coat or a body, it's the direction being drawn.
  • surface_kind: What it's drawn as: DIRT_SURFACE_FUR, DIRT_SURFACE_CLOTH (a taur suit's field) or DIRT_SURFACE_HAIR. On a body, DIRT_SURFACE_SKIN or DIRT_SURFACE_CLOTH.

proc
frame_masks
() as /datum/dirt_masks

Returns a wag's masks once every frame is baked: each layer's animated icon with dirt, registered in the resource cache. An icon built at run time can't hold the rewind flag. So a layer that rewinds gets its frames again in reverse after the last, at their delays. Its dirt then keeps step with the state it's drawn over. A layer without dirt in any frame draws none.

proc
frame_owners
(direction) as /list

Returns what each pixel of a wag's canvas shows in one direction at the frame being made (dirt_frame_owners()). It notes in frame_from whether the still sprite or an earlier frame shows the same there, comparing an MD5 of each. If one does, the frame takes the still's dirt as it is (carry_frame()), or that frame's bake (bake_frame()). The match is noted in sprite_frames too, and the still's pixels are noted there with the first frame. A kept wag's pixels are unpacked from sprite_frames, read from no sprite, and empty where an earlier frame shows the same.

proc
limb_runs
() as /alist

Returns skin's runs, once its pieces are made: alist(limb zone = dirt_shown_runs()), for each limb they draw on. Each is clipped to the pixels its image shows. So nothing floats in the margin past the body, and each limb's overlay draws only on its own limb. The chest's also takes the pixels past the body of the parts drawn under the skin there, such as a butt in profile. They carry their share in the skin's mess, so they're drawn with the chest. The parts taking its general deposits get theirs in part_runs, faded as the limbs' runs are (deposit_part_runs()).

proc
merge
(direction)

Merges one direction's splats into one mask, the thickest winning each pixel, with the parts drawn under and over the surface (dirt_merge_with_parts()). Each splat's packed raster is read as it merges, a general deposit's on its limbs alone. Mess's limbs are 0, so it's read whole.

↑
most_pieces
()

Returns the most pieces the job can take. A body's or a part's masks take a piece for each sprite direction of its parts that it reads, then a piece for each part read. Each splat then takes a piece a direction, but on a part one piece draws all four. A plunge, a blast or a douse takes a band a piece instead, as a canvas field on the tile does (dirt_field_pieces()). Then come a piece that finds only cached splats left, four merges, a piece for each sprite read, and the icons. A taur body's, a drawn part's or the hair's masks take their reads, then four canvas reads and up to three pieces for each splat in each direction. For mess those are the rider's mess on cloth and on skin, then the pools. Then come a piece that finds only cached splats left, four merges and the icons. At a height that stretches a taur body's rider, its masks read the canvas again in four more pieces. A wag takes a piece for each state of its sprite loaded, and its still sprite's dirt takes the same pieces as above, once. Then each frame takes four reads, four carries and a bake, and its icons come last. A carry takes a piece for each DIRT_CARRY_PIXELS of its canvas at most. Each deposit's canvas fields, douses or taur coats take a band a piece. Sweat's multiplier takes a piece for each direction, and one for the icon.

proc
next_taur_piece
()

Makes the next piece of a taur body's, a drawn part's or the hair's dirt, once its states are read (read_wanted_sprite()). A piece is what one direction of its canvas shows (dirt_taur_owners()), one splat in one direction (rasterise_taur()), one direction of blood below a taur's waist (rasterise_blood()), one direction's splats merged (the hair's by hair_merge()), or the icons (taur_masks()). At a height that stretches a taur body's rider, the canvas is read again with the rider stretched (height_owners). Returns:

  • TRUE: The masks are made.
  • FALSE: Pieces are left.

proc
rasterise_next
()

Rasterises the next splat in the next direction it lacks (dirt_rasterise()) and keeps the raster in SSdirt.rasters. First it takes the directions already rasterised on this template from there, which cost no piece. Mess is rasterised at its level in that direction (dirt_part_body_level()). A general deposit is kept whole, on every limb, so every carrier holding it shares it. Each carrier keeps to its own limbs as it merges (merge()). Its key has its material's shape class, so materials alike share it and take their colours in the bake. On a bare body, one that wears a painted design (dirt_painted_design()) is keyed by its material instead, as each material's design differs. One without a design shares a dressed body's rasters, because bareness (painted) changes only the design. A plunge, a blast or a douse is drawn a band a piece (field_for()). It's kept once its last band is drawn, as dirt_rasterise() draws it whole.

proc
rasterise_shape_deposit
(/datum/dirt_splat/splat, /alist/rasters)

Makes the next direction of a general deposit on a taur body or a drawn part (dirt_shape_deposit()), or on the hair as hair (dirt_shape_rasterise()). Directions already made on this shape (and rider) come from SSdirt.rasters. Their key has the material's shape class rather than the material, so materials alike share them. A taur body's are made as an Average rider shows its canvas (taur_owners), so every height shares them. A deposit under its cause's first hair level (dirt_hair_levels()) makes none on the hair. Arguments:

Returns whether it made a direction or a band of its field. Either is a piece.

proc
rasterise_taur
()

Makes the next splat on a taur body or a drawn part in the next direction it lacks. First it takes the directions already made on this shape (and rider) from SSdirt.rasters, which cost no piece. A general deposit lands by the shape's own rules (rasterise_shape_deposit()). Mess makes pools, which keep clear of the rider's mess, the splat at its own level on the rider's cloth and skin. That rider's mess comes from SSdirt.rasters too, as the rider's own masks may have made it. Each one made here is a piece of its own. The pools are made with the rider at Average height (taur_owners), so every height shares their rasters. What's made goes in SSdirt.rasters.
