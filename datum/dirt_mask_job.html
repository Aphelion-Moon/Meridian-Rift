dirt_mask_job
/datum/dirt_mask_job
inherits /datum/dirt_job

Makes a request's masks a piece at a time (next_piece()). While it waits, SSdirt.mask_jobs holds it under its key, so a body asking meanwhile waits for it (wait()) rather than queueing it again.

next_piece
()

Makes the next piece of the masks: one sprite direction of its parts or taur body read, one part's pixels read, one splat rasterised in one direction (on a part, in every direction), one direction's splats merged, one sprite worn over or under them read, or the icons. A taur body's pools go by next_taur_piece().

Returns:

  • TRUE: The masks are made, or can't be (failed).
  • FALSE: Pieces are left.

Makes the next splat's pools in the next direction it lacks, after taking the directions already made on this taur body and rider from SSdirt.rasters, which cost no piece.
