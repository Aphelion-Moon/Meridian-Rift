skew
/datum/dirt_shape/skew

A shape widened on one side and narrowed on the other. A positive skew widens it round the limb's way. Mirrored halves of a back read as a printed butterfly, so a back's shape is skewed.

Vars

innerThe shape it changes.
skewHow much wider it is round the limb's way, and narrower the other.

Procs

NewStarts a skewed shape.
distanceReturns the inner shape's distance, with across scaled down on the skewed side and up on the other.
