dirt_draft_words
/datum/dirt_draft_words

A seed text's draft-stream words, as every stream of it reads them. It holds the tempered words made so far. While it's SSdirt.draft_open, it also holds the twister's state to make more. When it's closed and a stream needs more, it's seeded again and twisted on to where it stopped.

Vars

countHow many words are made.
packedThe words made, each word's high then low half, cut in 24-bit pieces: three words in four numbers.
seedThe seed text.
stateThe twister's DIRT_DRAFT_WORDS words, high and low halves in turn, or null while it's closed.

Procs

NewStarts an entry for a seed text. Its twister isn't seeded until a stream needs a word.
fillMakes words until wanted are made, opening the twister first if it's closed.
openSeeds the twister again and twists it on past the words already made, closing the entry that was open.
twistTwists one word of the state in place. Words twist in order, so the next word is still untwisted. The word DIRT_DRAFT_REACH on is already twisted only when the slot is past the first 227, as one whole twist leaves them.
