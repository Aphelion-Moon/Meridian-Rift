dirt_shape_source
/datum/dirt_shape_source

A drawn shape's sprite as dirt reads it: the states of each of its layers, as it draws them on its canvas (dirt_canvases()). A layer's colour layers are taken together (a matrixed part's channel images). The shape is a taur body, a tail, wings, ears, horns or another drawn part. It's made once and never changed.

Vars

cached_keyshape_key(), once built.
canvasThe DIRT_CANVAS_* define it's drawn on.
iconThe sheet: an icon file, or an icon made at run time.
kindThe DIRT_SHAPE_* define it takes dirt as.
layer_sheetsalist(layer key = the sheet its states are drawn from) for each layer drawn from a sheet other than icon, as the hair's outer pieces are. Null when there's no such layer.
layer_statesalist(EXTERNAL_* layer key = list of the states drawn at that layer).
stackIts layer keys, lowest drawn first. A taur body's include its rider (dirt_taur_stack()).
