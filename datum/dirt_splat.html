dirt_splat
/datum/dirt_splat

One piece of dirt on a carrier, at a level. It's a variant of a zone's mess, or of a general deposit, a cause's dirt in a material. More shots on the zone raise a mess splat's level. Past the top level, another splat starts.

Vars

causeA general deposit's DIRT_CAUSE_* define, or null for mess, whose cause is "aimed_".
last_serialWhen it last landed or was raised, from SSdirt.next_serial(). Past a cap, the splat with the lowest goes first.
level1 to DIRT_MESS_MAX_LEVEL for mess. For a general deposit, 1 to its cause's top level.
limbsThe limbs a general deposit covers, DIRT_LIMB_BIT() of each. Its masks keep to them (dirt_pixels_from_drawn()). 0 for mess. On a general deposit, 0 means every limb.
materialWhat it's made of: a DIRT_MATERIAL_* define, or "slime#RRGGBB" for slime (dirt_material()).
serialWhen it first landed, from SSdirt.next_serial(). Where a surface's mess and general deposits merge, they merge in this order (cmp_dirt_splats_landed()).
variant0 to DIRT_VARIANTS - 1.
zoneA mess splat's DIRT_ZONE_* define, or null for a general deposit.
