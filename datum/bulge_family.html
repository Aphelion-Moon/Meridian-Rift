bulge_family
/datum/bulge_family

Bulge families: the set of shapes a garment bulges with. A garment names its family by typepath in its bulge_family var (garment_families.dm).

A family is never instantiated. Its data are vars initialised here and read with initial() from the typepath: nothing is allocated or retained, there is no lookup list, and a read costs about what a datum var read does.

The shapes are named from these vars: "[shapes][half|hard][size][plain|digi]" up to small_cap, in the template sheets (icons/templates, found by templates.dm), and past it one per penis shape, "[shapes][half|hard][size][plain|digi]_[penis shape]", derived at run time from the penis sprites (top_row, hem, contained, curtain and long say how). Those five vars belong to the shapes stem, as a loincloth's worn_icon, worn_icon_state, first_column, last_column, start_x, start_y and drop belong to its drapes stem: what is derived is kept for the round by its name, so the families that share a stem must agree on them (bulge_derivation checks it). Where a family draws another family's shapes by design, it names that family (half_shapes_of, hard_shapes_of, large_shapes_of) instead of repeating them. A garment whose approved look differs from its family's is given a subtype of the family that says how.
