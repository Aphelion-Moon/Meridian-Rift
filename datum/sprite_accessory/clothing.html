proc
chest_cut_state
(physique, /mob/living/carbon/human/wearer, flat_suffix, /list/pec_suffixes)

Returns the state a top cut for a chest with breasts draws on wearer: its own state over breasts, and on a female body unless its breasts are pecs; on any other chest, without breasts or with pecs, the state its suffixes name.

Arguments:

  • physique: The physique it's drawn for.
  • wearer: Who wears it, if anyone. Worn by nobody, like a mannequin's, it draws its own state.
  • flat_suffix: The suffix of its state for a chest without breasts.
  • pec_suffixes: Pec shape, as garment_fit_shape names it -> the suffix of its state over those pecs, for a top drawn apart for some of them; any other pecs take flat_suffix.

proc
fitted_sprite
(/mob/living/carbon/human/wearer, bodyshape, icon_state_to_use, cut, digitigrade_mask)

Returns this style's sprite as make_appearance() draws it on wearer, fitted to their fitness limbs and to pecs drawn under it: a cached resource. Null to draw it as it is: on a Normal-sized body with no pecs under it (its garment_fit is null), a style that isn't fitted, worn by nobody, like a mannequin's, a fit that leaves it as it is, or one still being made by SSgarment_fitting, which draws the body again once it's done (GARMENT_FITS_DEFERRED; a dummy's fits are made as it's drawn).

The legs are fitted through their digitigrade templates where the legs drawn are digitigrade art: a "_d" state (a style with has_custom_digi_sprite) or the GAGS digitigrade mask (digi_icon_state). A plantigrade state on a digitigrade body is fitted as the human legs it draws, and a taur body's legs aren't fitted. A style worn over the chest is also fitted to pecs drawn under it (pecs_under_underclothes()), and while they flex, animated with them (/datum/garment_fit_job/underclothes/flex). Its fits over those pecs flexing, one for each pec flex sheet, are queued the first time it's drawn over them and made in the background, so it moves from their first flex on; a flex that comes before its fit is made draws the still fit meanwhile. On a dummy, or with GARMENT_FITS_DEFERRED off, nothing is queued ahead: each is made as the flex that first needs it draws.

Each fit is made once a round, and cached by the fit and the style's state, cut and mask.

Arguments:

  • wearer: Who wears the style, if anyone.
  • bodyshape: The body shape it's drawn for, as make_appearance() has it.
  • icon_state_to_use: The state it's drawn from.
  • cut: The female cut's flags, or a false value for none.
  • digitigrade_mask: Whether the GAGS digitigrade mask draws its legs.
