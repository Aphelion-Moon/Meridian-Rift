participants_accept_interactionChecks the preferences required at both execution boundaries.
participants_are_actionableReturns TRUE only while both participants can consciously take part in an interaction.
save_aphelion_textWrites back whichever optional Aphelion text is set.
save_chest_textWrites back whichever optional pec and nippleless text is set.
texts_forThe list to pick messages from: nippleless_texts, if given, replaces everything when that chest is pecs drawn without nipples, and pokes_out_texts replaces everything while the bulge's penis pokes out, since head-through-cloth lines no longer apply then. sweaty_texts and pit_hair_texts are extra options, added to the pool while the sweaty participant is visibly sweating or the target grew physical pit hair.
