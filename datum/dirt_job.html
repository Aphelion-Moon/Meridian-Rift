dirt_job
/datum/dirt_job

Work SSdirt does a piece at a time (next_piece()), each piece in its own try/catch (make_piece()), so a piece that runtimes drops its job, cached as failed, and can't hold up the jobs after it.

Vars

failedWhether it can't make what it's for: a piece runtimed, or what it reads is missing.
pieces_madeHow many pieces it has made, across every fire: past most_pieces() it's dropped.

Procs

finishedCaches what it made, or that it failed, once it's out of the queue.
most_piecesReturns the most pieces it can take, across every fire. One that takes more is dropped as failed.
next_pieceMakes the next piece.
