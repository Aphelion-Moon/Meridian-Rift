delete_files

Deletes the library's file, its backup and its staged copy, as a library that holds no pieces and no Unfinished piece keeps none. A later save() writes the file again as a new one.

Returns:

  • TRUE/FALSE: Whether none of the three is left. A store that refuses writes deletes nothing.
