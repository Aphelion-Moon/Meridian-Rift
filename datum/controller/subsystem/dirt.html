templatesBuilt templates by key, or FALSE when one can't be. proc
masks_for
(/datum/dirt_mask_request/request, /mob/living/carbon/human/redraw_body, redraw_flags) as /datum/dirt_masks

Returns a request's masks, or null while they're made or when they can't be. Masks not made yet are queued, behind their template and the measure if those aren't made either, and redraw_body is drawn again once they are; without DIRT_MASKS_DEFERRED they're made now.

Arguments:

  • request: What the masks are made from.
  • redraw_body: The body drawing them, drawn again once they're made.
  • redraw_flags: DIRT_REDRAW_* flags, what to draw again on it.

redraw_later

  • body: The body.
  • redraw_flags: DIRT_REDRAW_* flags, what to draw again.

()

  • job: The job.

stat_entry

Shows how many jobs wait, how many bodies wait to be drawn again, and the longest fire this round.

proc
template_for
(/list/zone_sources)

Returns the template built from a body's sprites, queueing it if it isn't built or queued yet; without DIRT_MASKS_DEFERRED it's built now. One the cache dropped that a queued job still holds goes back in the cache rather than being built a second time.

Arguments:

  • zone_sources: The body's sprites, as dirt_zone_sources() returns them.

Returns:

  • /datum/dirt_template: The template.
  • /datum/dirt_template_job: The job building it, queued.
  • FALSE: It can't be built.

↑
vv_edit_var
(var_name, var_value)

Refuses admin edits of the caches and the jobs waiting: a mask, template or measure edited by hand would be drawn for the rest of the round, and a list of jobs replaced would break masks_for() for the round.

Arguments:

  • var_name: The var.
  • var_value: Its new value.

Returns:

  • TRUE/FALSE: Whether the var was edited.

proc
work_on
(/datum/dirt_job/job, yielding)

Makes pieces of a job until it's done or, when yielding, until the tick runs short. A job that runs past its most_pieces(), counted across every fire, is dropped as failed.

Arguments:

  • job: The job.
  • yielding: Whether to stop when the tick runs short, as a fire does.

Returns whether the job is done.
