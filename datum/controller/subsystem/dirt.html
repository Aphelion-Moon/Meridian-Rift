proc
drop_mask_job
(key, /mob/living/carbon/human/body, redraw_flags)

Stops a body waiting for the masks queued under a key. Once no other body still around waits for them, it drops their job, part made or not. So masks that nothing would draw aren't made, such as a wag's frames whose splats changed first. It never drops the job through finished(), which would cache the masks as failed for whoever asks next.

Arguments:

  • key: The masks' key.
  • body: The body that no longer draws them, or is being deleted.
  • redraw_flags: The DIRT_REDRAW_* flags of the drawing that no longer draws them, or null for all of the body's. If the body still waits with other flags, for another drawing with the same masks, it keeps the job.

proc
finish_all
(rounds)

Makes every queued job now and draws the bodies waiting for them again. It's for whatever has to see dirt drawn at once. In a round the queue's own pace keeps ticks short instead.

Arguments:

  • rounds: The most rounds to go, each making what's queued and drawing again what waited for it. If it stops after one, what that round's draws asked for stays queued, such as a garment's sweat multiplier.

proc
masks_for
(/datum/dirt_mask_request/request, /mob/living/carbon/human/redraw_body, redraw_flags, /datum/dirt_masks/previous, key) as /datum/dirt_masks

Returns a request's masks, or null while they're made or when they can't be. Masks not made yet are queued, behind their template and the measure if those aren't made either, and redraw_body is drawn again once they are. Sweat's multipliers are kept in wet_icons, every other mask in masks.

Arguments:

  • request: What the masks are made from.
  • redraw_body: The body drawing them, drawn again once they're made.
  • redraw_flags: DIRT_REDRAW_* flags, what to draw again on it.
  • previous: The masks the caller draws while it waits for these, or null. A skin's job made here holds them for limb_masks() (/datum/dirt_mask_job/var/previous), which no other job runs. A job already waiting keeps its own.
  • key: The request's mask_key(), from a caller that has it, or null to work it out.

vv_edit_var

Refuses admin edits of the caches, the jobs waiting, the bodies waiting or being drawn again, and the drafting state.
