fire
(resumed)

Rebuilds waiting previews' mobs in order while the tick has room, then starts the drawings asked for since the last fire. Waiting mobs go first: they were asked for earlier, and however many drawings wait to start, the queue never goes longer than CHARACTER_PREVIEW_REBUILD_OVERDUE without a rebuild while this fires, which is what ends every wait for a turn. The first rebuild may take what is left of the tick, and later ones only this subsystem's share of it.

Each loop goes round at most once for each entry its list had when the fire began, whatever the work adds to it meanwhile, so neither can go round forever; what is added waits for a later fire.
