Procs

fireRuns queued editors' work in order until the tick runs short. Costly work can't be split: what it runs past its tick's limit comes out of the overrun budget, and while that is spent, costly work waits for it to refill. A rebuild's drawing never waits once its body is built, but what it runs over comes out of the budget too. Work that has to wait goes back in the queue.
stat_entryShows how many editors are waiting, and what is left of the overrun budget.
