piping_hot
/datum/component/piping_hot

Food fresh out of a hot vendor. It steams until it cools, and every bite before then burns the eater's tongue a little and puts them off eating for a moment. The bite still goes down. Masochists keep eating. Eaters the heat can't hurt don't notice it.

One timer cools it; nothing processes. Food taken out of a hot pack gets the heat that is left through pass_to().
