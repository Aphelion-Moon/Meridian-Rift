erp_leash
/datum/component/erp_leash

The leash's hook on the leashed. The free end is whoever holds or wears the leash, or the leash itself on the floor.

The leashed follows their holder's route a step at a time, and holds the holder back where they can't follow. A leash on the floor follows the leashed the same way, but never holds them back.
