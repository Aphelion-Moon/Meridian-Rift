dirtiness
/datum/component/dirtiness

The mess a person or an item carries, as splats: each a variant of a zone's mess at a level, from masks SSdirt makes. A person's skin splats draw through one /datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_skin on each limb skin mess draws on, under what they wear, and fade out beside it. Their underclothes' splats are kept with each piece, and draw over it through one /datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_underclothes on the chest while it's worn and shows. Their parts' splats draw with each part's own front layer, and their taur body's pools with the taur body's own images. A garment's splats draw inset over it while a person wears it, and a taur suit's back splats as pools along the taur body's back too. The parts a surface's mess reaches shape it (dirt_surface_parts()). Washing clears it, as it clears the floor's decals.

Proc Details

proc
add_mess
(zone, organ_slot, levels, variant, redraw, new_variant) as /datum/dirt_splat

Puts a mess on a zone of this carrier: its skin or cloth, or the part in an organ slot. As a shot lands it, the levels go on the zone's newest splat, up to DIRT_MESS_MAX_LEVEL, and what's left starts another splat at that level, at most the top one, with a variant this carrier doesn't have on that zone yet while one is left. A variant given places a new splat at levels with that variant.

Arguments:

  • zone: A DIRT_ZONE_* define.
  • organ_slot: The part's ORGAN_SLOT_* define, or null for the skin or cloth.
  • levels: How many levels it adds, 1 or more.
  • variant: 0 to DIRT_MESS_VARIANTS - 1, or null.
  • redraw: Whether to draw the change. A caller that changes more first, as a landing past the cap does, draws once after (mess_changed()), so no masks are made for splats it's about to take off.
  • new_variant: The variant a splat started without variant takes, or null for one the zone doesn't have yet: a part takes the variant of the shot's splat on the surface under it.

Returns the splat it started, or the one it raised when it started none.

  • redraw: Whether to draw the change, as add_mess()'s.

Returns the splat it started or raised, or null when the person doesn't wear that piece.

Returns whether the body draws the sprites the skin's masks were made from, as a limb's draw checks them: its own, and for the chest the arms and hands, the eyes and whether the head is a human's (dirt_zone_sources_match(), which checks the eyes whichever limb asks). When it does, notes what they're drawn from (skin_reads).

Arguments:

  • body: The person.
  • limb: The limb asking.

proc
stack_mess
(/list/splats, zone, levels, variant, new_variant) as /datum/dirt_splat

Adds a mess to one surface's splats as add_mess() describes, and returns the splat it started, or the one it raised.

Arguments:

  • splats: The surface's splats, oldest first. Added to.
  • zone, levels, variant, new_variant: As add_mess()'s.

proc
sync_skin_overlays
(/mob/living/carbon/human/body)

Puts a skin overlay on each limb skin mess draws on while the skin carries any, and takes them off once it doesn't. Nothing is drawn until the body's next update_body_parts().

Arguments:

  • body: The person.

proc
sync_underclothes_overlay
(/mob/living/carbon/human/body)

Puts the underclothes overlay on the chest while the underclothes carry mess, and takes it off once they don't. Nothing is drawn until the body's next update_body_parts().

Arguments:

  • body: The person.
