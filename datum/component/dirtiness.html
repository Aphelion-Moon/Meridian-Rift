dirtiness
/datum/component/dirtiness

The dirt a person or an item carries, as splats (/datum/dirt_splat). SSdirt makes the masks they draw with. A person's skin splats draw through one /datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_skin on each limb they lie on, under what the person wears, and fade out beside it. The splats on their underclothes are kept with each piece. While the piece is worn and shows, they draw over it through one /datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_underclothes on the chest. The splats on a person's parts draw with each part's own front layer. A taur body's pools and general deposits draw with its own images. The splats of tails, wings, ears, horns and other drawn parts draw with each part's images (appendages.dm). The hair's draw over the hair while it shows (hair.dm). A garment's splats draw inset over it while a person wears it. A taur suit's back splats and general deposits draw on the taur canvas round it too. Washing clears it, as it clears the floor's decals.

check_coverHas the skin's masks made again if what the body wears over its skin changed since they were asked for. Its splats then fade out beside what it wears now, and its general deposits draw painted only while it's bare (dirt_bare_bodied()). The underwear's masks are made again if its general deposits are painted while the body isn't bare, or unpainted while it is. So a piece made again meanwhile can't hide skin masks made before. The garments are judged by a change since this last looked.
check_taur_bloodHas a taur's blood below the waist drawn again where what it's drawn from changed since it was asked for. It's also drawn again where it was drawn on the other side of the dirt from the one it takes. It does this for the taur body's images and for the taur-fitted garment's. The taur body's side is read against the chest's drawn key (waist_blood_over()). The garment's is read against what its last draw noted (dirt_cloth_run_over()). So a key or an image read for anything else can't hide a side left undrawn. That garment gets a carrier of its own to draw the blood through, made here, outside any render. This runs at every cover check (check_cover()), on anyone, taur or not. It deletes any carrier that holds nothing (holds_nothing()), whatever made it. Unless the uniform or the suit carries the blood now, its carrier first drops any blood it drew, then is deleted if it holds nothing. So is this carrier, once no worn garment's blood would draw below a taur's waist. Its taur body is then drawn again without the blood.

proc
add_mess
(zone, organ_slot, levels, new_variant) as /datum/dirt_splat

Puts a mess on a zone of this carrier: its skin or cloth, or the part in an organ slot. As a shot lands it, the levels go on the zone's newest splat, up to DIRT_MESS_MAX_LEVEL. What's left starts another splat with that many levels, at most the top one. The new splat takes a variant this carrier doesn't have on that zone yet, if there's one. A splat raised keeps its place and counts as the newest (/datum/dirt_splat/var/last_serial). It draws nothing. The caller draws once it's done (mess_changed(), dirt_draw_landing()), so a shot past the cap asks for no masks for splats it's about to take off.

Arguments:

  • zone: A DIRT_ZONE_* define.
  • organ_slot: The part's ORGAN_SLOT_* define, or null for the skin or cloth.
  • levels: How many levels it adds, 1 or more.
  • new_variant: The variant a splat it starts takes, or null for one the zone doesn't have yet. A part takes the variant of the shot's splat on the surface under it.

Returns the splat it started, or the one it raised when it started none.

Its splats then fade out beside what it wears now, and its general deposits draw painted only while it's bare (dirt_bare_bodied()). The underwear's masks are made again if its general deposits are painted while the body isn't bare, or unpainted while it is. Has the masks the body's parts shape made again if what it draws of them changed since they were asked for, and draws the body. proc
check_taur_blood
()

Has a taur's blood below the waist drawn again where what it's drawn from changed since it was asked for. It's also drawn again where it was drawn on the other side of the dirt from the one it takes. It does this for the taur body's images and for the taur-fitted garment's. The taur body's side is read against the chest's drawn key (waist_blood_over()). The garment's is read against what its last draw noted (dirt_cloth_run_over()). So a key or an image read for anything else can't hide a side left undrawn. That garment gets a carrier of its own to draw the blood through, made here, outside any render. This runs at every cover check (check_cover()), on anyone, taur or not. It deletes any carrier that holds nothing (holds_nothing()), whatever made it. Unless the uniform or the suit carries the blood now, its carrier first drops any blood it drew, then is deleted if it holds nothing. So is this carrier, once no worn garment's blood would draw below a taur's waist. Its taur body is then drawn again without the blood.

Returns whether the taur body's images need drawing again.

Masks made for another sprite (lying down, another kind, another hairstyle or hat) are dropped and made again. On a taur body, so are masks made for another rider or height. A wag never has them made again, as a tail is read still. A steady draw compares a few vars, and the rider is read again only once a limb or the eyes change (rider_unchanged()).

Arguments:

  • organ_slot: The part's ORGAN_SLOT_* define, or DIRT_CARRIER_HAIR.
  • source: The part as it draws now, or null.

proc
ready_skin_masks
(/obj/item/bodypart/limb) as /datum/dirt_masks

Returns the masks the skin's splats are drawn with on a limb, or null while there are none to draw. The first ask after a change queues them with SSdirt, which draws the body again once they're made. Until then the masks before them are drawn, so mess already there stays while more is added. Masks made for other sprites than the body draws (a new size, a swapped limb, other eyes) are dropped and made again. A steady draw only compares what each limb is drawn from with skin_reads. The masks fade out beside what the body wears (dirt_cover_sprites()). The parts the splats reach shape them (dirt_surface_parts()). Every part's front image draws over the skin, and a butt's back image and the pecs guide under it. General deposits draw on the person's dirtier side, and on a bare body (dirt_bare_bodied()) as their painted designs. The parts drawn over the skin take them on their own pixels (dirt_parts_over()), drawn with each part. A body hidden under a worn sprite larger than its tile (skin_hidden()) draws none. Its request is made, but no masks are.

proc
ready_wag_masks
(organ_slot, /datum/dirt_wag_source/wag, ask) as /datum/dirt_masks

Returns the masks of a wagging tail's dirt, drawn with its wagging images, or null while there are none to draw (/datum/dirt_drawing/proc/wag_shown()). They're the wag's own masks (/datum/dirt_wag_source), asked for at the first wag after the splats change. Where a one-frame wag shows the still sprite's pixels, they're the still masks ready_shape_masks() returned instead. Nothing is drawn while frames are made, because the still dirt would lie still over a moving tail. A one-frame wag draws the still dirt while its job finds out. The job of masks asked for before the splats changed goes once other masks are asked for, or would be (ask). Call it after ready_shape_masks().

Returns whether the body is hidden under a worn sprite larger than its tile (dirt_body_hidden()). The answer is read once and kept with everything it was read from (hidden_read). That's the uniform's and suit's entries in overlays_standing, and the organ in the taur body's slot. The organ decides whether a wide suit is a taur suit. Adds a general deposit to one surface's splats, and returns the deposit it started or raised. If a deposit there has the same cause and material, it takes the new one.
