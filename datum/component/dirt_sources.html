dirt_sources
/datum/component/dirt_sources

The listener for the events on a person that land general dirt in a round. Every human but a preview dummy gets one as it's made, and keeps it. It stores the cooldowns that the gate (dirt_source_land()) holds those events to.

Vars

burning_sinceThe world.time when this person last caught fire, or null while they aren't burning.
cooldown_endsThe world.time each source with a cooldown can next roll its chance on this person, by cooldown_key(), or null before any. An end time is cheaper than tg's TIMER_COOLDOWN_START(), which adds a timer for each start.
next_floor_checkThe world.time when check_floor() next looks at the floor under this person. Every step reads it, so it's a var of its own, not a key in cooldown_ends.
