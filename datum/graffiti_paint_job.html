graffiti_paint_job
/datum/graffiti_paint_job
inherits /datum/custom_sprite_work_owner

A spray can painting a saved piece onto a wall, a floor or a full-tile window over time: a do_after while each layer appears in turn from the bottom, left to right in strips, the surface recomposited at each step. The can is charged once, as the piece's paint first shows. Stopping leaves the paint revealed so far, and puts the piece in the painter's Unfinished slot, ready to resume on that surface, unless a pane loosened under it, which drops the piece's paint whole.

The steps are costly work that SScustom_sprite_work runs within its overrun budget, as it runs the editors' composites, so under load they come late rather than at their time; the finish shows the whole piece when the do_after ends.

Make one, then start() it. A player runs one at a time, kept in SSgraffiti.paint_jobs while it runs.

Proc Details

New
(/mob/living/user, /obj/item/toy/crayon/spraycan/can, /turf/surface, /list/piece, /list/resume, piece_id)

Sets a job up, continuing a stopped one where resume is for this surface and the paint it left is still there, which paid for the piece.

The job takes GRAFFITI_REVEAL_BASE plus GRAFFITI_REVEAL_PER_PIXEL for each painted pixel, half that for taggers, at the painter's action speed as its do_after does. Its steps come about every GRAFFITI_REVEAL_MIN_STEP at most, a quick piece revealing several strips a step, and spread over the whole job. A resumed job takes what is left of that.

Arguments:

  • user: The painter.
  • can: The spray can, in the painter's hand.
  • surface: The wall, floor or window tile, the left tile of a two-tile piece.
  • piece: A copy of the canonical piece to paint.
  • resume: The Unfinished slot's resume data, or null.
  • piece_id: The library piece painted, or "unfinished" for the Unfinished slot's piece.

proc
interrupt
(keep)

Stops the job, leaving the paint revealed so far. The piece goes in the painter's Unfinished slot, with where to resume it while that paint is still on the surface. A pane loosened under the piece drops all of the paint revealed, both halves of a two-tile piece, and the piece goes in the slot with nowhere to resume.

Arguments:

  • keep: Whether the piece goes in the Unfinished slot. An admin deleting its paint stops the job with nothing kept.

proc
owner_ckey
()

Returns the account the job paints for, which keeps its placements and its Unfinished piece even when the painter's body loses its player.

Returns:

  • text: The ckey the job runs under in SSgraffiti.paint_jobs, or the painter's before it starts.

proc
paint
(progress)

Shows the piece on its tiles as far as progress reveals it: new art for each tile's placement, or, once a tile's art has paint, a new placement on top of its stack. Its time counts against every painter's composites (SSgraffiti's spend_composite()). The halves of a two-tile piece share one placement id. The first step whose paint shows charges the can (charge()). A step whose paint took the painter's own oldest pieces past their caps says so once, even one that stops the job.

Arguments:

  • progress: Strips revealed, at least 1.

Returns:

  • TRUE/FALSE: FALSE when a placement is gone or the surface refused the paint, which stops the job.
