dirt_surface
/datum/dirt_surface

One direction of a body's dirt surface. Dirt lies on limbs, not on the screen: every pixel of the body and of a margin round it maps to a limb, an angle round that limb (theta) and a height along it (h), so a mark keeps its place on the body as the mob turns.

  • theta: degrees round the limb, 0 at the body's front, 90 its right side, 180 its back and -90 its left.
  • h: 0 at the limb's lowest row, 1 at its highest.

Per-pixel lists are indexed y * width + x + 1, in the reference generator's coordinates: x from the left and y from the top, both from 0. That pixel is (x + 1, height - y) in an icon.

Proc Details

New
(direction, /list/masks, /list/owners, /list/eyes, human_head)

Builds one direction of a body's surface from the sprites it draws.

Arguments:

  • direction: The direction it's seen from.
  • masks: Each drawn zone's sprite pixels, from dirt_zone_pixels().
  • owners: The zone each pixel shows, from dirt_zone_owners().
  • eyes: The eye sprites' pixels, or null for a body without eyes.
  • human_head: Whether the head is a human's, whose mouth is guarded.

proc
find_rim
(/list/limbs, /list/distances)

Marks the rim (DIRT_PIXEL_RIM): the silhouette's edge, body pixels with a side off the body or off the canvas, and the margin's first ring. Each leg's lowest row is its sole and stays off it. The body's pixels off the rim are its interior.

Arguments:

  • limbs: Each body pixel's limb, from map_body().
  • distances: Each pixel's ring, from spread_margin().

proc
guard_face
(/list/eyes, human_head)

Guards the face: nothing on an eye or the pixels beside it, film at most on its diagonals, and nothing on a human mouth, whose pixels are the human head sprite's.

Arguments:

  • eyes: The eye sprites' pixels, or null for a body without eyes.
  • human_head: Whether the head is a human's.

proc
map_body
(/list/owners)

Returns each body pixel's limb: its zone's, except below the belt, where the torso's pixels go to the legs. Facing south or north they split down the middle; in profile they go to the near leg. Each body pixel's zone goes in its flags.

Arguments:

  • owners: The zone each pixel shows, from dirt_zone_owners().

proc

Finds the rows each limb covers and its span in each, from its zones' whole sprites, the torso's only above the belt. A leg also spans the torso pixels it took below the belt, in the rows its own sprite misses.

Arguments:

  • masks: Each drawn zone's sprite pixels, from dirt_zone_pixels().
  • limbs: Each body pixel's limb, from map_body().

pixel_index

Returns a pixel's index in the per-pixel lists, or null off the canvas.

proc
place_pixels
(/list/spread)

Places each pixel of the body and its margin on its limb, from the limb's span in the nearest row it covers, the upper one on a tie. Theta is the angle whose sine is how far the pixel sits from the span's middle, as a fraction of its half width, turned to the direction; h runs from the limb's lowest row, 0, to its highest, 1.

Arguments:

  • spread: Each pixel's limb, the margin included, from spread_margin().

proc
spread_margin
(/list/limbs, /list/distances)

Returns each pixel's limb with the margin added: DIRT_SURFACE_MARGIN rings round the body, each margin pixel taking the limb of the first pixel of the ring before it to reach it. A ring is reached from in row-major order, each pixel trying right, left, down and up, as the reference generator does, so a margin pixel between two limbs goes the same way.

Arguments:

  • limbs: Each body pixel's limb, from map_body().
  • distances: Filled with each pixel's ring: 0 on the body, then 1 to DIRT_SURFACE_MARGIN.

proc
widen_row
(/alist/spans, limb, row, column)

Widens a limb's span in one row to take in a column.

Arguments:

  • spans: alist(limb = alist(row = list(leftmost column, rightmost column))).
  • limb: The limb.
  • row, column: The pixel.
