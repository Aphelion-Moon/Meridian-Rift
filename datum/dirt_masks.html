/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types — Var Details

dirt_masks
/datum/dirt_masks

A surface's masks: its dirt baked into four-direction RGBA icons (dirt_baked_icon()) and registered in the resource cache. Each icon is drawn as it is, because its colours and alpha are in its pixels.

Vars

bakedFor cloth: the baked icon on the whole canvas, which the garment's own pixels clip. For a part, the same, already kept to the part's pixels. Null when it draws nothing.
baked_by_layerFor a taur body's or a drawn part's dirt: alist(EXTERNAL_* layer = baked icon), each on its canvas, kept to the pixels that layer shows (dirt_taur_owners()). A taur suit draws its own from baked, over the whole canvas. So does the hair, kept to its pixels less its darkest strands (dirt_hair_dirt()). Hair with pieces drawn over hats draws from here instead, under DIRT_CARRIER_HAIR and DIRT_HAIR_OUTER.
baked_by_limbFor skin: alist(limb zone = baked icon), each limb's clipped to the pixels its image shows.
baked_by_partFor skin: alist(a part's /datum/dirt_part_source/proc/placed_key() against the skin = baked icon). Each icon holds the skin's general deposits on a part drawn over the skin that they reach. They lie on the part's own pixels, in the directions it draws over the skin (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/deposit_part_runs()). They're drawn under the part's own mess in its container (dirt_container_over()). Null if there are none.
keyThe request's mask_key().
limb_keysFor skin: alist(limb zone = an MD5 of what its baked icon draws, dirt_runs_key()), so only a limb whose pixels changed caches new images.
part_keysFor skin: alist(the same key = an MD5 of what its baked icon draws, dirt_runs_key()), for the part's render key. Null if there are none.
part_kindsFor skin: alist(kind = TRUE), each kind of part a key in part_keys starts with (/datum/dirt_part_source/proc/part_key()). Null if there are none.
shows_stillFor a wag's masks (/datum/dirt_wag_source): whether its one frame shows the still sprite's pixels in every direction, so the part draws its still masks while it wags.
wetFor cloth holding sweat, its wet pixels, which a garment's multiplier is made from (dirt_wet_drawn()). Null without sweat. For sweat's multiplier itself (DIRT_MASKS_WET) it's null too, and baked is the multiplier.
