dirt_template_job
/datum/dirt_template_job
inherits /datum/dirt_job

Builds a dirt template one direction at a time, in the reference generator's order (south, north, east, west), so SSdirt can spread the work over ticks. A body whose limb names a state its sheet lacks gets no template: the job fails on its first piece.

Vars

built_countHow many of them are built.
directionsThe directions it builds, in the order it builds them.
templateThe template being built.
zone_sourcesThe sprites it's built from, as dirt_zone_sources() returns them.
