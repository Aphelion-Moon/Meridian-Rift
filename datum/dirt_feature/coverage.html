coverage
/datum/dirt_feature/coverage

A coat over the whole body from the field in mob space, rather than on one limb. It lies on every pixel of the surface, the margin round the body included. It's a coat, never a recolour. So every level keeps windows, and light materials clump rather than coat. Its body field tells it its material and surface before it binds the rows (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/body). It's a veil, so on a face it keeps to dirt_face_zone()'s limits.

Proc Details

proc
at
(column, row, limb, theta, along, index, /alist/samples)

Returns how thick the coat lies on a pixel of the surface.

Arguments:

  • column, row: The pixel, from the left and from the top, from 0.
  • limb: Its DIRT_LIMB_* define.
  • along: Its h, its height along the limb.
  • samples: The field's samples (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/var/samples).

proc
bind_rows
(/datum/dirt_surface/surface, first_row, last_row, /alist/samples)

Works out the coat on every pixel of some rows of the surface, the margin's too, by at(), and adds it to drawn_pixels. Each pixel is worked out alone. So binding the rows a band at a time from the top gives what binding every row at once gives, in the same order. A body field binds them that way (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/body).

Arguments:

  • first_row: The first row, from 0.
  • samples: The field's samples (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/var/samples), the noise its levels share.

Arguments:

  • material: The /datum/dirt_material it's made of, or null.
