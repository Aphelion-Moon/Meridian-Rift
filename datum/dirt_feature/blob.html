blob
/datum/dirt_feature/blob

An irregular patch whose outline wobbles round it, with a film ring past it.

As in the reference generator, a blob whose ring has a width is ringed too (/datum/dirt_feature/var/ring), so a broken film ring also runs round it.

Vars

radius_xIts radius round the limb, in pixels.
radius_yIts radius up the limb, in pixels.
raggedHow far its outline wobbles, as a fraction of its radius.
ring_widthHow far its film ring reaches past its outline, as a fraction of its radius: 0 for none.

Procs
