damp
/datum/dirt_feature/damp

Where cloth soaks through with sweat. It's wettest in the middle with a soft rim. Large noise makes its outline wander, since sweat wicks out unevenly and is never round or level. Its shape (/datum/dirt_shape) gives each point's distance from the middle, 1 at the edge.
