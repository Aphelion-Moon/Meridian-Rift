douse
/datum/dirt_feature/coverage/douse

A douse over the body. Each column's top edge is read from the body's own pixels, as gravity is down in every view.

Vars

drawsIts draws, from dirt_douse_draws().
mouth_columnsThe columns of a mouth in the view it's drawn on, set with tops.
topsEach column's top body row, indexed by column + 1, or null where the column has none. It's set as the first rows are bound (bind_rows()) and kept until the douse is unbound (unbind()).

Procs

atReturns the douse under its column's top edge, and the run-off on the torso. It's film at most over the torso's wandering collar, and clumped for a light material. On a face the cap is a veil, film at most, but a stream down the face stays dark, except on the mouth's columns.
bind_rowsReads each column's top body row from the whole surface, and the view's mouth columns, as its first rows are bound. The mouth columns apply to any head, not only a human's.
unbindFrees the douse for another view. Its tops and mouth columns go with its pixels, because they're read from the view it's bound on.
