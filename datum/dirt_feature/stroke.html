stroke
/datum/dirt_feature/stroke

A streak between two surface points, tapering toward its end. A fringe draws a film edge round it. A fluid's drip goes without, since a fringe would dither a 1 px streak. A fringe on one side only keeps two streaks from merging into one blob.

Vars

film_sideThe side its fringe keeps to, 1 or -1 (which side of the line from its start to its end), or 0 for both.
fringeWhether a film edge runs round it.
span_acrossPixels round the limb from its start to its end.
span_squaredIts length in pixels, squared.
span_upPixels up the limb from its start to its end.
start_alongThe h it starts at.
start_thetaThe theta it starts at.
taperHow much of its width it loses by its end.
widthIts width at the start, in pixels.

Procs

NewStarts a streak.
thicknessReturns the body tone within the streak's half width of its line, and a film fringe 0.7 px past that. A fringe that keeps to one side draws on that side only.
