stamps
/datum/dirt_feature/stamps

Marks put on the screen rather than on a limb, because a face and hands are too small to wrap dirt round. The face takes them on a human head only. A cause picks each cheek's and each hand's level, which needn't be the deposit's. dirt_overlay_stamps() lays them over the deposit's other marks, never on an eye or a mouth.

Vars

coveredWhether the deposit covers the face, which runs the dirtier cheek's smudge on down to the jaw.
face_levelThe dirtier cheek's level, 0 for none.
hand_levelsalist(hand zone = level): each hand's level, 0 or less for none.
other_face_levelThe other cheek's level, 0 for none.
sideThe dirtier side, 1 (the body's right) or -1.

Procs

NewStarts the marks.
pixelsReturns the marks in one view: alist(index = DIRT_ROLE_* define, DIRT_STAMP_CLEAN or DIRT_STAMP_LIT). The other cheek's marks never replace the dirtier cheek's body tone, and a hand's marks replace body tone only to wipe it clean. Facing north the backs of the hands show, a level lighter. From level 5 they keep their level. Drawn at level 4, a solid hand would have its highlight wiped clean, which leaves a bright hole.
