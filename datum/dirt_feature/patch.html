patch
/datum/dirt_feature/patch

A worn-in patch that breaks up toward its edge with noise. Below full strength it draws film only.

Vars

densityHow much of it is drawn at its middle, from 0 to 1.
radius_xIts radius round the limb, in pixels.
radius_yIts radius up the limb, in pixels.
scaleThe noise's lattice spacing, in pixels.
strength1 to draw the body tone where it's thickest, less for film only.

Procs

NewStarts a patch.
thicknessReturns film where its noise passes a bar rising toward its edge, or the body tone where it's high at full strength.
