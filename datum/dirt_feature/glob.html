glob
/datum/dirt_feature/glob

A rounded lump of goo where it landed: thickest underneath, a thin film round it, its outline wobbling a little with noise. A glint (/datum/dirt_feature/glint) goes on top.

Vars

radius_xIts radius round the limb, in pixels.
radius_yIts radius up the limb, in pixels.
raggedHow far its outline wobbles, as a fraction of its radius.
undersideWhether its lower part is the thickest. A drop has none.

Procs

NewStarts a lump.
thicknessReturns the body tone in its wobbling outline, contact low in an underside, and film in a ring 0.2 radii past it.
