speck
/datum/dirt_feature/speck

One speck: a pixel in the body tone, or a 1x2 film drip. Specks are drawn after the coats and kept clear of them and of each other (dirt_scatter()). A strict speck (kicked up) never shares a row or a column with another. A loose one (splashed) only keeps a pixel clear.

Vars

dripWhether it's a drip.
strictWhether it keeps off the rows and columns of the strict specks before it.
