coatline
/datum/dirt_feature/coatline

A coat up from a limb's lower end. It's contact at the bottom and the body tone up to a jagged line. The line tilts from front to back, and at levels 2 and 3 it rises in a tongue with a film tip. Film runs along under the line, so the coat's edge follows the line and is never one level row. One or two 1x2 film windows break the body tone.

Procs
