mantle
/datum/dirt_feature/mantle

What settles from above: a coat down from a limb's top. It's deepest on the side it faces, and the back takes back of its depth. It reaches a pixel and a half deeper on the dirtier side, and large noise roughens its lower edge. It's body over the top body_fraction of it, film under that, then a haze fringe: a gradient, never a bib with a hard hem. Bare skin takes it broken: a step lighter, with about half the skin left clean.
