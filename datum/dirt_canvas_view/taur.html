taur
/datum/dirt_canvas_view/taur

A taur body in one direction as its contact coats read it: its silhouette, with every layer's states together, and each pixel's distance through it to the floor. It's also a surface as dirt_halo() reads one: the body's pixels lie on it, and those with a side off the body are its rim.

Vars

distancesEach pixel's distance through the body to the nearest pixel touching the floor, indexed by pixel. Null where no path through the body reaches the floor. Steps across and down count 1, diagonals 1.414.
feetalist(index = TRUE) on the pixels touching the floor that are hooves, paws or leg tips: not a resting coil.
maskalist(index = TRUE) on the taur body's pixels.
undersidealist(index = TRUE) on its pixels that have no pixel of its own directly under them.

Procs

NewReads a taur body's silhouette in one direction and measures it.
measureMeasures each pixel's distance through the body to the floor (distances), by Dijkstra's search from the pixels touching it, nearest first. Each pixel ends at the least of its neighbours' distances plus the step to it, whatever order they're taken in. So ties need no order of their own.
