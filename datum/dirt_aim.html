dirt_aim
/datum/dirt_aim

A mess aimed at a zone: one deposit's random draws, made once, and the features each level draws from them, as the reference generator's aimed cause draws them.

  • Level 1: one streak laid along the shot's direction, ending in a small lump with a glint, and a drop. On the butt a shot beads and runs down instead, as streaks across the buttocks read as a crack or a scrawl.
  • Levels 2 and 3: level 1, then separate runs straight down instead of fills, each from a bead with a one-pixel glint, with skin between them in every view (dirt_runs_apart()). Fills read as a bib, a vest or a dressing. The face keeps level 1's look up to level 3, with more streaks with drips, broken into beads at level 3.
  • Level 4, the top: level 3, then a glaze over it from draws of its own: two translucent tongues hanging past the zone, three beads with glints and runs, and two small pools.

Every draw happens whatever the level, so the levels nest, each level its own features from the same draws.

Proc Details

New
(zone, variant)

Makes a deposit's draws from the "aimed_:" stream, or takes those already made for the same zone and variant. They're the same wherever it lands, so each is drawn once a round.

Arguments:

  • zone: A DIRT_ZONE_* define, not DIRT_ZONE_COCK.
  • variant: 0 to DIRT_VARIANTS - 1.

proc
capped
(limb, top, bottom, seed, knee, /list/runs)

Returns a drip's or a run's bottom. On the thighs it ends above the knee, at its own inset, and a matching run on the other leg is halved. Elsewhere it's left as it is.

Arguments:

  • limb: The DIRT_LIMB_* define it runs down.
  • top: The h it starts at.
  • bottom: The h it would end at.
  • seed: The shot's seed.
  • knee: The lowest h of its area.
  • runs: The thighs' drips so far, each list(limb, top, bottom), which it adds to. Only the thighs read it, so streaked() passes none: its thigh streaks never drip.

proc
covered
() as /list

Returns the features of the top level, the glaze: level 3's exactly, then on top, from the deposit's own draws:

  • two translucent tongues hanging from where they landed, running past the zone.
  • three opaque beads, each with a one-pixel glint and a run straight down ending in a bulb.
  • two small pools with their grey underside, a translucent rim and a glint. A tongue that reaches the torso's last row carries on down the leg under it, never onto the arms. Seen in profile, where the arm hides the back, the back's coat shows as a thin run-off down the back of the near leg. The coat fades at an outline and breaks near a profile's (dirt_rasterise_level()). The opaque runs keep apart from level 3's and each other, as runs do. Side by side they fuse into panels that read as garments. The beads, the pools and their glints stay where they land.

proc
features
(level) as /list

Returns the features of one level, made once. You should NEVER modify the list it returns.

Arguments:

  • level: 1 to DIRT_MESS_MAX_LEVEL.

proc
glint_at
(limb, end_theta, end_along, glint_type) as /datum/dirt_feature/glint

Returns the glint over a streak's end or a bead: 0.3 px back round the limb and 0.3 px up.

Arguments:

  • limb: The DIRT_LIMB_* define it lies on.
  • end_theta, end_along: The streak's end or the bead.
  • glint_type: The glint's type. /datum/dirt_feature/glint/highlight lights one pixel only.

proc
hang_runs
()

Makes levels 2 and 3's runs and drops, once for both: hung_groups, hung_early and hung_anchor.

proc
hanging
(limb, top_theta, top_along, spread, seed, knee, carry, /list/runs) as /list

Returns a run hung straight down from a point, ending in a 2 px bulb. One carried on goes past its bulb to a smaller one from level 3. With no room for another run, a level-up would show nothing.

Arguments:

  • limb: The DIRT_LIMB_* define it hangs on.
  • top_theta, top_along: Where it hangs from.
  • spread: Its drip draw, 0.2 to 0.8, which sets its length.
  • seed: The shot's seed.
  • knee: The lowest h of its area, which a thigh's run stays above.
  • carry: Whether it carries on past its bulb at level 3.
  • runs: The thighs' drips so far (capped()).

proc
hung
(level) as /list

Returns the features of levels 2 and 3 off the face: level 1's exactly, then runs straight down instead of fills. Level 1's streak hangs a run from part of its length (level 2) and level 1's drop one from its middle (level 3). Later shots come from the twelve drawn, in draw order. Each is a bead with a one-pixel glint and one run straight down, at level 1's length. It lands at least DIRT_AIM_RUN_PITCH px round its limb from every shot and from level 1's drop. The other drops follow. A run plan holds level 3's set, so each view keeps the same ones at every level.

Where level 2 shows nothing new in the zone's main facing, level 1's drop runs a little to a smaller bead, so every level-up shows (dirt_runs_apart() decides).

Arguments:

  • level: 2 or 3.

proc
land
(limb, start_theta, start_along, aim, streak_length, seed, /list/area) as /list

Returns where a streak starts and ends: list(its start's h, its end's theta, its end's h). It's laid along the shot, then held in its zone's heights where the zone bounds them.

Arguments:

  • limb: The DIRT_LIMB_* define it lies on.
  • start_theta, start_along: Where it starts.
  • aim: The streak's direction, in degrees.
  • streak_length: Its length in pixels.
  • seed: The shot's seed.
  • area: The area it landed in, list(limb, lowest theta, highest theta, lowest h, highest h).

proc
odd_side
(limb, theta)

Returns whether a streak's or a drip's insets are odd: odd on one side of a pair, even on the other, the shot's direction picking which. On the thighs the sides are the legs, elsewhere either side of the midline.

Arguments:

  • limb: The DIRT_LIMB_* define it lies on.
  • theta: Where it starts round the limb, which the thighs don't read.

proc
shot_counts
() as /list

Returns how many shots streak, or hang runs, at each level: fewer for a shot that beads. You should NEVER modify it.

proc
streaked
(level) as /list

Returns the features of level 1, and of the face's levels 2 and 3: streaks laid along the shot, each ending in a small lump with a glint, drips running down from them from level 2, and drops round them. At level 3 each streak breaks into beads.

Arguments:

  • level: 1 to 3.

proc
tilted
(aim)

Returns a groin streak's direction turned 45 degrees off upright, on the same side, as two side by side up the belly read as bars. It mirrors the reference generator's arctangent of fixed sines, not a literal 45 or 135.

Arguments:
