landing
/datum/dirt_job/landing

A landing that waits for a later SSdirt fire (dirt_source_land()). What it lands is fixed when the event happens.

Vars

causeA DIRT_CAUSE_* define.
colourSlime's colour, "#RRGGBB", with DIRT_MATERIAL_SLIME.
levelThe deposit's level, which dirt_land() keeps from 1 up to the cause's top.
materialA DIRT_MATERIAL_* define.
target_refA weak reference to the person it lands on.

Procs

NewNotes what lands, as dirt_land() takes it, and on whom.
finishedCounts it out of SSdirt.landings_waiting.
next_pieceLands it in one piece, unless the person was deleted or is now the plain monkey species.
