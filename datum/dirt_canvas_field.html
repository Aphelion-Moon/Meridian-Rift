dirt_canvas_field
/datum/dirt_canvas_field

A general deposit's levels on a whole canvas in one direction, as dirt_canvas_rasterise() draws them before keeping hair to its crown. It's drawn some rows at a time (draw_rows()). A mask job draws a field once, for all the directions it serves (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/field_for()), a band a piece (dirt_canvas_field_band()). In each of those directions the field is clipped to its shape, except on a taur body. It keeps the noise its levels share (samples) and the lattice its bands sample (dirt_drafted()). A field lives no longer than the job drawing it.

materialThe /datum/dirt_material it's made of.
mergedThe levels drawn so far, stacked. Once it's done, this is the whole field.
next_rowThat level's next row, from 0.
samplesThe noise its levels share, as alist(term = alist(position = value)). The first level that asks samples it (dirt_field_spread_noise()), and the later ones read it. A position is a pixel's index, or the one coordinate its term takes. A term's other arguments are the same at every level, drawn alike from the same stream or fixed. Every band of a field is drawn in one drafting state. So a kept value is what sampling again returns. A mask job's fields for one deposit share one table in every direction (/datum/dirt_mask_job/var/field_samples). They can share it, because a term's point and seed come from its position and the deposit's random draws, whichever way the field faces. The field drops its reference once it's done.
seedThe seed text of the random stream each level's features are drawn from.
sideThe dirtier side, 1 (the body's right) or -1.
surface_kindDIRT_SURFACE_FUR, DIRT_SURFACE_CLOTH, DIRT_SURFACE_SKIN or DIRT_SURFACE_HAIR.
viewThe canvas's view in the direction.

Procs

NewArguments:
draw_rowsDraws the field's next rows. As a level begins it makes the level's features and coats, then it draws its rows. Once the last row is drawn, it adds the level's finish (dirt_canvas_finish_level()) and stacks the level on the ones before, the later winning a tie. After the last level, its samples go. A cause the canvas doesn't draw ends the field. Call it while drafting.

proc
draw_rows
(rows)

Draws the field's next rows. As a level begins it makes the level's features and coats, then it draws its rows. Once the last row is drawn, it adds the level's finish (dirt_canvas_finish_level()) and stacks the level on the ones before, the later winning a tie. After the last level, its samples go. A cause the canvas doesn't draw ends the field. Call it while drafting.

Arguments:

  • rows: The most rows to draw.

Returns whether every level is drawn.
