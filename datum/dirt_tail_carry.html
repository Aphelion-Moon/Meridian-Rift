dirt_tail_carry
/datum/dirt_tail_carry

A wag frame's dirt carried from the still sprite's in one direction, some of its pixels at a time (carry_pixels()). Each pixel of the frame's shape takes the dirt of the still's pixel nearest it along the shape and across it (dirt_tail_coordinates()). It takes none where that pixel has none. So the dirt swings and bends with the tail and draws as it does when the tail is still. The still's pixels are looked through a pixel along at a time, out from the frame pixel's place. The look stops one past the first of those that holds any, because one further across can lie nearer.
