garment_fit_job
/datum/garment_fit_job

One garment fit, made a piece at a time (next_piece()): its setup, each view of each direction's frames (fit_garment_view()), and its end, which caches what it made. While it waits, its place in its cache holds the job itself, so a garment drawn meanwhile finds it and waits for it (wait()) rather than queueing it again.

Vars

cacheThe cache list the fit goes in, under cache_key: the fitted sprite, a resource, or FALSE when the fit leaves the garment as it is.
changedWhether a view changed.
delaysAn animated sprite's frame delays.
fitWhat the garment is fitted to.
garmentThe sprite being fitted, a 32x32 /icon with four directions, named "", changed in place.
modeHow it's fitted, a GARMENT_FIT_* define. Worn clothing that doesn't set its own reads it from its sleeves in setup().
next_viewThe next of its views to fit, counted in the order they're fitted (GARMENT_FIT_PIECE_VIEW()).
view_coloursIts views' colours, as fit_garment_view() takes them, by view; or null to read them from its pixels.
viewsHow many views it has, four for each frame, once set up.
waitersWeak references to the bodies that drew the garment as it is while this waited -> GARMENT_FIT_REDRAW_* flags.

proc
start
(/mob/living/carbon/human/wearer, redraw)

Starts the fit for a garment drawn on wearer: queues it, noting wearer to draw again once it's made; or, without GARMENT_FITS_DEFERRED or for a dummy, makes it now.

Arguments:

  • wearer: The body the garment is drawn on.
  • redraw: The GARMENT_FIT_REDRAW_* flag of the slot it's drawn in.

Returns what's cached for the garment: the fitted sprite, or FALSE, once made; this job while it waits.
