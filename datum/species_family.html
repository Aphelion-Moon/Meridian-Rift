species_family
/datum/species_family

A family on the species page.

GLOB.species_page_families files each species under one; see /datum/species/proc/get_species_family(). The page lists the families in sort_order, with the name and icon they give here.

Vars

iconA Font Awesome FA_ICON_* or a tgfont "tg-" icon, as quirks give theirs.
nameShown on the family's tab and above each of its species' names.
sort_orderFamilies are listed lowest first.
