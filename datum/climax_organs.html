climax_organs
/datum/climax_organs

The organs one climax is running with.

Every prompt a climax opens is a chance for the mob to lose an organ, be deleted, or turn their ERP preference off, so none of it survives a prompt on trust. Call release() before opening one so we hold no hard references across the sleep, then reacquire() after it to re-resolve everything and find out whether we can still continue.
