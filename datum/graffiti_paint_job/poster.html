poster
/datum/graffiti_paint_job/poster

A paint job spraying a saved piece over a poster: its paint lands only on the poster's own pixels, over the poster's graffiti, and a stopped piece resumes nowhere.

↑
New
(/mob/living/user, /obj/item/toy/crayon/spraycan/can, /turf/surface, /list/piece, /list/resume, piece_id, /obj/structure/sign/poster/poster)

Sets a job up on a poster. A stopped piece starts over, as a poster keeps nothing to resume on.

Arguments:

  • user: The painter.
  • can: The spray can, in the painter's hand.
  • surface: The poster's tile.
  • piece: A copy of the one-tile canonical piece to paint.
  • resume: Unused.
  • piece_id: The library piece painted, or "unfinished" for the Unfinished slot's piece.
  • poster: The poster.

↑
credit_surface
()

Marks the poster as the painter's work.

↑
paint
(progress)

Shows the piece over the poster's graffiti as far as progress reveals it. Its time counts against every painter's composites (SSgraffiti's spend_composite()), and the first step whose paint shows charges the can (charge()).

Arguments:

  • progress: Strips revealed, at least 1.

Returns:

  • TRUE/FALSE: FALSE once the poster is gone or its graffiti washed off, which stops the job.

↑
piece_records
(/list/piece)

Each layer with paint left only on the poster's own pixels, so the job's time counts only the paint that shows.
