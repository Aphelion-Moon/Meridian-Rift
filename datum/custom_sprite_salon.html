custom_sprite_salon
/datum/custom_sprite_salon

One artist's custom work for one recipient and drawing target.

Hair work covers one drawing. Tattoo work covers every region of the body, and only the regions the draft changes are proposed, checked and applied. States: drafting -> awaiting approval -> applying -> completed. Declining, interruption or a stale approval returns to drafting and keeps the draft. Approval binds to an immutable copy of the exact reviewed revision, and every step checks the participants again.

complete_application
(token, finished)

Applies the approved packages after the timed action. Every check runs again first.

Only the drawings the proposal changes are applied, and each keeps its previous look as round history. Achievements are awarded once per application.

Returns:

  • TRUE: Applied. The session is deleted, so a replayed call finds nothing to apply.
  • FALSE: Stale, duplicate, interrupted or invalid. Nothing changed.

proc
part_problem
(/mob/living/carbon/human/recipient, key)

Why a drawing's part can't take work right now, or null.

The part must be the one work started on: the same bodypart, and the same taur organ for the taur region. It must also be reachable: not missing, covered, husked or hidden. This is cheap enough to run for every region on every stroke.

proc
participant_problem
(/list/keys)

Returns why the work can't proceed, or null when it can.

This covers both participants and their controlling players, consciousness, the held tool and reachability, then each drawing it checks: the part it's on, whether that can be reached, and its look since work started.

Arguments:

  • keys: Style keys of the drawings to check. By default the proposal's, or the drawings this work would change as it stands.
