graffiti_manager
/datum/graffiti_manager

One admin's Graffiti Manager window: the current map's graffiti a page at a time, to find, look at, jump to and delete.

Vars

pageThe page shown, from 1.
searchWhat a listed placement's ckey or painter's character name holds, escaped as character names are kept, or "" to list them all.
sortThe rows' order: "date" for the newest first, "ckey" or "area".

Procs

confirmAsks the admin to confirm a deletion. The question waits for their answer, so this also checks they are still an admin once they give it.
placements_withReturns the map's placements whose field has a value, such as one account's or one piece's.
reportLogs an admin's deletion and tells the other admins.
ui_actHandles the window's actions. Each deletion asks the admin first, then is logged and told to the other admins with how many placements went. Nothing is deleted while the map's graffiti is still loading.
ui_dataLists one page of the map's graffiti.
ui_interactOpens the window, or refreshes the open one. It refreshes only after its own actions, as listing the rows reads every placement.
ui_stateAdmins only.

Proc Details

proc
confirm
(/mob/user, question)

Asks the admin to confirm a deletion. The question waits for their answer, so this also checks they are still an admin once they give it.

Arguments:

  • user: The admin.
  • question: What goes, and how much of it.

Returns:

  • TRUE/FALSE: Whether to go ahead.

proc
placements_with
(key, value)

Returns the map's placements whose field has a value, such as one account's or one piece's.

Arguments:

  • key: The field, such as "ckey" or "pid".
  • value: Its value.

Returns:

  • list: The packed placements, oldest first.

proc
report
(/mob/user, text)

Logs an admin's deletion and tells the other admins.

Arguments:

  • user: The admin.
  • text: What they did, after their name.

↑
ui_act
(action, /list/params, /datum/tgui/ui, /datum/ui_state/state)

Handles the window's actions. Each deletion asks the admin first, then is logged and told to the other admins with how many placements went. Nothing is deleted while the map's graffiti is still loading.

Arguments:

  • action: The action.
  • params: Its params, as the window sent them.
  • ui: The window.
  • state: The window's state.

Returns:

  • TRUE/FALSE: Whether the window updates.

↑
ui_data
(/mob/user)

Lists one page of the map's graffiti.

Arguments:

  • user: The admin.

Returns:

  • list: SSgraffiti.admin_rows()'s "rows" and "total", with "search", "sort", "page", "pageSize" and "mapName".

↑
ui_interact
(/mob/user, /datum/tgui/ui)

Opens the window, or refreshes the open one. It refreshes only after its own actions, as listing the rows reads every placement.

Arguments:

  • user: The admin.
  • ui: The open window, when tgui refreshes it.

↑
ui_state
(/mob/user)

Admins only.

Arguments:

  • user: The admin.

Returns:

  • /datum/ui_state: The admin state.
