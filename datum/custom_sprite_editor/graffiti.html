graffiti
/datum/custom_sprite_editor/graffiti

The graffiti editor: the custom sprite editor's shell on a graffiti piece, whose canvas, tools and history it reuses.

A studio session edits a piece for its owner's library, which its saves go into. A surface session paints live on a wall, a floor or a full-tile window: the piece goes on top of the tile's stack at its first stroke and is recomposited there at a paced cadence, the can is charged once the session ends with paint on the surface, and a piece it didn't save goes in the library's Unfinished slot, ready to resume on that tile.

The editor belongs to the account it is registered to in SSgraffiti.editors, and ends with its window.

proc
end_session
(/mob/user, keep)

Ends the session and closes its window. The strokes still waiting go in first. A surface session leaves its piece on the surface through leave_surface(). A piece that changed since it was opened or saved goes in the library's Unfinished slot, under the name and description its library piece has (take_library_names()), resumable on the surface while it is still there. A pane loosened under the piece drops all of its paint first, both halves of a two-tile piece, and the piece goes in the slot as a plain draft, with no tile to resume on.

Arguments:

  • user: The owner.
  • keep: Whether the piece is kept. An admin deleting its paint ends the session with nothing kept: no last composite, no charge and no Unfinished piece.

proc
end_surface
(/mob/user, /list/piece, keep)

Ends a surface session on its surface. A kept piece stays there through leave_surface(), unless a pane loosened under it, which drops all of its paint first. Either way the tiles' decals let go of their merges.

Arguments:

  • user: The painter.
  • piece: The piece as it ends, or null when nothing is painted.
  • keep: Whether the piece is kept, as end_session() takes it.

Returns:

  • list: Where the Unfinished piece resumes: the map, its left tile's anchor and its placement id.
  • null: It resumes nowhere.

leave_surface
(/mob/user, /list/piece)

Leaves a surface session's piece on the surface as the session ends: composited one last time, without an empty piece or a half of a two-tile piece left without paint. With paint left, the can is charged once, never refused, unless the session resumed a draft and changed nothing, and each tile the piece is on is marked as the painter's work and the painter given the graffiti achievement, as for a finished paint job.

Arguments:

  • user: The painter.
  • piece: The piece as it ends, or null for no last composite: nothing is painted, or a pane loosened under it.

Returns:

  • list: The placements the piece left on the surface, packed.

proc
place_tile
(packed, /turf/tile, /list/art, half, /mob/user, /list/decoded, /list/evicted)

Puts one tile's art of the piece on the surface: as new art for the tile's placement, or as a new placement once the art has paint. The halves of a two-tile piece share one placement id. A tile that refuses the paint, as a full map of connected painters' graffiti does, isn't asked again for GRAFFITI_PLACE_RETRY; meanwhile its refusal shows again, and a placement it has keeps its old art.

Arguments:

  • packed: The tile's placement so far, packed, or null.
  • tile: The tile.
  • art: The tile's art.
  • half: 1 or 2 for the left or right half of a two-tile piece, else 0.
  • user: The painter.
  • decoded: The art's graffiti_art_decode().
  • evicted: Filled with the ids of the painter's own placements a new one took past their caps, for apply_live() to tell the painter once.

Returns:

  • text: The tile's placement now, packed. One that is gone stays as it was, so the session sees it is gone, and so does one refused its new art.
  • null: The tile has none: its art has no paint yet, or the surface refused it.

save_drawing
(/mob/user, copy)

Saves the piece into its owner's library: over its library piece, or as a new one the first time or when asked. An unchanged piece is acknowledged without writing, and writes wait for graffiti_library_write_due(). The piece keeps the name and description its library piece has (take_library_names()), so a rename alone writes nothing.

Arguments:

  • user: The owner.
  • copy: Whether to save a new piece rather than over this one.

Returns:

  • TRUE/FALSE: Whether the library holds the piece as it is now. On FALSE the status line says why.

proc
tile_background
(/turf/tile, packed)

Returns the graffiti a tile shows beneath this session's piece, which the window draws under the canvas. A stack the decal keeps no merge of, as one shown from its sheet, is merged here, as the session opens, which GRAFFITI_START_COOLDOWN paces, and the decal keeps the merge while the session paints there.

Arguments:

  • tile: The tile.
  • packed: This session's placement on it, packed, or null.

Returns:

  • list: The merged art of the pieces beneath this session's, or of the whole stack before it has one there.
  • null: Nothing else shows there, or someone has painted over this session's piece.
