poster
/datum/custom_sprite_editor/graffiti/poster

The graffiti editor on a poster: a freehand session whose canvas takes paint only on the poster's own pixels, as the hair and markings editors keep paint to the body, with the poster and its graffiti beneath the canvas. The piece goes on the poster rather than in the map's graffiti, one tile wide, and resumes nowhere: an unsaved piece is kept as a plain draft.

end_surface
(/mob/user, /list/piece, keep)

Ends the session on the poster: a kept piece stays there (leave_surface()), and none resumes there.

Arguments:

  • user: The painter.
  • piece: The piece as it ends, or null when nothing is painted.
  • keep: Whether the piece is kept.

Returns:

  • null: A poster's piece resumes nowhere.

↑
leave_surface
(/mob/user, /list/piece)

Leaves the piece over the poster as the session ends, composited one last time, as on a wall: with paint left, the can is charged once, the poster is marked as the painter's work and the painter given the graffiti achievement. A piece that paints nothing leaves the poster as it was.

Arguments:

  • user: The painter.
  • piece: The piece as it ends, or null when nothing is painted.

Returns:

  • list: Empty: no placement holds a poster's piece.
