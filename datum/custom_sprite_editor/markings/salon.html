salon
/datum/custom_sprite_editor/markings/salon

The salon's tattoo context: the whole-body canvas on another player's live look.

Every region starts from the recipient's live paint and base markings. Only regions the draft changes are proposed, and the session checks each of them before anything is applied.

A region is locked exactly when finishing would refuse work on it: its part is covered, husked, hidden, missing or replaced, or its look changed on the body since work started. Paint drafted there stays, visible under the scanlines, until it unlocks.
