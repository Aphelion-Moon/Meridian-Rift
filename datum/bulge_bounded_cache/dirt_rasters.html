dirt_rasters
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_rasters

Keeps the newest DIRT_RASTER_CACHE_SIZE splats' rasters, each one text: its drawn() in each direction, in dirt_mask_directions() order, joined by DIRT_RASTER_SEPARATOR, empty where it isn't rasterised yet.

store_direction
(key, direction, drawn)
