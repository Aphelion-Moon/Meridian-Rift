dirt_wag_source
/datum/dirt_wag_source
inherits /datum/dirt_shape_source

A tail's sprite read wagging, with its states animated. Each frame is read as it's made, from sprites its job holds (dirt_frame_owners()). Each takes the still sprite's dirt, carried along the tail (/datum/dirt_tail_carry), and is baked into one animated icon for each layer (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/bake_frame()). The source is made once. Only shows_still changes, once a job finds it out.

Vars

framesThe most frames a layer has, which is how many frames are made.
layer_framesalist(EXTERNAL_* layer key = list(each frame's delay, whether it rewinds)), from the layer's state with the most frames, so its dirt plays as that state does.
shows_stillWhether its one frame shows the still sprite's pixels in every direction, once a job has found it does. If so, the still masks draw while it wags, and no job is asked for.
state_framesalist(state = its frame count).
stillThe part read still. Every frame takes its dirt, carried along it, or as it is where a frame shows its pixels.

Procs

sprite_keyReturns what the wag's frames and its still sprite draw, as text. SSdirt.wag_sprites keeps what its frames show under this key (/datum/dirt_mask_job/var/sprite_frames). Wags with the same key show the same pixels in every frame, whatever their dirt or colours.
