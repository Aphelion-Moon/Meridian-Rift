cover_field
/datum/dirt_canvas_feature/cover_field

A plunge's or a blast's coat, head to toe at level 3, from the body's own field. It samples the same random draws in mob space, so the lines meet. A light material clumps where its noise marks a point as facing up.

Vars

causeDIRT_CAUSE_PLUNGE or DIRT_CAUSE_BLAST.
drawsIts draws, from dirt_cover_draws().
facing_atFor a blast, alist(pixels across = how squarely a point there faces it), each worked out the first time its column asks. A feature is made for one level of one field, so the list belongs to one view.
glossyWhether its material is glossy (DIRT_MATERIAL_GLOSSY), set by the rasteriser.
levelIts level, 1 to 3.
lightWhether its material is light (DIRT_MATERIAL_LIGHT), set by the rasteriser.
