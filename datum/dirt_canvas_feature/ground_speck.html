ground_speck
/datum/dirt_canvas_feature/ground_speck

One kick-up speck over a ground coat: a pixel of the body tone, or a 1x2 film drip, placed after the coats. In profile it lies along the body. Facing south only the specks at the front show, and facing north those at the back.

Vars

alongHow far along the body, front +.
dripWhether it's a drip.
lateralHow far across it.
up_atIts height over the floor.

Procs

first_pixelReturns the first pixel, in row-major order, where the speck lies thick enough for film, or null. It lies within 0.6 px across and from 1.05 px under to 0.6 px over its height. So only the rows and columns round there are tried.
placeReturns where across the view the speck lies, in pixels across from the tile's middle, or null where the view doesn't show it.
thicknessReturns, where the view shows the speck, the body tone within 0.6 px of it. A drip draws film instead, a pixel across and down to a pixel under it.
