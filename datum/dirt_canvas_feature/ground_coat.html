ground_coat
/datum/dirt_canvas_feature/ground_coat

Mud or dust up from the floor. It's solid where it meets the floor, then the body tone up to a jagged line. The line tilts along the body and drags up in one place, its tongue. Film follows under the line, haze lies just over it, and a film window or two break the body tone.

Vars

filmHow many pixels of film follow under the line.
line_atalist(pixels across = the line's height there), each worked out the first time its column asks. A feature is made for one level of one field, so the list belongs to one view.
solidThe height up to which it lies solid, from the floor.
tiltHow far the line climbs from the body's middle to 16 px along it.
tongueWhere the line drags up the body, list(along, across, how much higher, how wide), or null.
topThe line's height over the floor at the body's middle.
windowsThe film windows, each list(pixels across, pixels up).

Procs

lineReturns the line's height over the floor at a point of the body. Facing south or north, the tilt along the body is the same everywhere, so the line tilts sideways and jags instead.
thicknessReturns solid up to the solid height, and haze just over the line. Under the line it's film in its first pixels and the windows, and the body tone below.
