dirt_shape
/datum/dirt_shape

A damp patch's shape. It gives each point's distance from the shape's middle, 1 at its edge. A point is given in pixels round the limb and up it from where the patch is anchored.

extentWhere its distance is 1.6 or less, in pixels from the anchor: list(least across, most across, least up, most up).
