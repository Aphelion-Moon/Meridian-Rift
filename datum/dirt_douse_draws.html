dirt_douse_draws
/datum/dirt_douse_draws

Every random draw a douse makes, whatever its level, in the reference generator's order. Anything else drawn from the same field makes the same draws from the same random stream (dirt_douse_draws()). So a stream of the douse down one shape meets the one down the next.
