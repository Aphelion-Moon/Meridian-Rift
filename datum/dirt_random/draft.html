draft
/datum/dirt_random/draft

The original draft's stream: Python's random.Random(seed text), word for word, so a general deposit draws as the draft drew it. A Mersenne twister (MT19937) seeded from text as CPython seeds it, on 16-bit halves, every product through bytes so nothing passes 2^24. random() is CPython's 53-bit fraction rounded to float32, as the reference rounds it. randrange(), randint(), choose() and shuffle() take whole draws as CPython's _randbelow() does, rejecting those past the count. gauss() keeps the second of each pair for its next call. choices() walks the weights' running sums to the first past random() times their total. That picks what CPython's bisect picks. draw() and sample() are the portable stream's alone, and crash on this one.

Every stream of one seed text shares its words (SSdirt.draft_words), so a text is seeded once while it's kept.
