bulge_sheet_state
/datum/bulge_sheet_state

One state of a template sheet and where it sits: its sheet, its name and the pixel offsets of the sheet's window. A state derived at run time (a shape past small_cap, which is also the anatomy it leaves bare, or a loincloth's cut) has no sheet and sits on the whole tile: only its name, and its pixels are never read from a file.

Vars

iconThe sheet; null for a derived state.
icon_stateThe state's name.
pixel_xOffset of the sheet's window on the 32x32 tile, from its left.
pixel_yOffset of the sheet's window on the 32x32 tile, from its bottom.
shows_anyWhether the state has any opaque pixel, for a mask: null until bulge_mask_shows() reads it.
