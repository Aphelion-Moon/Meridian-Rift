dirt_taur_draws
/datum/dirt_taur_draws

Every random draw a taur coat makes. They don't depend on its level, so its levels nest. A speck's or a tongue's pick is kept as its random(), and turned into a place once the list it picks from is known (dirt_taur_picked()).

Vars

reachHow far through the body the coat's line reaches at each level.
seedThe seed of its noise, a whole number.
specksIts specks, each list(pick, how far past the line, whether it's a drip).
tonguesIts tongues, each list(pick, how much higher the line climbs there).
