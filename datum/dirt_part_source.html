dirt_part_source
/datum/dirt_part_source

A part's sprite as mess reads it: the images a genital draws in the layers the reference generator draws that part from (dirt_part_layers()), their colour layers' states taken together, or the plain pecs' shape a human chest without breasts takes its mess in (dirt_pecs_guide()). A mess aimed at the part's zone takes its share on these pixels (dirt_part_own()). Made once and never changed.

Procs

NewArguments:
layer_atReturns the layer of the topmost image that shows the source in one direction: the least layer number of its states with pixels there (dirt_sprite_facings()). INFINITY when none has (the guide lies under everything). A butt shows its front image facing north and its back image, under the body's skin, the other ways. Worked out once for every direction.
layeredReturns whether the source's images draw at more than one layer, so what it draws over can change with direction.
part_keyReturns what the source draws, as text: sources with the same key take mess on the same pixels.

read_layersWorks out layer_at() in every direction, and top_layer().
top_layerReturns the layer of the topmost image the source draws in any direction.
underReturns whether the source draws under a surface in one direction, as its topmost image there does (layer_at()), so its share is drawn in that surface's masks rather than on itself.
