muscles
/datum/preference/numeric/muscles

A character's muscle size or definition, picked while their muscles don't follow their stats; shown on a slider, with each step's name. One for the whole body. Applied before species, as the leg shape is, so a species change builds the limbs at it; with the species unchanged, the limbs are swapped here.
