cyborg_sprite
/datum/preference/choiced/cyborg_sprite

Character preferences consumed by the cyborg creator and live renderer. Layouts live in the cyborg_customization.json sidecar. The ones only cyborgs have are manually rendered - the editor window shows them, character setup leaves them out.
