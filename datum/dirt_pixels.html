dirt_pixels
/datum/dirt_pixels

One mask's pixels: a tone role and a thickness for each. Lists are indexed y * width + x + 1 in the reference generator's coordinates, as a /datum/dirt_surface's are: x from the left and y from the top, both from 0.

Vars

coversWhether it's a splat at the top level, which covers: where splats merge, it keeps every piece (dirt_merge()).
drawn_indexesFor a mask made again from a cached raster (dirt_pixels_from_drawn()), the pixels it was drawn on, so a merge walks those alone; null for one drawn here. Pixels cleared since stay listed.
heightPixels down.
rolesEach pixel's DIRT_ROLE_* define, or null where nothing is drawn.
thicknessesEach drawn pixel's thickness, from 0 to 1. Where masks merge, the thicker one wins a pixel its role ties on.
widthPixels across.

Procs

NewStarts an empty mask.
copyReturns a copy of the mask, its own lists.
drawnReturns a mask's drawn pixels alone, which is all a cached raster keeps, packed as text: "1" if it covers, else "0", then three characters a drawn pixel, in row-major order, for its index, role and thickness (dirt_thickness_code()). Equal masks pack as equal text. SSdirt.rasters keeps a splat's four directions joined in one text, its key's own. Three base-64 characters hold indexes up to 2048, the 64x32 taur canvas: a bigger mask can't be packed.
grid_textReturns the mask as text, one line per row joined by newlines, one glyph per pixel: "." none, "h" haze, "f" film, "b" body, "c" core, "k" fleck, "g" glint.
put_indexDraws a role on a pixel of the mask by its index.
