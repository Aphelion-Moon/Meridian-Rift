art_stylesheet
/datum/asset/simple/art_stylesheet

Art too big for tgui's bundle, which every window loads and the lobby inlines: textures and fonts sent as asset files, with a stylesheet written when the asset registers that names them by the URLs the asset transport gives them. Each texture becomes a custom property on scope, property_prefix and its file's name without the extension, and each font a face. A page loads the stylesheet as soon as it hears of it (loadMappings).

The URLs are written in because Chrome resolves a relative url() in a custom property against the stylesheet that uses it, not the one that declares it.
