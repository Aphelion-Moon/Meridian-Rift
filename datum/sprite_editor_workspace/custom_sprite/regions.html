regions
/datum/sprite_editor_workspace/custom_sprite/regions

The whole-body markings canvas.

Every pixel of paint belongs to a region, so every tool, the eraser included, stays inside the regions. Fill stops at region edges and Clear works on one region. markings_context and emissive are zone-keyed maps here; replace transactions swap them whole, so undo restores them.

Vars

lockedRegion characters no tool may change right now, such as regions worn clothing covers.
region_mapDirection -> row strings naming the region that owns each pixel, "0" for none.
resetsZone -> views ("2" -> TRUE) that Clear, an import or a restoration replaced outright. Paint other limbs cover there goes too.

Replaces the canvas, the per-region base markings and emission as one undoable action.

new_resets, when given, also changes which regions are replaced outright in which views.

Returns TRUE, replaced or already identical: regions may hold more than CUSTOM_SPRITE_MAX_COLORS colors between them (see update_palette()), so no replacement is refused for its colors.
