leash
/datum/beam/held/leash

The leash's line, blank while the ends are out of reach. It leaves the outer edge of the holding hand, with offsets read off the human body sprite. West mirrors east.

Vars

held_indexThe holding hand's index. Hands past the first pair draw held items shifted, so the line shifts too.
staleTRUE while a redraw is waiting for a later move or catch_up().
steppingTRUE while an end steps with the other end's move, which draws the line once done.

Procs

DrawDraws only while the ends are on one z-level and in reach. Skips CheckToolReach(), whose dummy object would fall into a chasm under the line.
add_extra_hand_offsetExtra hands, like a moth's chest arms, shift held items, so the line shifts with them.
catch_upDraws a waiting redraw that no move picked up, gliding over the step or ERP_LEASH_TURN_GLIDE for a turn.
handle_dir_changeTurning moves the hand, so the line is redrawn from it this tick.
redrawingDraws the line once a tick, after the last end has moved. A step with the other end's move, or a diagonal's first half, waits for the next move or catch_up(). Blank while an end is inside something or on another z-level, where a normal beam would delete itself.
set_handMoves the line to another of the holder's hands.
update_offsetsA leash on the floor draws its line from itself, not a hand.
