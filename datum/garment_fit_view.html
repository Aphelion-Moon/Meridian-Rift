garment_fit_view
/datum/garment_fit_view

One view of a garment being fitted: its frame, the pixels read from it, and the pixels the fitting writes over them.

Pixels are addressed as the templates give them, from the top left from 0. A pixel is a colour as GetPixel() returns it ("#rrggbb", or "#rrggbbaa" when partly transparent), or 0 where it's clear.

Proc Details

proc
apply_bands
(/list/templates, pattern, /list/sleeves)

One pass of the fitting over the templates' bands. Solid: each row the garment covers at the old edge and the pixel inside it moves its edge pixel out to the new edge and fills the gap with that inside pixel. Pattern: each new pixel copies the pixel 2 further in, wherever the garment covers any of the 3 pixels from the old edge in.

A row a limb grew into above its old top is fitted only under a sleeve, so straps over the shoulder stay put. Under a sleeve, a solid row is also fitted where the garment covers its old edge alone, a hem a pixel thick against the arm's edge, and its gap takes the edge pixel when the one inside it is bare. An arm's sleeve is judged from the garment's front view (sleeves), anything else's from the upper third of its own baseline pixels in this view; a template without them, like a pec's, never has one.

Arguments:

  • templates: The templates, fitted in order: template limb -> its template, or a part of the body by any name -> its template in the same form.
  • pattern: Whether to fit as pattern rather than solid.
  • sleeves: Template limb -> whether the garment has a sleeve over it, from garment_front_sleeves(), or null.

proc
bare_near_forearm
()

Side views, for a garment that leaves the near forearm bare below its sleeve, like the polo shirts (/datum/sprite_accessory/clothing/proc/bares_near_forearm()): the forearm shows at the bigger arm's own width.

A forearm row is one where the garment as drawn leaves some of today's near arm bare. In each, the garment is cleared over the whole near arm of the fitted size. That also takes away the strip a polo edges its cut-out with, which apply_bands() moves out across the bigger arm as it would a sleeve's one-pixel hem. Rows where the garment covers all of today's arm, its sleeve, are left as fitted. The near arm is the right one facing east and the left one facing west; one at the Normal size has no template, and nothing changes.

proc
fit
(mode, pec_shape, pec_nipples, /list/sleeves, straight, bare)

Fits the view to its limbs in mode (fit_limbs()), then to pecs drawn under it, still (fit_pecs()), and draws what changed onto frame.

Arguments:

  • mode: A GARMENT_FIT_* define.
  • pec_shape: The shape of pecs drawn under the garment, as /datum/garment_fit has it, or null.
  • pec_nipples: Whether those pecs draw nipples.
  • sleeves: Fitted arm -> whether the garment has a sleeve over it, from garment_front_sleeves(), or null.
  • straight: Whether the garment hangs straight over those pecs (fit_pecs()).
  • bare: Whether the garment leaves the near forearm bare in its side views at the arm's own width (fit_limbs()).

Returns:

  • TRUE: The frame changed.
  • FALSE: Nothing in this view changed, and the frame is the state's own.

proc
fit_limbs
(mode, /list/sleeves, bare)

Fits the view to its limbs in mode: the arms, the legs and a male chest's glutes, then the mode's own shading, then, in a side view, a sleeve's hem down the bigger arm (close_hem()) and, for a garment that leaves the near forearm bare, that forearm at its own width (bare_near_forearm()).

Arguments:

  • mode: A GARMENT_FIT_* define.
  • sleeves: Fitted arm -> whether the garment has a sleeve over it, from garment_front_sleeves(), or null.
  • bare: Whether the garment leaves the near forearm bare in its side views (/datum/sprite_accessory/clothing/proc/bares_near_forearm()).

proc
fit_pecs
(/list/templates, nipples, mode, straight)

Fits the view, after its limbs, to pecs drawn under the garment.

Front and back views: where a pec reaches the chest's own side, the garment's chest panel moves out over it (pec_panel_bands()), unless the garment hangs straight over the pecs, then the front view shades the pecs' lower curve, in the modes the GARMENT_FIT_PEC_SMILE_* defines pick (shade_pec_smile()). Side views: the pec's bulge past the body's front is fitted as a limb's bands are.

Arguments:

  • templates: The pecs' templates by view: their shape's still ones, or its ones on one frame of a flex (garment_pec_flex()).
  • nipples: Whether the pecs draw nipples, which picks the smile's pixels.
  • mode: A GARMENT_FIT_* define.
  • straight: Whether the garment hangs straight over the pecs, its chest panel left as drawn and the pecs peeking out past it at the armholes.

proc
knit_sleeves
()

Ribbed knit sleeves: each sleeve's ribs laid again from its outer edge, in columns anchored to its widest row, its shadow against the body thinned to one mid pixel, and its first cuff row ribbed light. Works on the view in place, row by row.

The ramp's four brightest colours are the knit's: rib, groove, mid and dark. A garment with fewer isn't a knit.

  • limbs: Template limbs, as /datum/garment_fit names them.

proc
move_panel_edges
(/list/bands, fill, knit)

Moves a solid or knit top's chest panel edges out over the pecs, by the bands of pec_panel_bands(): each edge pixel and its own detail, up to the first pixel of the fill and 2 pixels in all, keep their order at the new edge, and the gap behind them takes the pixel after the detail, or on a knit the pixel a whole number of 2 pixel rib periods further in.

Arguments:

  • bands: From pec_panel_bands().
  • fill: The view's fill colour, from fill_colour().
  • knit: Whether the top is a ribbed knit.

proc
pec_panel_bands
(/list/template, /list/brightness)

Returns the bands a front or back view's chest panel moves out by over pecs drawn under it: y, step back into the body, new edge, old edge, distance, and FALSE for a row that isn't a cap, as apply_bands() takes them.

Each row the pec spans moves on each side where the pec's outermost pixel reaches the chest's own side; rows where it stays inside, like the collar's, never move. Over a bare pec pixel, the garment's outline moves out: from its first pixel inward, when it also holds the next two. Under the garment, like a sleeve over it, the panel's own edge moves: its armhole seam, the first pixel walking out from the centre that is no brighter than 3/4 of the fill. Either moves at most PEC_PANEL_MAX_MOVE pixels, every one of them over the pec.

Arguments:

  • template: The pecs' template for this view.
  • brightness: The view's colours -> their brightness, from ramp().

proc
pixel
(x, y)

Returns the view's pixel at x, y as the fitting has it so far, reading the frame the first time.

Arguments:

  • x, y: From the view's top left, from 0. Off the view, the pixel is clear.

proc
ramp
()

Returns the view's colours, without alpha, brightest first: the garment's own ramp. Counts them the first time: from the frame's own counts and what has been written over them, when they're known, otherwise by reading the whole view.

Brightness is 299 R + 587 G + 114 B, Rec. 601 luma in whole numbers. Colours as bright as each other keep the order they were counted in, which only a read gives, so garment_metadata.json leaves out the views that have any.

Arguments:

Arguments:

proc
shade_step
(pixel_brightness, /list/brightness)

Returns the garment's colour one shade below a pixel: of its darker colours, the one whose brightness is nearest 86% of the pixel's, the brighter of two as near. A fixed drop, so a top of many close greys still shades visibly. Null when no colour is darker.

Arguments:

  • pixel_brightness: The pixel's brightness.
  • brightness: The view's colours -> their brightness, brightest first, from ramp().

proc
sleeve_colours
()

Returns each fitted arm's sleeve's own colours, the only ones shaded sleeves step through: fitted arm -> its colours, each colour -> TRUE. Read once, after the limbs are fitted and before the sleeves are shaded, so both steps of the shading take the same colours. The garment's whole ramp would give a sleeve its skirt's, belt's or boots' colours.

A sleeve's own colours are those the view has over the arm, and the view's other colours of the same family, so a garment of one material keeps its whole ramp. A lit colour is of the family when it's a grey and a lit colour over the arm is too, or its hue is within SLEEVE_MAX_HUE_SPREAD degrees of every lit hue there, which keeps the sleeve one hue, as garment_sleeve_mode() judges plain sleeves. A dark one, an outline's or a shadow's, whose hue tells less, when it's a grey and a colour over the arm is too, or its hue is within the spread of any hue there.

proc
sleeve_steps
(/list/ramp, /list/colours, source)

Returns the colour a pixel of colour source takes at each step of the view's ramp: the step's own colour where it's the sleeve's own and kin to source (garment_colours_kin()), otherwise the next darker one that is, or null when none is. One step longer than the ramp, its last null, so the step past the darkest can be asked for.

Arguments:

  • ramp: The view's colours, brightest first, from ramp().
  • colours: The sleeve's own colours, from sleeve_colours().
  • source: The stepped pixel's colour, without alpha.
