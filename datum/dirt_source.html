dirt_source
/datum/dirt_source

A game event that lands general dirt through the gate, dirt_source_land(). It's never made. The gate reads its cause, material, level, chance, cooldown and deferred off the type, and keys its cooldown by the type. The event's own procs read the extra vars on subtypes, and pass the gate what the event works out, such as a slime's colour.

Vars

causeA DIRT_CAUSE_* define, or null where the call passes it.
chanceThe chance that it lands, in percent, where the call passes none.
cooldownHow long, in deciseconds, one roll of the chance holds off the next on a person, landed or not, or 0 for none.
deferredWhether its landings wait for a later fire of SSdirt (/datum/dirt_job/landing), for a source that can reach a crowd in one tick.
levelThe deposit's level where the call passes none. dirt_land() keeps it from 1 up to the cause's top.
materialA DIRT_MATERIAL_* define, or null where the call passes it.
