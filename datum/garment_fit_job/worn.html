worn
/datum/garment_fit_job/worn

A worn item's sprite or glow fitted (fit_worn_icon(), fit_worn_glows()): the icon build_worn_icon() built, or a state of its worn sheet itself. Only a 32x32 sprite with four directions is fitted; an animated one keeps its frame delays, read from its sheet, and isn't fitted where they can't be read, like a generated icon's.

Vars

sheetThe worn sheet the sprite is drawn from.
sheet_stateWhether the sprite is that state itself, rather than an icon build_worn_icon() built over it.
stateThe state in it.

Arguments:

  • cache, cache_key, fit: As /datum/garment_fit_job has them.
  • building_icon: The icon build_worn_icon() built, which is fitted as a copy (the bodyshape icon caches can hand theirs out), or null to fit the state itself.
  • sheet, state: The worn sheet and state the sprite is drawn from.
  • mode: A GARMENT_FIT_* define, or null for the mode its own sleeves pick (garment_sleeve_mode()).
