underclothes
/datum/garment_fit_job/underclothes

An underclothes style's sprite fitted (/datum/sprite_accessory/clothing/proc/fitted_sprite()), as make_appearance() draws it: its state fitted, then cut for a female body. A style whose legs the GAGS digitigrade mask draws is cut and masked first, and the result fitted, legs and all. A state's first frame is fitted.
