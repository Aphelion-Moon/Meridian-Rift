dirt_material
/datum/dirt_material

What dirt is made of: its tone roles' colours and the flags that change how it's drawn, as the reference generator's materials. One of each is made and shared (dirt_material()). Slime's and blood's are made per colour.

tintsEach role's list(colour, alpha from 0 to 1), indexed by DIRT_ROLE_* define, haze included: the film's colour at half its alpha. A material without a glint of its own glints in its fleck's tint. You should NEVER modify it.
