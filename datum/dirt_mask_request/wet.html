wet
/datum/dirt_mask_request/wet

What sweat's multiplier on a garment is made from. The multiplier is the image that darkens the wet cloth. It's made from the wet pixels of the masks the garment draws (/datum/dirt_masks/var/wet) and the garment's own sprite as it's drawn. Garments drawn alike with the same masks share the multiplier, kept in SSdirt.wet_icons.
