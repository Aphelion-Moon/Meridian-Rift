MeridianOS theme art
modular_aphelion/modules/meridian_ui/assets/README.md

Art a MeridianOS theme draws with that is too big for tgui's bundle, which every window loads and the lobby inlines. /datum/asset/simple/art_stylesheet (code/art_stylesheet.dm) sends a theme's files to its players' windows and lobby with a stylesheet it writes when it registers, naming each texture as a custom property on the theme (--mt-<file name without extension>) and each font as a face. Foundry's textures and its title and label faces are the forge markings room's own files (modular_aphelion/modules/markings_room/assets/); what is here is the theme's own.

textures/foundry-smoke.webpFoundry's arrival smoke: a soft-alpha plume of forge smoke lit warm from below, 128x176Original, procedural

The smoke is value-noise fBm with a domain warp, stretched upward into wisps, densest low and in the middle, fading to nothing at the edges, made with a fixed seed by the lab's foundry-theme run (smoke/make_smoke.py). Nothing third-party goes into it. A portrait's arrival scales and drifts it while it fades (_portrait-arrival.scss), so it is drawn small and soft.
