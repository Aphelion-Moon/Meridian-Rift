Recolourable bulky augments

Module ID: BULKY_AUGMENTS

Description:

Bulky augment finishes for cybernetic arms, with their hands, and legs, drawn on the fitness module's Muscular outline in colours the player picks for each augment slot in character setup. The finishes whose design has lights glow.

Not offered yet. While BULKY_FINISHES_OFFERED is FALSE, the finish pickers leave the finishes out, the server refuses one picked anyway, and the paint sheet isn't sent with the preferences window. A character saved wearing a finish still wears it.

Finishes. Each finish is a /datum/robotic_style/recolorable subtype (code/finishes.dm), so it joins GLOB.robotic_styles_list, the augments' finish picker and every path that takes a robotic style, with no special case there.

A finish is data only: its states in the sheets and a subtype. A finish's name is its save key, so renaming one drops it from every save that wears it.

A finish's robotic style vars are fixed by the abstract type: icon (the human skin sheet), supported_slots (ARMS|LEGS), has_digi and bodyshape_override = BODYSHAPE_HUMANOID. Each subtype sets limb_id_override, its id, the prefix of its states, so Nova's set_icon_greyscale() screams at once over a missing <id>_<zone> instead of drawing a blank limb. Its own data is channels (the channels its art uses, each with the label the editor shows), default_colors and presets (name to colour string). Only a finish whose design has lights has a "lights" channel.

Colours. A finish draws from up to four player colours, kept as one colour string: "#rrggbb" four times run together, lowercase, in the order paint, accent, frame, lights (sanitize_augment_colors() checks one). A finish without lights keeps the fourth colour unused.

  • Paint is the main plating, accent the trim, frame the joints and inner structure, and lights the strips and clusters.
  • Two small untinted shines are fixed: AUGMENT_SHINE_W (#fafbff) and AUGMENT_SHINE_X (#c4c7d0).
  • The skin, synthflesh and the thigh above a leg's join, is the body's own skin or mutant colour.

Each slot keeps its own colours (/datum/preferences/var/augment_limb_colors, slot to colour string), and a slot without any draws its finish's default_colors.

Sheets. Three sheets are generated from each finish's art sources; regenerate them whenever an art source changes rather than editing them by hand. Every state is 32x32 in four directions, and zone is one of BULKY_AUGMENT_ZONES: r_arm, l_arm, r_hand, l_hand, r_leg or l_leg.

The skin base covers the whole outline, so the limb's own emissive blocker covers the whole limb, and its texture and alpha behave as an organic limb's.

Applying a finish. apply_style_to_limb() hands a recolourable finish to its apply_to(), whatever augment item it is on. The limb then:

  • carries /datum/element/augment_paint, which draws its paint and keys its icons (below);
  • draws greyscale, its skin base as its own image;
  • takes the slot's colours, or the finish's default;
  • glows only when the finish has lights and the limb's own zone has a lights mask, and only while the character's allow_emissives preference is on, as all player-set glow does (a limb made without preferences, like an admin spawn, glows). The zone is checked once, here, never as the limb is drawn;
  • is MUSCLE_SIZE_MUSCULAR, so clothes and underclothes fit it as a Muscular limb. It's set at runtime, never as a type var, since init_fitness_limb_families() would take a typed robot limb for a fitness family.

A finish's skin parts take the body's skin tone or mutant colour, which a limb learns only as it is set up for its owner (update_limb()'s is_creating). The body's own limbs in the preview have it already. A limb spawned for a finish (the spawn branch of /datum/augment_item/limb/apply()) is set up so once it is on the body, and a protean's regrown limb is too.

clear_augment_finish() takes a finish off: the element and the colours go, and the glow and muscle size go back to the limb type's own. The element goes on only while a limb wears no finish yet, so it is never added twice.

The character setup preview changes the body's own limbs in place, so it also takes a finish off again:

  • Where a slot keeps an augment, apply() clears the limb first and gives it the muscle size its spawned augment will have, the item type's (Normal). A brand finish's preview so fits clothes as the spawned limb will.
  • Where a slot has no augment, the reset loop in limbs_and_markings.dm clears the limb. The body's shape and garment fit are then taken again from its limbs, which the loop changed after they were first taken.

A protean's regrown limb wears the finish its lost limb wore: the protean's limb snapshot keeps the colours, glow and body shape, and restore_finish() hands the regrown limb to the finish's apply_to(), then gives it the lost limb's glow back.

Drawing. /datum/element/augment_paint (code/limbs.dm) is on finished limbs only, so every other limb redraws as it always has. It listens to two signals tg's limb drawing already sends:

  • COMSIG_BODYPART_GET_LIMB_ICON, at the end of get_limb_icon(), after the leg split, height and textures: it adds the paint (draw_augment_paint()). After the limb's images go a copy of its own image drawing its zone's _paint state and one drawing its _glow, and the same for an arm's hand. Each is coloured by a numeric 4x4 colour matrix (augment_paint_matrix()), rows paint, accent and frame (or lights and the two shines), then the limb's alpha. The copies keep the limb's layer, height, textures and facing. A leg's are split into its two halves through the masked leg cache, as draw_muscle_definition() splits definition. A zone without a packed state draws no copy for it.
  • COMSIG_BODYPART_GENERATE_ICON_KEY: it points the limb at the skin sheet in its owner's ramp, the humanoid one under a mutant colour (update_augment_skin_sheet()), and keys the limb by its colours, its glow, its alpha and that sheet. The limb icon cache is shared by every body, so without them one body's arm would be served to another. A body keys a limb after update_limb() has learnt its owner's species_color and before drawing it; a dropped limb keeps the sheet it had when it was last keyed or set up.

The lights glow through tg's own limb emissives: a limb that is_emissive draws <state>_e from its skin sheet before the leg split, so the mask is split, offset and flagged as the limb is. tg lights an arm's hand along with the arm, so the generator gives the hand of every lit arm a mask too, empty where the hand has no lights: no finish lights a hand today.

Anything else the limb lights, like a glowing marking, would light the plates over it in the dark. So then each _paint copy also gets an emissive blocker, a copy of the limb's own. A limb with nothing else lit gets none. Only an emissive counts, told by the constant row of its colour matrix; the emissive blockers that overlays (bodypart overlays, a bulge) bring along don't. The finish's own lights, the limb's and its hand's masks, are never under a _paint pixel, so they stay lit.

The matrices are numeric 16-number lists, never rows of colour strings, which map_colors_inferred() would write into the drawn character preview's iconforge recipe. Each is built once per colours, state and alpha, and kept until 2048 are kept (AUGMENT_PAINT_MATRIX_CACHE_SIZE); then the cache starts over, so applying colours without end can't grow it. No icon is made for any colour: a zone draws its skin base, at most two copies and its lights mask.

Preferences. The colours save as augment_limb_colors in the character's save data. On load (load_augment_colors()) an entry is kept only when its slot wears a recolourable finish and its string is valid, then lowercased; anything else is dropped, never drawn. The preferences import sanitiser keeps their text values.

Changing a slot to another finish, or to none, clears its colours, so a new finish starts at its own default. Changing the augment item with the finish kept keeps them.

/datum/preference_middleware/augment_paint (code/preferences.dm, key augment_paint) gives character setup:

  • constant data, sent once in preferences.json: each finish by name, with its id, channels ({key, label}), default, presets ({name, colors}) and zones (zone to {paint, glow}, 0 or 1, read from the paint sheet), the editor's swatches, metal for paint, accent and frame, and lights, and brands: each brand style by name, with its key and the zones it has sprites for (below);
  • window data: augment_colors (slot to colour string), augment_paint_skin ({color, humanoid, feminine}: the preview body's chest colour, whether it is a mutant colour, and whether the body is drawn feminine, its limb_gender "f", so a dimorphic brand head or chest tries on as head_f or chest_f) and augment_paint_emissive (whether the lights glow);
  • the augment_paint spritesheet, sent with the window;
  • the actions set_augment_colors {slot, colors} (a colour string, or null for the finish's default) and copy_augment_colors {slot} (to every slot wearing a recolourable finish). Each needs a slot wearing a recolourable finish, redraws the preview body, and answers with augment_colors alone;
  • the action augment_brand_sprites (no params), which sends the augment_brands spritesheet (send_augment_brand_sprites(), as the species page sends its sheets).

The window's data is sent as each change is made, and may read the preview body before it is rebuilt. So the skin follows the rebuilt body too: the character preview module's answer, sent right after the rebuild, carries augment_paint_skin whenever it changed since the page last got it (add_preview_answer()).

set_bodypart_aug_style's answer carries augment_colors too, since a new finish clears a slot's.

The editor's sprites. /datum/asset/spritesheet_batched/augment_paint (code/assets.dm) cuts the sheets into 32x32 views, which the editor recolours on the client:

  • augment_paint32x32 <id>-<zone>-<layer>-<dir>, layer being skin, skinh (the humanoid ramp), paint or glow (only where the paint sheet holds the state), and dir south, north, east or west;
  • augment_paint32x32 body-<dir>, the Muscular body, the preview's silhouette.

/datum/asset/spritesheet_batched/augment_brands (code/assets.dm) holds the finish picker's try-on sprites, augment_brands32x32 brand-<key>-<zone>-<dir>: each brand style's limbs, head and chest as the game draws them. They are about 240 KB (932 sprites, against the finishes' 162 KB), and only the finish picker's try-on uses them. So they are not sent with the window, which would make every preferences open pay for them. The window asks for the sheet once, in the background, as the Augments room is about to be needed (augment_brand_sprites), and nobody who stays away from it loads it.

A brand sprite is the state an augment limb in that style draws (augment_brand_states()), resolved as apply_style_to_limb() and get_limb_icon() resolve it: <limb id>_<zone> in the style's icon, the limb id being the style's limb_id_override or robotic, which every styled augment limb has. An arm's hand comes with it. A digitigrade leg is the zone r_leg_digi or l_leg_digi, state <limb id>_<leg>_digi, only for a style that keeps the digitigrade shape (has_digi and no bodyshape_override dropping it): the others draw their plain leg on a digitigrade body. A head and a chest are the zones head and chest. Where the style's dimorphic_overrides make one dimorphic, the game ends its state in the body's limb_gender: head or chest is then the masculine body's <limb id>_<zone>_m, and head_f or chest_f every other body's <limb id>_<zone>_f. The robotic head and chest a styled augment is aren't dimorphic of themselves, so the brands without overrides have plain <limb id>_head and <limb id>_chest. Only zones a slot can wear the style on, and states with four directions, are in. key is the last part of the style's type (augment_brand_key()). A brand sprite is drawn as is; the game tints it with the body's skin when its augment item draws greyscale, which character setup's augment items carry as uses_greyscale.

A styled augment item also carries default_style (build_augment_choices()), the brand whose sprites are exactly its own plantigrade look with no style chosen, which the finish picker tries on for "None": apply()'s look, the limb type's icon, or its greyscale icon, and state in its zone, an arm's hand coming with it. The Cyborg head, chest, arms and legs take Cyborg and the Prosthetic arms and legs Surplus, greyscale variants included. A leg tries "None" on only on a plantigrade body: on a digitigrade body it becomes a digitigrade cybernetic leg (digitigrade_parts.dmi), which no brand draws, so the room shows no leg there.

Iconforge makes both sheets from the game's icons, in the background at init (SSasset_loading), and the batched spritesheets' smart cache keeps them across rounds, so each is made once per change of its icons.

The editor. In the Augments+ room, a part card whose finish is recolourable gets a palette button on its FINISH row, and the Readout shows that slot's colours as chips. The palette opens a flyout over the card's side of the stage: a scan preview of the part in four directions over the body's silhouette, recoloured on the GPU (SVG feColorMatrix) as colours change, with a lights-off view; the finish's presets; a row per channel with a picker and quick swatches; and Copy to all parts and Reset to default. Nothing reaches the server until Apply, a preset, a copy or a reset. With allow_emissives off, the lights row says the lights won't glow until it's on.

In the finish picker, a part's finishes are thumbnails (thumbs.tsx), four to a row: "None" first, then the recolourable finishes and the brand styles under headings of their own. A head and a chest, which take no recolourable finish, show "None" and the brand styles. Each is the slot's part in that finish facing south, drawn up four times in a crop that holds the slot's part in every style: a recolourable finish in its default colours, or the slot's own on the one it wears, a brand in its sprites, tinted with the skin where the item draws greyscale, and "None" in the augment's own look, or crossed out where it has none. A check marks the installed finish and a palette a recolourable one, and each tile's accessible name says both. One floating tooltip names the tile under the pointer or the keyboard, below its tile where there's no room above and moved along to keep 8 px inside the window. The search narrows the tiles, scrolling stops on whole rows, and the picker opens with the installed finish's row in full view; the arrow keys go round the grid, Home and End to its ends, and Tab leaves it. Until the brand sheet comes, a brand tile shows the body's silhouette faintly, and fills in where it stands. A tile's art is drawn once from its sprites and colours and kept: the tiles in view draw before the picker's first frame and the rest once the window is idle, and once the stage has shown and the window is idle, every slot's thumbnails are drawn ahead a few at a time, which a press or a key puts off.

The finish under the pointer or the keyboard is tried on the body scan at once, client side, as the markings room tries markings on: its sprites from the augment_paint sheet, or a brand finish's from augment_brands, drawn over the scan's facing and lined up with it, with the part it replaces masked out of the character through the preview's region map, and a far leg in profile kept behind the near one. A brand's head or chest on a body drawn feminine (augment_paint_skin.feminine) is its feminine sprite, where it has one. The visible augments' own pixels, a pair of cybernetic eyes or an implant's overlay, stay over what's tried on, as the game draws them over the part; the rest of what's drawn over a head goes with its pixels, as the preview is one picture, and a Cyborg head draws no hair. The finish the part wears is not tried on, and "None" shows the augment's own look when its item names a brand style for it (default_style), except on a digitigrade body's leg, where it shows nothing. A finish whose sprites can't be drawn yet, as a brand finish's before its sheet has come, changes nothing on the body until they can. The window asks for that sheet once the pointer or the keyboard rests 100 ms on the Augments+ tab or the room's Augments button, or once the stage has shown and the window is idle, and a window opened again asks again, in case an ask went unanswered. On a digitigrade body every slot offers only the styles with digitigrade sprites, the ones the server keeps there. Only a pick reaches the server. A press anywhere outside an open picker or editor closes it, except on the window's drag and resize handles and on the scan's rotate buttons and lights key, which sit over the stage's scrim so the character can be turned and lit with one open. What is tried on stays on through the trip to them and while they are used: tiles or rows the pointer crosses heading out for them don't take over, and it comes off once the pointer rests anywhere else. The scan has the other previews' lights key, and each card's trace lands on its part's pixels in the scan's current facing: a part hidden in that facing lands on its pair, a missing limb keeps its card's fixed port, and a taur's legs land on its taur body.

Not done yet:

  • Digitigrade art. Like every brand finish, these draw plantigrade legs on a digitigrade body (bodyshape_override = BODYSHAPE_HUMANOID with has_digi), and the body's clothes follow its legs. Digi art needs _digi states, on the Muscular digi outline (mammal_*_leg_digi in the size sheets), and each finish's bodyshape_override dropped.
  • Recolourable heads and chests: the finishes are arms and legs only, while brand styles try on everywhere.
  • Recolouring in the round: colours are set in character setup and travel with the limb.
  • The spraycan restyle, which hands image() a /datum/robotic_style (crayons.dm) and so breaks every finish.
  • Lit hands. A finish whose art lit a hand but not its arm would not glow there: the glow follows the limb's own zone.
