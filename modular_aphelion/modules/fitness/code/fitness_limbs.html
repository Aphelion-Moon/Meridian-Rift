#define

#define
FITNESS_LIMB_DRAWING
(family)

Gives a limb family's fitness subtypes (family/fitness and its sizes) the overrides they draw with. They sit on the fitness subtypes rather than on /obj/item/bodypart, so a plain limb's redraw runs none of them.

  • update_limb() keeps the limb on its size's art whatever sheet it was last given: its type's own, the humanoid one skin tones off give it, or a reset's. A body's redraw runs it on each limb before making the limb's icon cache key. Only a plain sheet that has size sheets is looked up again; a limb already on its size's sheet keeps it. Set up for an owner (is_creating), a new limb takes the owner's definition, and a chest, whose male or female art is set then, refits its owner's garments to its glutes (update_garment_fit()).
  • generate_icon_key() and generate_husk_key() key the limb by its size and definition: the size sheets share the plain sheets' state names, and the limb icon cache is shared by every body, so without it one's art would be served for another.
  • get_limb_icon() draws the definition (draw_muscle_definition()).

Arguments:

  • family: The limb type the fitness subtypes are under, like /obj/item/bodypart/arm/left.
