modular_aphelion/modules/fitness/code/female_cut.dm

/proc/female_cut_over_pecsReturns the female cut a garment gets on wearer, as wear_female_version() takes its flags: flags themselves, unless the wearer has pecs, which keep the torso out of it. Then only the lower body is shaped: FEMALE_UNIFORM_LOWER_ONLY where the cut would take in the crotch, or null, no cut at all, where it would shape only the torso. Pecs of any shape count, whether they show or not.
/proc/female_lower_cut_maskReturns the mask a FEMALE_UNIFORM_LOWER_ONLY cut is drawn through: masking_helpers.dmi's full cut, less the pixels its torso-only cut shares with it, so it shapes the lower body alone. Made with each new cut, which wear_female_version() caches.
