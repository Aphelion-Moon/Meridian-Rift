/proc/species_has_fitness_limbsReturns whether a species' own limbs have fitness subtypes, which is when its characters get a muscle size and definition.
/proc/wide_height_mapReturns a height map tiled three wide, as tg tiles its texture filters over wide images. A map is centred on what it stretches, so one 32 px wide stretched only the middle of a taur suit, wings or a big tail and left a step at each edge. Its rows are each one colour, so the tiles join exactly.
