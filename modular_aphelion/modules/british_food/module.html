British food

Module ID: BRITISH_FOOD

Description:

British dishes with their recipes, grab-and-go vendor versions, and the vendor that sells them.

  • Dishes. Hunter's chicken, steak and ale pie, pork pie, chicken and mushroom pie, Cornish pasty, beans on toast, Welsh cawl, Eton mess, scones with jam and cream, sticky toffee pudding, the deep fried Mars bar and the sausage, bean and cheese melt. The steak pie cuts into five slices. The pork and chicken pies are single servings that cut into quarters. Eton mess comes in a drinking glass: the recipe takes one and eating leaves it, as bowl dishes do with bowls. Welsh cawl is a soup from the soup pot, served in a bowl.
  • Drinks. Irn-Bru and dandelion and burdock cans, each with its own in-hand and crushed sprites. Both also stock the standard Robust Softdrinks machine.
  • Deep fried Mars bar. Each bar holds saturated fat, which takes minutes to clear from the blood. Three bars within about five minutes overdose and damage the heart, and each bar past that hurts more; one now and then never does.
  • Vendor food. /obj/item/food/british_pack items are eaten from their container. Most can also be used in hand to take the food out, which keeps the bites already taken and leaves the empty container. They are junkier and less nutritious than the home-cooked dishes.
  • Mrs. Luvvit's Pie & Mash (/obj/machinery/vending/imported/luvvits), an imported vendor. It is mapped into the Interlink, the ghost cafe, two station maps and five ruins, and its refill joins the imported vendors' cargo crate. Hot food comes out piping hot (/datum/component/piping_hot): it steams for 10 to 30 seconds, and each bite before it cools burns the eater's tongue a little and stops them eating for a few seconds. Masochists keep eating. It can't hurt mobs that resist heat or lava, can't feel pain, or have a body built for lavaland heat (lizards, golems, kobolds) or hotter.
