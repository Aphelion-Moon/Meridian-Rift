Bulge

Module ID: BULGE

Description:

A penis that is covered and at least half hard shows through what covers it: half hard a soft bulge, fully hard a tent. The size follows the penis's length: sizes 1 to 4 under 5, 8 and 11 inches and from 11 up, and for the garments that go further, sizes 5 to 8 from 37, 49, 57 and 65 inches, where the penis starts to show past the cloth. A print is derived from the sprite the game actually draws, so a recoloured or species-fitted garment carries its own print, and a tentacle penis past size 4 draws the size-4 bulge.

Each garment names a family (code/garment_families.dm): boxers, loose boxers, briefs, jockstrap, jeans, long johns, jumpsuits, khaki shorts, athletic shorts, track pants, two loincloths, and six skirt styles. A suit over the groin hides the bulge unless it was reviewed to leave the groin clear (bulge_suit_rule). A chastity device, a sheath (until fully hard), a husk, a taur or Cerulean body, "Never show" and "Above all clothing" draw none.

A garment covers the penis only when it is drawn in front of it. With Custom "Above underwear" the penis is drawn over the underwear and undershirt as Nova draws it: no bulge on them and nothing cut, until the player layers it back ("Below underwear" or "Hidden by clothes"). A uniform or a suit over it still carries the bulge.

Underwear always takes precedence over what is worn over it: no penis hangs out of a skirt over underwear unless the underwear lets it out too. The bulge is still drawn on the outermost cover, in its cloth, colour, layer and offsets, but underwear worn and drawn under that cover, in front of the penis as it is layered, holds the penis in (bulge_restraint(), bulge_held_size() in code/look.dm):

  • The cover draws its own shape at the underwear family's cap for the arousal, where the bulge is bigger: a skirt over briefs or a jockstrap still tents, and a jockstrap under the leotard keeps the leotard's bulge at size 4.
  • None of the penis is drawn: no exposed tip or head, no clearance or drape, and the cover is never cut. A hard loincloth over underwear that holds the penis in is not lifted, and draws no bulge.
  • Underwear whose family is none (the chastity belt and cage sprites, groin plating) holds it in completely: no bulge on any garment over it.
  • Underwear whose own look would show some of the penis at that size and arousal lets it out, and the cover's look is as it would be without it: past size 4, where its shape leaves anatomy bare (briefs from size 5, long johns' tip hard from size 5), and a loincloth when hard (its drape).

Bulge interactions ask has_bulge() (Nova's lewd_helpers/human.dm), which is true exactly while a bulge look is drawn: not under a suit that hides it, on a garment that draws none, on underwear the body does not draw, over a chastity sprite worn as underwear, or with the penis layered above its only cover. Whatever starts or ends a look (arousal, clothing, a device, layering, organs) ends in the body update that resolves it, so the answer is current from that update on. bulge_cover_name() names the garment the look is drawn on.

bulge_pokes_out() (code/look.dm) answers what of the penis a drawn look still shows, for the interactions that ask for it (self_pokes_out, target_pokes_out) and anything else: BULGE_POKES_PAST_CLOTH while some of it is drawn over the cloth (bulge_anatomy_mask: the tip or head past the garment, what a printed patch leaves bare, the shaft over a revealing skirt), BULGE_POKES_UNDER_COVER while it is drawn behind a cover cut or lifted for it (bulge_anatomy_behind on a cover drawn cut: a revealing skirt or a loincloth, hard), NONE otherwise. Any pixel the look draws counts, in any facing. It reads a few of the look's vars, so it follows the layering and suits as the renderer does. Two looks it does not answer exactly, as the look does not record what of them shows: a penis drawn behind a cover not cut for it (a skirt half hard, a long curtain skirt) shows wherever it reaches past the cover, below the hem or past the curtain, and is not counted; and the smallest Tentacled (Alt) penis under a hard loincloth counts as under the cover, though its drape hides all of it. Answering those exactly would take a test of each such look's pixels.

Layered above the underwear, the penis is exposed instead: Nova's covered_by_clothing() (lewd_organs/_genital.dm) does not count the undershirt, underwear or bra over a genital set to Custom "Above underwear", which is drawn over them, so is_exposed() offers it to the plain interactions (and to portals, examine and a breast bounce) while only underclothes are worn. A uniform or a suit over it still covers it. With only underclothes on, such a penis has no bulge interaction and the plain penis interactions instead; every other layering is as before.

How it draws

One renderer, on the penis's own bodypart overlay: a body update asks the overlay whether it draws, and a covered penis resolves its look, keeps it, and resolves it again only when something it was resolved from has changed: the penis, the slots as drawn, the cover and how it is drawn, and the underwear under the cover, so underwear swapped under an unchanged skirt or leotard redraws its look and its cut. The look's key stands in for the penis's own render key, so the chest's limb icon cache holds a bulge like any limb image. A look drawing mess (on its anatomy, or a cover's over its cloth) carries DIRT_PRIVATE_KEY, so the wearer keeps those images.

Underwear under the cover is read before anything is drawn (above): it can cap the size every shape below is drawn at, and take away the anatomy and cut they would draw. bulge_wanted_cut(), which a cover's own render asks, decides by the same bulge_held_size(), so a skirt over underwear that holds the penis in is drawn whole.

  • Plain garments: the shape's kinds of cloth (cloth, light, highlight, shade, deep shade) as white masks, tinted on the client with the adaptive palette of the garment's cloth colour. The masks are derived once per shape; the colour is sampled once per sprite and tint, its palette computed once per colour. A garment drawn through a colour matrix or filter carries no print: its cloth is sampled from the untreated sprite and drawn through the same filter.
  • Printed garments (those marked bulge_printed and every reviewed skirt): one patch derived on the server from the sprite the garment is drawn with, by the approved print rule, so it follows recolours, species fits and digitigrade sprites. Printed loose boxers, briefs, jeans and track pants keep it past size 4, to size 8 (print_cap); the large heart boxers draw their precoloured patches instead.
  • Revealing skirts and loincloths, when hard: the cover is drawn with its lifted flap cut away (the uniform and underwear renders call bulge_cut_worn()), and the penis shows through it: under a loincloth's drape, or behind the skirt and over its cloth inside the skirt's clearance.
  • Sizes 5 to 8: the penis shows over the cloth inside the shape's exposed mask. Facing east and west, every penis shape but the human one takes amendments to the derived shape: no curtain under the tip, the head covered from the waistband down where it pokes out above it (but for its two outermost pixels on the waistband), a one-row dip of the shaft below the hem covered, and gaps of one or two pixels in the cloth filled. The large heart boxers draw their patch in place of the boxers' shape, over the same bare part of the penis: the patch drawn for that penis shape's profile where the sheet has one (and for its untoned sprites, where they differ), else the human penis's. The tentacles, and Nondescript (Alt), Tapered (Alt) and Hemi (Alt) past their print_cap (4), draw the family's small_cap shape (and its print) instead, in every family.
  • Templates: the shapes up to a family's small_cap, the skirts' clearances and flaps, and the large heart boxers' patches are read from the template sheets. Past small_cap the shapes, the anatomy each leaves bare, and a hard loincloth's drape and cut have no sheet: they are derived on the server from the game's own penis, testicle and loincloth sprites by the approved rule (code/templates.dm), each once, when a look first asks for it, and kept for the round. Nothing is derived at init.
  • Blood and dirt on the cover draw over the cloth too, kept to its pixels. On a cover drawn cut they are cut with it, and the cloth carries them uncut.

Adding a garment

Give its type a block in code/garment_families.dm naming its family, and only what it needs beyond that: bulge_printed = TRUE when its own print should carry onto the bulge, bulge_cloth_layer when its cloth is not its first GAGS layer, bulge_suit_rule for a suit that leaves the groin clear. Subtypes inherit their parent's bulge, so a new subtype of a listed garment either keeps it or gets a block of its own. A revealing skirt's hem class is chosen by eye (icons/README.md). Then update the reviewed manifest (below).

Mind the type order: garment_families.dm lives in modular_aphelion, which compiles before modular_nova, so its blocks declare Nova's garment types before Nova does and move them earlier in BYOND's type order (typesof() lists them earlier, and create_and_destroy creates them earlier). The four map preview sheets that ExportMapPreviews() writes in that order (icons/map_icons/clothing/suit/_suit.dmi, under/_under.dmi, under/costume.dmi and under/dress.dmi) are committed as this tree generates them: their pixels equal upstream's, only the order of their states differs. A new block for a garment with a map preview can reorder them again; a unit test run then reports "Generated map icons were different than what is currently saved" and rewrites them: commit the rewritten sheets. Upstream changes to these sheets will conflict with ours; take upstream's change, then regenerate.

Adding or editing a shape

Up to a family's small_cap, draw it in its sheet under the name the accessors compute (icons/README.md), in the five kind greys, and keep the sheet an 8-bit palette PNG: Dream Maker re-saves sheets as 1-bit or 4-bit palette PNGs, which rust-g cannot read. A garment look that no family's shapes give is a new family subtype in code/families.dm.

Past small_cap nothing is drawn: a family's shapes there follow from its vars (top_row, hem, contained, curtain, long, large_digi_forms) and the penis sprites, with the amendments for every penis shape but the human one (bulge_derive_large()), and its loincloth drapes and cuts from its loincloth vars and its own sprite. A change to those vars changes bulges on screen: bulge_derivation then fails against its frozen record. Once the change is approved, record the new value the test prints in BULGE_DERIVATION_RECORD (code/modules/unit_tests/~nova/bulge_templates.dm).

Adding a penis shape

A new penis shape (a /datum/sprite_accessory/genital/penis subtype) needs no bulge art. Past a family's small_cap its bulges, the anatomy they leave bare and the loincloths' drapes are derived from its own sprites, so it needs:

  • its sprites in its sprite accessory's icon: m_penis_<icon_state>_<size>_<0|1>_FRONT_UNDER, with _s before _FRONT for the skin-toned ones where it has skin tones (has_skintone_shading), and _primary at the end for a shape coloured by a matrix (USE_MATRIXED_COLORS), drawn inside the window a derivation reads of them (BULGE_SPRITE_* in code/templates.dm; bulge_derivation/pin fails a sprite that draws outside it);
  • its caps: max_sprite_size_affix, and skintone_max_sprite_size_affix where its skin-toned sprites stop sooner;
  • in code/templates.dm, bulge_untoned = TRUE when its untoned sprites draw differently, or bulge_hyper = FALSE, as the tentacles have, when it should draw the small_cap shape past small_cap and show no anatomy; and print_cap below 8 when past it every family should draw it so too (its small_cap shape, and its print where printed).

Under the large heart boxers it draws the human penis's patch, unless printed_patches.dmi has patches named for its icon state (and _untoned ones where its untoned sprites draw differently), hand-drawn for its profile (icons/README.md).

The merge that adds it fails bulge_derivation/pin, bulge_derivation and bulge_template_identities, whose frozen records count every penis shape. Review its bulges; once they are approved, record the new values the tests print in BULGE_DERIVATION_INPUTS, BULGE_DERIVATION_RECORD and BULGE_IDENTITIES_RECORD in code/modules/unit_tests/~nova/bulge_templates.dm.

When the compiler fails in a file you did not touch

DM 516.1687 miscompiles depending on how many distinct variable names the whole tree declares (vars, proc locals and arguments), var/static locals included (the compiler makes each one a global variable). One name more can make an untouched file fail ("undefined var", "invalid variable"), warn falsely (an "unused_var" warning on a variable that is used), or compile code that fails at run time: here one added name, and later one added var/static local, made _species.dm warn that basic_holder was unused and fail at initialisation on spoken_languages. The effect does not grow with the count; a count that builds cleanly can stop one name later and build again two names later.

So: reuse a name the tree already declares for any new variable (a local named like others elsewhere costs nothing), add no var/static local you can do without, and read a build's whole warning list, not its count. When a build breaks or warns somewhere you did not touch, look at the names your change added before suspecting the code.

Checks

The unit tests:

  • bulge_manifest pins every family's data, every sheet's MD5, and for every garment type with a bulge value what it inherits (family, print, suit rule, cloth layer) and what the renderer reads from its own definition (worn sprite and state, digitigrade sprite, GAGS configs and colours, groin coverage, worn layer, the sprites and GAGS configs it declares for a species' body; a suit's toggled state, offsets, dimensions, alpha and colour; an underclothes accessory's sprite, static colour, layer, GAGS colours, digitigrade state and groin coverage), against code/modules/unit_tests/~nova/bulge_manifest.txt. Reviewing a change: below.
  • bulge_template_identities resolves every identity the families declare through the accessors and checks every sheet state it reaches: four directions, one frame, only kind greys or opaque white, nothing facing north. Past small_cap every identity must derive, and the identities resolved, by kind, must be its frozen record (BULGE_IDENTITIES_RECORD).
  • bulge_derivation derives every shape past small_cap, the anatomy each leaves bare, and every loincloth drape and cut, and holds them to a frozen record (BULGE_DERIVATION_RECORD: the states by kind, the shapes that cover the whole penis, and a digest of every pixel, the mask tiles and the cuts' keep masks included), taken from the sheet states they replaced, which they matched pixel for pixel. bulge_derivation/pin pins what they are derived from: the penis and testicle sprites, the loincloths' own sprites and the penis shapes' data. Either failing means bulges past small_cap look different on screen.
  • bulge_renderer/* and portal_test/bulge test the renderer's behaviour.

Reviewing a manifest change

bulge_manifest fails when a family's data, a sheet or a listed garment's own definition changes, and writes what it found to data/bulge_manifest.txt. Before copying that over the committed file:

  1. Diff the two. On a garment line, a new worn file or state is a re-sprite, groin=0 means the garment no longer covers the groin (no bulge), a new gags= or colours= changes the colour the cloth is sampled from.
  2. Look at it in game: dress a character in the garment, soft, half hard and hard, and compare its bulge with the old sprite's. For a family or sheet line, check the looks it changes.
  3. Then copy the file over and commit it with the change, saying what was reviewed.
