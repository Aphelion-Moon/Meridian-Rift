Bulge template sheets
modular_aphelion/modules/bulge/icons/README.md

templates/ holds the shapes a bulge is drawn from up to each family's small_cap, the skirts' clearances and flaps, and the large heart boxers: 5 sheets, 656 states, each with four directions (north is empty) and one frame. Open them in Dream Maker's icon editor. A state is named for what it is, and the code finds it by that name; nothing maps one name to another.

Past small_cap nothing is read from a sheet. The shapes, the anatomy each leaves bare, and a hard loincloth's drape and cut are derived at run time from the game's own penis, testicle and loincloth sprites by the approved rule (code/templates.dm), under the names below, once each and kept for the round. They were drawn in sheets once (1,576 states), and the unit test bulge_derivation holds what is derived to a frozen record of those states.

What the sheets hold

sheetstatesholds
cloth_small162sizes 1 to 4 of every garment family, and jeans hard at size 5
skirt_shapes186every skirt style, and each revealing skirt's own hem where it differs
skirt_clearance42where a skirt's shaft shows above the cloth; an empty state draws the anatomy behind the skirt only
skirt_flaps54the flap a revealing skirt lifts away when hard
printed_patches214the large heart boxers, sizes 5 to 8 (198 for the profiles of the other penis shapes), made by the print rule

A shape is a kind map: each kind of pushed-out cloth in its own grey, the approved shading over mid-grey cloth, so a sheet shows the bulges as they look on grey cloth. Cloth #808080, light #a3a3a3, highlight #b7b7b7, shade #646464, deep shade #4b4b4b. Draw with exactly these five; nothing else is read. Masks are opaque white.

Names

  • Shapes up to a family's small_cap: <shapes>_<half|hard>_<size>_<plain|digi>, for example briefs_hard_3_digi.
  • Past it, derived: <shapes>_<half|hard>_<size>_<plain|digi>_<penis shape>, with _untoned for a penis shape whose untoned sprites have shapes of their own (Human (Alt), Hemi), for example boxers_hard_6_plain_knotted_alt. Digitigrade versions exist only where the family says so (large_digi_forms); a penis whose sprite stops growing keeps size 6's shape at 7 and 8. The anatomy a shape leaves bare is derived with it, under the same name; a shape that covers the whole penis leaves none.
  • A skirt's clearance: its shape's name without the legs, <shapes>_<half|hard>_<size>, as a clearance is the same on plain and digitigrade legs. Its flap has its hard shape's name.
  • Drapes, derived: <drapes>_<penis shape>_<sprite size>, with _untoned, and _flat from the family's flat_tip_from. Cuts, derived: <drapes>_cut_<hem>, the hem being the lowest row the drape reaches facing south (the family's lifted_hem for a drape that stays above the hem rows).
  • The large heart boxers' patches: <printed_patches>_<half|hard>_<size>_<plain|digi> for the human penis, drawn by every penis shape without a patch of its own; a shape's own patch is named the same with the penis shape's icon state appended, for example heart_boxers_hard_7_plain_knotted, and _untoned after that for a shape drawn without skin tones whose untoned sprites draw differently (bulge_untoned), where its untoned profile needs a patch of its own: Human (Alt) half and hard 6 to 8, for example heart_boxers_hard_7_plain_human_alt_untoned; untoned at 5 it draws its skin-toned patch. Every other penis shape has its own (198 states) where its profile facing east and west needs one: drawn there along the anatomy the shape leaves bare, north and south copied from the human patch. Where the penis reaches below the hem (the shaft's underside, hard 6 to 8, in the knotted, barbknot, flared, hemiknot and tapered profiles), the cloth covers it too, as the boxers' leg hangs over it, and at hard 5 the cloth ends at the shaft's lower outline, with no curtain below it; knotted hard 6 has its own patch for that row. The tentacles, and Nondescript (Alt), Tapered (Alt) and Hemi (Alt), whose print_cap is 4 (code/templates.dm), have none: in every family they draw the family's small_cap shape past it, and under the heart boxers its print, at every size. Each frame is 32x10, the tile's whole width from row 20 to row 29 counted from the top (the patches reach row 28).

<shapes> and <drapes> are the family's vars in code/families.dm. A family that draws another family's shapes by design names it there (half_shapes_of, hard_shapes_of, large_shapes_of); a garment whose approved look differs from its family's has a subtype of the family, as the revealing skirts' hems do, whose own_shape_sizes, own_clearance_sizes and own_flap_sizes say at which hard sizes it has its own. Where two families' shapes merely happen to look the same, both are stored.

Each sheet is cropped to one window; the pixel offsets that place it on the 32x32 tile are defined in code/templates.dm next to the accessors. Keep a sheet's frame size, or change its offsets with it.

Revealing skirts

The common revealing skirt, /datum/bulge_family/skirt/short_reveal (92 skirts), is hemmed at row 25 of the 32x32 tile facing south, counting rows from 0 at the top. hem_24 (40 skirts) and hem_23 (14) are hemmed one and two rows higher; the miniskirts, the lone skirts and eleven single garments have subtypes of their own, because their hems differ at some sizes (code/families.dm says which). Which class fits cannot be read off a sprite by any simple rule, so place a new revealing skirt by eye:

  1. Look at the common class on the garment in game at each hard size, on plain and digitigrade legs: the garment with its flap cut away and its print carried onto the bulge, and the shaft over the cloth inside the clearance.
  2. Where the flap cuts above or below the garment's lifted hem, try short_reveal_hem_24_* and short_reveal_hem_23_*, then the single garments' classes.
  3. Give the garment a block in code/garment_families.dm: bulge_family the class that fits, and bulge_printed = TRUE, as every reviewed skirt is printed from its own sprite.
  4. If no class fits, the skirt needs a family subtype with its own shapes, clearance and flaps where they differ, which is new art to review.

Saving sheets

A changed sheet also changes its MD5 in the reviewed manifest the bulge_manifest unit test checks: once the change is reviewed, copy the data/bulge_manifest.txt the failing test writes over code/modules/unit_tests/~nova/bulge_manifest.txt.

Keep every sheet an 8-bit palette PNG. Dream Maker re-saves a sheet of few colours as a 1-bit or 4-bit palette PNG, which rust-g's DMI reader, behind tg's icon_exists(), cannot read: re-encode such a sheet at 8 bits with the same pixels and DMI metadata. A re-encoded sheet has a new MD5 for the manifest, as above. Do not run ZopfliPNG or other PNG optimisers over the sheets: rust-g's DMI reader failed on their output too (PNGs of fewer than eight bits per sample, Zopfli streams).
