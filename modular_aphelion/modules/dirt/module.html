Dirt overlays

Module ID: DIRT

Description:

Dirt that lands on a person shows on their sprite until it's washed off. Mess lands where a shot was aimed. General dirt, a cause's dirt in a material (mud from walking, soot from smoke), lands on everything that shows where its cause reaches.

  • Splats. /datum/component/dirtiness holds a person's or an item's splats (/datum/dirt_splat): a zone's mess at level 1 to 4 in one of DIRT_VARIANTS variants, or a general deposit. A person carries DIRT_MAX_SPLATS at most, across their skin, underclothes, parts, hair and what they wear, and the one landed longest ago goes first.
  • Aiming. A shot offers a region for each zone that shows on its target (dirt_mess_regions()), and dirt_mess_land() puts it on them. A shot's amount adds levels to the zone's newest splat, and what's left past the top starts another.
  • Masks. A surface's splats merge into one mask a direction, a tone role to each pixel (dirt_merge()). The masks are baked at run time into four-direction icons (dirt_baked_icon()), one for each limb, layer, garment or part they draw on. Each pixel takes its role's colour and alpha for its material.
  • Materials (materials.dm): mud, soot, oil, slime, sweat and the rest, each with its role colours, an edge and its flags. dirt_material() returns the shared one for an id, and slime and blood are made from any colour.
  • The generator. dirt_rasterise() draws a splat on a body's template: mess as streaks, globs and drips, and each general cause as its features (features.dm, coverage.dm, causes_ground.dm, causes_upper.dm). The face and hands are too small to wrap dirt round, so they take stamps (stamps.dm).
  • Templates (surface.dm). Dirt lies on limbs, not on the screen. A body's template maps each pixel to a limb, an angle round it and a height along it, built from the limb sprites the body draws, so every species gets its own.
  • SSdirt makes templates and masks in the background, a piece at a time, and every cache it keeps is bounded. A body draws its old masks until its new ones are made, and is drawn again once they are. SSdirt.finish_all() makes everything queued at once.
  • Where general dirt lands (dirt_land()). A cause reaches a fixed set of limbs (dirt_cause_reach()), and every carrier that shows on one of them takes the deposit there (dirt_shown_carriers()). The same cause and material raise a deposit rather than add one, and a bare body takes the painted designs (painted.dm).
  • What lands it (sources.dm). Slime, tool work on machines, dirt underfoot, blasts, fire, smoke and mining land general dirt in a round. So do falls, lying down and rolling on dirt, reagents and sweat, each through dirt_source_land(). Undefining DIRT_SOURCES turns them all off.
  • Where a shot lands (dirt_mess_surface()): the outermost garment over the zone, else the piece of underclothes that shows the most of it, else the skin.
  • Skin (skin.dm, limb_cache.dm). Each limb the skin's dirt lies on draws it in one container image, and an arm draws one more for its hand. A dirty limb's render key carries DIRT_PRIVATE_KEY, so its images stay on its own mob, out of tg's shared limb cache. Skin dirt fades out over DIRT_FADE_RINGS pixels before what's worn over it.
  • Clothes (worn.dm). A garment keeps its dirt when it comes off, and draws it inset in its worn wrapper while it's worn. On a garment, whichever of blood and dirt landed last draws on top (stain_over()).
  • Sweat (sweat.dm) darkens the cloth it lands on through a multiplier, and never lands on bare skin.
  • Underclothes aren't items, so the person holds their dirt, kept with the piece it's on.
  • Parts (parts.dm, genital_overlays.dm). Breasts, pecs, the penis, the testicles, the vulva and the butt take a share of a shot at their zone, and draw it with their own images.
  • Taurs (taur.dm, taur_coat.dm, taur_overlay.dm). A back shot lands on the rider and as pools along the taur body, which takes general dirt of its own. A taur suit takes the pools itself and draws its hem band.
  • Blood below a taur's waist (blood.dm). A uniform's or suit's stain, cut at the waist, runs down onto the taur body or a taur-fitted garment's cloth, drawn from the garments' own blood at the rider's height.
  • Drawn parts (appendages.dm, canvas.dm). Tails, wings, ears, horns and the other mutant parts carry their own general dirt, drawn by the canvas generator. A typecache of the parts that take dirt (dirt_shape_kinds()) lets every other part leave at the first check. A wagging tail draws its dirt on every frame of its wag.
  • Hair (hair.dm) carries its own general dirt under DIRT_CARRIER_HAIR while it shows, its pieces drawn over hats too.
  • Emissive blockers. Each baked image but sweat's multiplier draws a blocker in its shape and alpha (dirt_emissive_blocker()), so in the dark dirt dims a glow under it. A garment's dirt draws them only while blood or a glow of the garment's own lies under them (dirt_glow_under()).
  • The overlay budget. However many splats a body holds, its skin and underclothes dirt takes four of its overlays, six with a dirty hand, and each sweat multiplier one more. Their images share one holder a layer, and their blockers one group (prepare_bodypart_overlays(), in worn_emissives).
  • Washing clears it through the clean signal: showers, washing machines, soap. Clear Dirt (Debug, and every mob's VV menu, R_ADMIN) takes it all off a human.
  • Pixel parity. The masks match the reference generator's pixel for pixel, case by case of dirt_corpus.json against dirt_manifest.txt, so a change meant to move pixels starts in the reference generator.

Known limits:

  • The hair's dirt follows the stock hairstyle, not hair paint, and a drawn part's dirt ignores per-facing offsets.
  • Big Legs taurs, parts on no canvas and tail spines take no general dirt.
  • On bare skin tg's blood draws over skin dirt, whatever landed last.
  • A blocker dims only what glows under its layer, so skin dirt leaves the eyes, the genitals and the parts drawn in front glowing.
  • Blood below a taur's waist neither glows nor blocks.
