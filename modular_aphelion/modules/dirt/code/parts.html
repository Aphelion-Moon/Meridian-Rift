/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types — Define Details

modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/parts.dm

DIRT_PART_FIELDSHow many parts' fields dirt_part_field() keeps, packed.
/datum/dirt_part_sourceA part's sprite as mess reads it: the images a genital draws in the layers the reference generator draws that part from (dirt_part_layers()), their colour layers' states taken together, or the plain pecs' shape a human chest without breasts takes its mess in (dirt_pecs_guide()). A mess aimed at the part's zone takes its share on these pixels (dirt_part_own()). Made once and never changed.
/proc/dirt_parts_keyReturns parts' keys as one line of text, to key masks by: each with where it draws against a surface in each direction, when one is given (placed_key()).
/proc/dirt_body_parts_keyReturns a body's parts as one line of text, with what they're drawn for and at what layers. It tells when a change to them may shape its mess otherwise.
/proc/dirt_part_layersReturns the layers mess reads a kind of genital from, as the reference generator draws it (its own parts take every one): the butt's front and back (ADJ) images, every other kind's front image alone. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_part_kindReturns what a genital is drawn as for mess: a DIRT_PART_* define, or null for one that takes no share (the anus, the womb). The breasts organ is pecs when it's built from a pecs accessory.
/proc/dirt_part_zoneReturns the zone (DIRT_ZONE_) whose shots a kind of part (DIRT_PART_) takes a share of.
/proc/dirt_part_roundedReturns whether a kind of part is rounded. Then its mess follows its form in lobes (dirt_lobe_rasterise()), and the body's own mess leaves its pixels out. The penis isn't, and takes a bead behind its tip instead.
/proc/dirt_part_body_levelReturns the level of a splat's mess on the body where parts take a share of it: down by 2 where a penis, testicles or vulva does, else by 1 where any part does, never under 1. A part takes a share in a direction where it has pixels. The top level stays, so the body keeps its glaze there, still off the rounded parts' pixels, and the parts draw theirs at DIRT_PART_MAX_LEVEL.
/proc/dirt_part_ownReturns the pixels a part takes its share on in one direction, as indexes in row-major order: its own. The pecs guide keeps to where the chest shows. The vulva is too small for a lobe of its own. So on skin it also takes the ring of visible pixels round its own (the body's, and the parts'). It never does on cloth.
/proc/dirt_part_fieldReturns one splat's mess on a part's pixels, which keeps every piece where it merges with others: lobes on a rounded part, a bead behind the tip on the penis. Its fields have levels 1 to DIRT_PART_MAX_LEVEL, so a splat past it draws at it. A field rests on its arguments alone, and every mask its splat is on draws it again, so the newest DIRT_PART_FIELDS are kept, packed.
/proc/dirt_part_rasteriseReturns mess on a part too small to round, the penis. A bead sits just behind its tip, with a glint and a grey underside. The tip itself is translucent, as a bead over the whole end reads as a white cap. At levels 2 and 3 a run goes back from the tip, with a drop or two along the part. A translucent fringe edges the mess inside the part. It's drawn from the "part:aimed_:" stream, on its pixels only.
/proc/dirt_part_fringeLays a part's translucent fringe: film on each of the part's pixels beside its solid mess, up, down or across, that holds none yet.
/proc/dirt_part_indexReturns a pixel's index on a 32x32 canvas, or null off it.
/proc/dirt_lobesReturns a rounded part's lobes, each a list of indexes in row-major order: each side of DIRT_PART_MIDLINE where the view shows both (front or back, the vulva excepted), else the whole part. A lobe under 4 px holds none.
/proc/dirt_lobe_rasteriseReturns mess following a rounded part's form (breasts, pecs, the butt, testicles, the vulva), from the "lobe:aimed_::" stream. A ragged blob lands on a lobe's upper slope. At levels 2 and 3 its drip runs down the lobe to a bead at the under-curve. The drip keeps its place across each row as the lobe widens and narrows. Level 3 adds a smaller blob with a short drip, and breaks the drips into beads. A pair's other lobe takes a smaller blob, clear of the first lobe's rows, as level with it the pair reads as a bra. Each drop is opaque, with a glint on top and a grey underside. A translucent fringe edges the mess, all on the part's own pixels.
/proc/dirt_lobe_shapeReturns a lobe's shape: list(its indexes, TRUE on each of its pixels, alist(row = list(leftmost column, rightmost column)), its rows from the top).
/proc/dirt_lobe_row_atReturns the row a fraction of the way down a lobe, 0 its top and 1 its bottom, not rounded.
/datum/dirt_lobe_rasterOne rounded part's mess while dirt_lobe_rasterise() lays it: each pixel's role so far and the glints.
/proc/dirt_pecs_guideReturns the source of the plain pecs' shape a human chest without breasts takes mess in, or null for any other chest. It's an undrawn guide (Muscular pecs and breasts are the breasts organ, drawn). You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_part_drawnReturns the source a genital is drawn as now, its organ slot filled in and each state's layer read from its image. It returns null while the genital draws none: hidden (dirt_shown()), a device drawn in its place, a bulge look drawn for it, no sprite in the layers mess reads it from (a taur's penis draws only behind the body), or a kind that takes no share.
/proc/dirt_body_partsReturns the parts a body draws that take mess, lowest drawn first: the pecs guide if its chest takes one, then each genital drawn (dirt_part_drawn()). A gloryhole's occupant draws their penis only through its relay, as can_draw_on_bodypart() decides (portal_relays_penis()). So the penis doesn't shape the body's mess.
/proc/cmp_dirt_parts_drawnSorts parts lowest drawn first: the higher layer number draws under.
/proc/dirt_surface_partsReturns the parts a surface's splats reach, lowest drawn first: those on a zone one of the splats is on. Where each draws against the surface is worked out in each direction (/datum/dirt_part_source/proc/under()).
/proc/dirt_split_partsReturns parts split by where they draw against a surface in one direction: list(those under it, whose share is drawn in its masks, those over it, whose share is drawn on themselves).
/proc/dirt_parts_overReturns the parts drawn over a surface in some direction, lowest drawn first, that share a limb with its general dirt (dirt_part_limbs()). Each takes it on its own pixels in those directions, as the reference generator composites the body's dirt over them (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/deposit_part_runs()). The pecs guide, never drawn, takes none.
/proc/dirt_part_source_ofReturns a genital's images as a part source, every layer's colour layers' states together with the layer each draws at. It returns null when none draws a sprite. Emissives and texture holders are left out.
/proc/dirt_part_limbsReturns the limbs a kind of part's pixels can lie on in a body's template, DIRT_LIMB_BIT() of each, as its sprites reach. Pecs lie on the torso and beside the arms. Breasts, the butt and the genitals lie on the legs too, from DIRT_BELT down (the largest breasts reach row 29, and a penis row 31).
/proc/dirt_container_overReturns a container of baked dirt images, kept together, just over a genital's or a taur body's images. It takes the layer and offsets of the last sprite among them, the one drawn over the others. It isn't nested in that sprite, as the sprite is coloured, which its overlays would take, and a filter on it doesn't reach them.
