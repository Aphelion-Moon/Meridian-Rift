modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/sweat.dm

DIRT_WET_CAPThe most of its own colour that wet cloth keeps, so even white cloth darkens when it's wet.
DIRT_WET_FLATTENHow far a soaked pixel moves toward the darkened colour around it. Wet cloth clings, and its folds flatten.
/datum/dirt_mask_request/wetWhat sweat's multiplier on a garment is made from. The multiplier is the image that darkens the wet cloth. It's made from the wet pixels of the masks the garment draws (/datum/dirt_masks/var/wet) and the garment's own sprite as it's drawn. Garments drawn alike with the same masks share the multiplier, kept in SSdirt.wet_icons.
/proc/dirt_wet_lookReturns a garment's look as text, which sweat's multiplier is keyed by. It holds the sprite, colour and greyscale colours, where the sprite lies from the body's canvas, and the sprites drawn over it that stay dry.
/proc/dirt_accessory_spritesReturns the accessories of a wearer's uniform drawn over a garment's sprite, which sweat on the garment leaves dry. They're on the uniform's own sprite, or on a suit or a tie that draws them over itself. The result is list(list(icon, icon state, pixels right, pixels up), ...) as each is drawn on the sprite, or null for none.
/proc/dirt_wet_keyReturns the key sweat's multiplier is kept under in SSdirt.wet_icons: an MD5 of the garment's look and its masks'.
/proc/dirt_holds_wetReturns whether some splats' materials hold a wet one (sweat), whose pixels make a garment's multiplier.
/proc/dirt_wet_drawnReturns the wet pixels of a cloth surface's merged masks, for sweat's multiplier, or null when none is wet. The result is alist(material key = a list of one text for each direction, in dirt_mask_directions() order). Each text is /datum/dirt_pixels/proc/drawn() of that direction's wet pixels. A wet material's pixels take no colour in the bake (dirt_baked_icon()).
/proc/dirt_wet_alphasReturns how wet each wet pixel is in one direction: alist(index = its role's alpha in its material's tints), read from each material's packed text run by run.
/proc/dirt_garment_pixelsReturns the pixels of a garment's sprite within two pixels of the wet ones, which are all the multiplier's 5x5 means read. The result is alist(index = list(red, green, blue)) of those with any opacity. The garment's colour is multiplied in as the reference generator multiplies it, each channel rounded down. Indexes are on the body's 32x32 canvas, and the sprite is read where it lies from that canvas.
/proc/dirt_wet_factorsReturns sweat's multiplier in one direction, as the reference generator makes it for a garment from its own sprite. The result is alist(index = list(red, green, blue)), each factor x 255 with halves rounded up. It holds each wet pixel on the garment's pixels that has a wet one beside it (lone ones read as dark dots). Each factor is 1 - a + a * m, where a is how wet the pixel is and m is the cloth's local colour. That colour is a 5x5 mean over the garment's pixels, at most DIRT_WET_CAP. The factor is then pulled toward the darkened mean by DIRT_WET_FLATTEN at full wet, so the cloth's own shading flattens rather than deepens. It's never over 1, as a multiply can't lighten.
/proc/dirt_wet_iconReturns sweat's multiplier baked into one four-direction RGB icon on a blank state, registered in the resource cache as dirt_baked_icon() registers its icons. Each wet pixel takes its factors as an opaque colour, and every other pixel is clear. Returns null when it has no pixels.
/proc/dirt_wet_imageReturns an image of sweat's multiplier, BLEND_MULTIPLY. Inside a garment's KEEP_TOGETHER worn wrapper it darkens the garment's own pixels in their own colour. Drawn on its own over the underclothes, it darkens those it lies on.
