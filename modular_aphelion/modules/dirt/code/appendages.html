modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/appendages.dm

DIRT_TAIL_ROOT_UPHow far over the floor a tail joins the body, in pixels: the hips. dirt_tail_coordinates() measures along the tail from there.
DIRT_TAUR_RUMP_BACKHow far behind the tile's middle a taur's tail joins its body in profile, and how far over the floor: its rump.
/datum/dirt_masksA surface's masks: its dirt baked into four-direction RGBA icons (dirt_baked_icon()) and registered in the resource cache. Each icon is drawn as it is, because its colours and alpha are in its pixels.
/proc/dirt_shape_kindsReturns the drawn parts that take dirt, each with its subtypes, and the DIRT_SHAPE_* define each takes it as. The defines are for tails, for wings, and DIRT_SHAPE_PART for the rest, which take the field causes only (dirt_shape_takes()). It's made on first use, so a proc static can hold it whatever order statics start in. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_canvas_ofReturns the canvas a sheet drawn at some offsets sits on (dirt_canvases()), or null for none.
/proc/dirt_shape_takesReturns whether a kind of drawn part takes a general cause's dirt in a material. It never takes dirt in a material that wets cloth. A tail takes the ground's dirt along its underside, and smoke, wear, machine work and kneeling as a sparse film. It takes the field causes too: falls, splashes and full coverage (a plunge, a blast, a douse). Wings take the same, with the ground's dirt and kneeling as two drips at level 3. They take slime from above or lain in on the back too. Any other part takes the field causes only.
/proc/dirt_shape_slotsReturns the organ slots of a person's drawn parts that take a general cause's dirt in a material, each while it's drawn. The taur body takes what reaches its lower half (dirt_taur_takes()). That's only when its kind has a back (dirt_taur_takes_pools()) and no taur suit takes it in its place. Every other drawn part takes it by its kind's rules (dirt_shape_takes()).
/proc/dirt_shape_depositReturns a general deposit on a drawn shape in one direction, by the shape's own rules, as the reference generator draws each. A taur body takes a contact coat from the ground (crawling coats as walking), on its fur or a taur suit's cloth. It takes slime in its own pixels and a douse along its own top edges. The rest comes from the canvas field, as fur, or as a taur suit's cloth. A tail draws by dirt_tail_rasterise(), wings by dirt_wing_rasterise(), and any other part from the field (dirt_shape_rasterise()). Each draws inside its own drafting.
/proc/dirt_shape_field_facingReturns the direction a general deposit's canvas field is drawn facing on a kind of shape seen one way. It's for a shape that takes the deposit from that field (dirt_shape_deposit(), the hair's dirt_shape_rasterise()). Returns null where it draws the deposit otherwise. A tail's field faces north whichever way the tail is seen (dirt_tail_rasterise()), so one field serves all four directions. The other shapes' fields face the way they're seen, one for each direction. A tail, wings and any other part take a fall, a splash, a plunge and a blast from the field. The hair takes all it takes from it but a douse and slime. A taur body takes all it takes from it but slime, a douse and the ground's coat (dirt_taur_coat()). A douse follows the shape's own top edges, so that field never draws it.
/datum/dirt_wag_sourceA tail's sprite read wagging, with its states animated. Each frame is read as it's made, from sprites its job holds (dirt_frame_owners()). Each takes the still sprite's dirt, carried along the tail (/datum/dirt_tail_carry), and is baked into one animated icon for each layer (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/bake_frame()). The source is made once. Only shows_still changes, once a job finds it out.
/proc/dirt_wag_framesReturns a part's wag as dirt draws it, from its sprite read wagging and still: a /datum/dirt_wag_source of its frames, from its sheet's DMI metadata. Its job finds out, a piece at a time, whether a one-frame wag shows the still sprite's pixels, so the still masks draw. Returns null for a sheet made at run time, whose frames can't be read. Returns null too for a wag on another canvas than the still sprite's, because its frames can't be carried from it. Either way, it draws no dirt while it wags.
/proc/dirt_frame_ownersReturns what each pixel of a wag's canvas shows in one direction at one frame, as dirt_taur_owners() gives a still sprite's. Each state is read at that frame as dirt_sprite_pixels() reads a still one, and a state with fewer frames shows its last. Each state's sprite is loaded once into the job's own sheets, and never kept in SSdirt.sprite_reads, which holds still sprites.
/proc/dirt_tail_coordinatesReturns where each pixel of a tail, or another part that wags, lies on it in one direction, as list(along, across, steps, root). It's worked out from what each pixel of its canvas shows (dirt_taur_owners(), dirt_frame_owners()), with its layers taken together.
/proc/dirt_tail_spreadSpreads the distances inside a shape for one of its pieces, out from where the piece starts (dirt_tail_coordinates()). They spread over the shape's pixels that no piece reaches yet. It changes distances and pieces. A step across or down adds 2, and a diagonal one adds 3.
/datum/dirt_tail_carryA wag frame's dirt carried from the still sprite's in one direction, some of its pixels at a time (carry_pixels()). Each pixel of the frame's shape takes the dirt of the still's pixel nearest it along the shape and across it (dirt_tail_coordinates()). It takes none where that pixel has none. So the dirt swings and bends with the tail and draws as it does when the tail is still. The still's pixels are looked through a pixel along at a time, out from the frame pixel's place. The look stops one past the first of those that holds any, because one further across can lie nearer.
/proc/dirt_wag_packReturns a frame's pixels or a still sprite's, packed as text as SSdirt.wag_sprites keeps them. Each pixel of the shape, in the canvas's order, gives its index in two digits and its layer's place in the stack in one. A frame's pixel then gives its carry's still pixel in two (0 for none). The digits are in base 64, "0" to "o" (ASCII 48 to 111), as drawn() writes them. dirt_wag_owners() and dirt_wag_carried() give its pixels and carry back as they were.
/proc/dirt_wag_ownersReturns what each pixel of a canvas shows, from a frame's or a still sprite's packed pixels (dirt_wag_pack()), as dirt_frame_owners() or dirt_taur_owners() read them.
/proc/dirt_wag_carriedReturns a wag frame's dirt carried from the still sprite's in one direction, as /datum/dirt_tail_carry carried it. It goes by the still pixel each of the frame's pixels takes (dirt_wag_pack()).
