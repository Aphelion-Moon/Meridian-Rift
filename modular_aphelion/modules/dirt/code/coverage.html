modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/coverage.dm

/datum/dirt_cover_drawsEvery random draw a plunge or a blast makes, whatever its level, in the reference generator's order. Anything else drawn from the same field makes them on the same stream (dirt_cover_draws()), so a coat's line meets across shapes.
/proc/dirt_cover_drawsReturns a plunge's or a blast's draws, from its random stream.
/datum/dirt_douse_drawsEvery random draw a douse makes, whatever its level, in the reference generator's order. Anything else drawn from the same field makes the same draws from the same random stream (dirt_douse_draws()). So a stream of the douse down one shape meets the one down the next.
/proc/dirt_douse_drawsReturns a douse's draws, from its random stream. It draws twelve candidate streams with uneven widths and lengths. It keeps each one that lies 3 px or more across from every stream kept before it.
/proc/dirt_shine_drawsReturns where a wet coat catches the light: five points, each list(pixels across, pixels up), drawn from a deposit's random stream after its other draws.
/proc/dirt_plunge_thicknessReturns how thick a plunge's coat lies at a point: haze over a jagged line, and film along it. Under the line it's the body tone, with film windows stretched into vertical runs that open up with height, so the garment's shading shows. Short runs of thicker material lie apart, and the floor's row is solid. A glossy material keeps almost no windows under level 2's line. Bare skin thins with height much faster, in uneven blotches mixed with drip streaks, because even weight reads as camo. A light material thins out more.
/proc/dirt_blast_thicknessReturns how thick a blast's coat lies at a point: large soft round patches, because a stretched field draws tiger stripes. They cover more with the level, and the more squarely the point faces the blast. From level 2 some lie on the lee side, as clouds wrap round. The head's and the shoulders' tops ramp up smoothly, because a step draws a headband. A light material covers less.
/proc/dirt_facing_amountReturns how squarely a point at theta faces the theta facing, from 0 (away) to 1 (straight at it).
/proc/dirt_light_coverReturns a light material's coat at a point. Pale dirt never coats, because a coat reads as a pastel recolour or white hair. So its film stays where 2-3 px noise allows, about 55% of it, and the body tone only as clumps on spots that face up.
/proc/dirt_cover_collarReturns the height along the torso (h) over which a whole-body coat lies as film at most. It wanders by column, because a dark band under the chin reads as a beard, and a level one as a neckline.
/proc/dirt_douse_capReturns how many pixels under a column's top edge a douse's cap reaches, by level, with a jagged lower edge.
/proc/dirt_douse_thicknessReturns how thick a douse lies at a point, depth pixels under its column's top edge. A cap follows that edge, mostly the body tone, then streams run below it, and from level 2 the run-off lies round the feet.
/proc/dirt_douse_streamsReturns how thick a douse's streams lie under its cap. Each wanders as it runs, thins, and ends in a soft drip a little wider than it. Its length is its share of the level's reach, and level 2 stops above the knees. Two run at level 1, four at 2 and five at 3.
/proc/dirt_runoffReturns how thick what runs off the head lies on the torso. It flows down the front and back centre lines, heavy near the top, then thins to a jagged end. A light centre with heavy sides reads as an open coat. It's nothing on another limb.
/datum/dirt_feature/coverageA coat over the whole body from the field in mob space, rather than on one limb. It lies on every pixel of the surface, the margin round the body included. It's a coat, never a recolour. So every level keeps windows, and light materials clump rather than coat. Its body field tells it its material and surface before it binds the rows (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/body). It's a veil, so on a face it keeps to dirt_face_zone()'s limits.
/proc/dirt_coverage_causeReturns whether a cause coats the whole body from the field in mob space (/datum/dirt_feature/coverage): a plunge, a blast or a douse. A body's deposit of one is drawn a band of rows at a time (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/body).
/datum/dirt_feature/coverage/coverA plunge or a blast over the body.
/datum/dirt_feature/coverage/douseA douse over the body. Each column's top edge is read from the body's own pixels, as gravity is down in every view.
/proc/dirt_cause_plungeReturns a plunge's features, for a body that fell or waded in: knee-deep, then chest-deep, then over the head. At level 3 it smudges the cheeks too.
/proc/dirt_cause_blastReturns a blast's features, for a body caught in an explosion or a cloud: the side facing it, then everything. From level 2 the face is smudged whichever way the blast came, as every facing it draws is within 60 degrees of the front.
/proc/dirt_cause_douseReturns a douse's features, poured over from above: the head and shoulders, then streams down to the feet. At level 3 it smudges the cheeks too.
