modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/canvas.dm

DIRT_FIELD_BAND_PIXELSHow many pixels a piece of a mask job draws of a canvas field (dirt_canvas_field_band()): 8 rows of a tile, or 4 of the taur canvas. A douse draws that many of its shape's pixels a piece, and a taur's coat that many of its body's pixels (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/taur_coat). A plunge, a blast or a douse on a body's template binds 8 of its rows a piece (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/body).
/proc/dirt_canvasesReturns each canvas dirt is drawn on besides the body's: alist(DIRT_CANVAS_* define = list(width, height, how far its left edge sits from the tile's, how far its bottom edge sits over the tile's floor)). Wide sprites are centred on the tile (center_image()). The 64x32 taur sheet sits 16 px left. The 45x34 wings sit 7 px left and 1 down, centred by their sheet's 46 px dimension_x. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_shape_canvasReturns the canvas a kind of shape's sprite is drawn on.
/datum/dirt_canvas_viewEvery pixel of one canvas in one direction, mapped to mob space, as a surface that dirt_halo() reads. Each pixel lies on it, flagged as the torso's, a limb nothing on a canvas reads. No pixel is its rim, and no face guards any.
/proc/dirt_canvas_viewReturns a canvas's view in one direction, made the first time it's asked for. You should NEVER modify it.
/datum/dirt_canvas_featureOne mark of a canvas cause. It gives how thick it lies at a point of mob space, from 0 (nothing) to 1.
/datum/dirt_canvas_feature/ground_coatMud or dust up from the floor. It's solid where it meets the floor, then the body tone up to a jagged line. The line tilts along the body and drags up in one place, its tongue. Film follows under the line, haze lies just over it, and a film window or two break the body tone.
/datum/dirt_canvas_feature/ground_speckOne kick-up speck over a ground coat: a pixel of the body tone, or a 1x2 film drip, placed after the coats. In profile it lies along the body. Facing south only the specks at the front show, and facing north those at the back.
/datum/dirt_canvas_feature/patchesWorn or sooty patches: film where noise passes a threshold set by the density, and the body tone where it's densest. The reference generator's patches keep to a height band that covers every canvas.
/datum/dirt_canvas_feature/cover_fieldA plunge's or a blast's coat, head to toe at level 3, from the body's own field. It samples the same random draws in mob space, so the lines meet. A light material clumps where its noise marks a point as facing up.
/datum/dirt_canvas_feature/facing_patchesPatches broken up by noise on whatever faced an impact, densest where it faced it square on: a fall's contact, or a splash's spatter.
/proc/dirt_canvas_groundReturns a ground coat and the kick-up specks over it, at one level. Every random draw is made at every level, so the levels nest.
/proc/dirt_canvas_featuresReturns a canvas cause's features at one level, or null for a cause the canvas doesn't draw. Crawling draws as walking does, and kneeling and machine work wear as wear does.
/proc/dirt_soft_speckReturns a speck that doesn't read as a hard pixel, as alist(index = DIRT_ROLE_* define), on the canvas pixels it may draw on. It's a film centre with a haze ring, or a drip that fades from film to haze as it runs down.
/proc/dirt_place_speckPlaces a speck on a level's pixels and returns whether it went. It goes only where no pixel drawn so far touches it outside its own pixels. Its rows and columns also have to be free of earlier specks, and it then takes them.
/proc/dirt_canvas_coatsReturns a canvas cause's coats among one level's features: every feature but the kick-up specks. A whole-body coat is told its material's light and glossy flags first.
/proc/dirt_canvas_coat_rowsDraws some rows of one level of a canvas cause. Each pixel takes the thickest of the coats. If there's only one coat, its thickness is taken directly.
/proc/dirt_canvas_finish_levelReturns one level of a canvas cause on a whole canvas, once its coats' rows are drawn (dirt_canvas_coat_rows()). Lone pixels go (dirt_cleanup()). Then come the material's edge, halo or tide, and the kick-up specks (dirt_place_speck()). A material's sheen draws nothing on a canvas, as it looks for a leg. Hair takes film unless a whole-body coat covers it, and never core.
/proc/dirt_draftedCalls a drawing proc while drafting, as every general deposit is drawn. SSdirt.drafting is set and the noise lattice emptied for it. Both are let go as the proc ends, well or in a runtime (dirt_end_drafting()). dirt_rasterise() draws a deposit on a body through it. So do the canvas generator's entry procs when asked for outside a deposit's drawing. So a tail, a wing, hair or a taur body draws the same random streams and noise as the body it meets. If it's called while drafting, with neither a keeper nor a holder, it draws on the lattice already in use. A field's band is drawn with the field as its keeper. The lattice its earlier bands sampled is put back as the band begins and taken back as it ends. So a field drawn a band a piece hashes no point that drawing it whole wouldn't. A mask job drawing a deposit is its holder, and keeps the lattice between the deposit's directions. A direction drawn, or a field's first band, goes on from that lattice, and a field gives it back once done. A point's value is its hash's wherever it's kept, so the numbers are the same.
/proc/dirt_end_draftingEnds drafting a general deposit (dirt_drafted()). SSdirt.drafting goes back to what it was. The lattice the deposit sampled goes back to the field whose band it was, until that field is done. After that it goes to the mask job drawing the deposit. If neither takes it, it's let go.
/proc/dirt_canvas_rasteriseReturns a general deposit on a whole canvas in one direction, before a sprite clips it. It merges levels 1 to level, each from the cause's same draws, by role rank, and the later level wins a tie. A plunge, a blast and a fall draw from the body's own stream (":"), so the canvas meets the body. Other causes draw from "canvas::". Hair keeps to its crown (dirt_hair_crown()). A cause the canvas doesn't draw gives nothing. It's drawn while drafting (dirt_drafted()), a level at a time by /datum/dirt_canvas_field, which a mask job draws a band at a time.
/proc/dirt_hair_crownKeeps a field drawn as hair to the hair's crown, once every level is drawn. At levels 1 and 2 it keeps only pixels over 25 px above the floor, because an even film reads as a new hair colour. From level 3 it breaks them up with noise. It changes the field's levels (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/var/merged) and returns them. Done twice, it changes nothing more. Call it while drafting.
/datum/dirt_canvas_fieldA general deposit's levels on a whole canvas in one direction, as dirt_canvas_rasterise() draws them before keeping hair to its crown. It's drawn some rows at a time (draw_rows()). A mask job draws a field once, for all the directions it serves (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/field_for()), a band a piece (dirt_canvas_field_band()). In each of those directions the field is clipped to its shape, except on a taur body. It keeps the noise its levels share (samples) and the lattice its bands sample (dirt_drafted()). A field lives no longer than the job drawing it.
/proc/dirt_canvas_field_bandDraws a band of a field's rows, DIRT_FIELD_BAND_PIXELS pixels' worth, or as many of its shape's pixels for a douse or a taur's coat. It's one piece of a mask job, drawn while drafting. The field keeps the lattice its bands sample (dirt_drafted()).
/proc/dirt_draw_field_bandDraws a band of a field's rows in dirt_canvas_field_band()'s drafting, and returns whether every level is drawn.
/proc/dirt_field_band_rowsReturns how many rows of a canvas a band of its field draws (dirt_canvas_field_band()): as many as hold DIRT_FIELD_BAND_PIXELS pixels, and one row at least.
/proc/dirt_field_spread_noiseReturns two octaves of value noise (dirt_spread_noise()) at a position, for one term of the noise a field's levels share. It's sampled the first time it's asked for and kept in the field's samples, then read back. A term takes the same seed and scale, and the same point at a position, wherever it's asked for.
/proc/dirt_field_noise_hashReturns a lattice point's noise value (dirt_noise_hash()) at a position, for one term of the noise a field's levels share. It's kept as dirt_field_spread_noise() keeps its noise.
/proc/dirt_field_piecesReturns the most pieces a mask job takes to draw one deposit's canvas fields on a canvas, a band a piece. That's its rows at every level, smoke's four at most, in each direction. A douse takes no more, because each band draws that many of its shape's pixels, level after level. Nor does a plunge, a blast or a douse on a body's template, on the tile, at three levels (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/body). Nor does a taur's coat. Its view takes a piece. Then each of three levels takes bands of its body's pixels, the canvas's at most, and a piece for its finish (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/taur_coat).
/proc/dirt_clip_to_shapeKeeps a raster to a shape's pixels, changing it, and returns it.
/proc/dirt_shape_edgesReturns each column's top or bottom row of a shape's pixels, indexed by column + 1, null where it has none.
/proc/dirt_sparse_filmReturns smoke, wear or machine work on a tail or a wing, or kneeling on a tail. It draws film at a large scale (small blotches read as a pinto pattern), from the "wing::" stream. Smoke at level 3 and up also draws a broken band along the top edge, where soot settles. It's drawn inside a canvas entry's drafting.
/proc/dirt_tail_rasteriseReturns a tail's dirt in one direction, the same in every direction. Ground dirt runs along the tail's own underside, from the "tail::" stream, thickest where the tail comes near the floor and fading up through it. Smoke, wear, machine work and kneeling draw a sparse film (dirt_sparse_film()), and a douse runs along its top edges. Any other field cause comes from the canvas facing north, clipped to the tail, because the tail hangs off the back. It's drawn while drafting.
/proc/dirt_wing_rasteriseReturns wings' dirt in one direction. Wings never touch the floor, so walking, crawling, settling dust and kneeling give no ground coat. They give only two drips off the lowest feathers at level 3. Smoke, wear and machine work show as a sparse film (dirt_sparse_film()). A plunge, a blast, a fall or a splash reach them from the canvas field like any surface. A douse runs along their top edges, and slime lands in their own pixels (dirt_splat_rasterise()). Mess never does, because a shot at the back lands on the back. It's drawn while drafting.
/proc/dirt_douse_rasteriseReturns a douse on a shape in one direction. Each column's own top edge takes the cap, and streams run down from it, from the body's random draws ("douse:", dirt_douse_draws()). So a stream down a rider meets the one down the taur under it. Levels 1 to level are merged, each cleaned up on its own. A light material clumps. Hair is soaked first, and never takes core. It's drawn while drafting by /datum/dirt_canvas_field/douse, which a mask job draws a band at a time.
/datum/dirt_canvas_field/douseA douse on a shape in one direction, as dirt_douse_rasterise() draws it. It's drawn some of the shape's pixels at a time (draw_rows()), level after level. A mask job draws it a band a piece (dirt_canvas_field_band()), as many pixels as a field's band draws of the canvas. The job keeps it among its fields (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/field_for()).
/proc/dirt_shape_rasteriseReturns any field cause on a shape read from its sprite in one direction: ears, horns, hair and other parts. A douse runs along its top edges. Slime latched on, hit or lain in with the head gives a douse's level-1 cap in lumps. A solid cap reads as dyed hair, and a whole mass in the slime's colour as dye or a beanie. Every other cause comes from the canvas field, clipped to the shape. It's drawn while drafting.
/proc/dirt_splat_regionReturns the pixels of a shape slime reaches in one direction, as alist(index = TRUE). On wings or a tail, slime lain on with the back reaches all of them, slime from above their top edges, and other slime none. On a taur body, slime from above or lain on reaches its back, deeper when lain on at level 3. A hit on the chest reaches its front where the view shows it, and slime on the legs its lowest third. Other slime reaches none, because the head and arms stay on the rider.
