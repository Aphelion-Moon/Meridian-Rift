modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/landing.dm

/proc/dirt_mess_landPuts a mess on a person where a shot was aimed (dirt_mess_place()), when the portal shows a zone that lands.
/proc/dirt_mess_landableReturns whether dirt_mess_land() lands a shot at a zone: the zones dirt_mess_zones() lists, and DIRT_ZONE_COCK, which dirt_mess_regions() offers through a one-part portal and which lands on the penis alone.
/proc/dirt_portal_showsReturns whether a portal shows the part of its occupant a zone lies on: through a one-part portal only DIRT_ZONE_COCK, through a wall the groin and the butt (its relay draws no legs), and anything with no portal.
/proc/dirt_lower_zonesReturns the zones below the waist: the groin, the thighs and the butt. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_mess_amount_levelsReturns how many levels a shot's amount adds where it lands: 1 for a normal shot, 2 for a heavy one, and straight to DIRT_MESS_MAX_LEVEL for a huge one.
/proc/dirt_mess_placePuts a mess where a shot at a zone lands on a person (dirt_mess_surface()), as /datum/component/dirtiness/proc/add_mess() does. Each part drawn over that surface which the zone reaches (dirt_body_parts()) takes a splat of the same variant in its own slot. A back shot on a taur also puts one in its taur body's slot (dirt_taur_takes_pools()), unless a taur suit's own splat draws the pools. DIRT_ZONE_COCK lands on the penis alone, while it draws its front image. Then it draws the landing once (dirt_draw_landing()).
/proc/dirt_draw_landingTakes the oldest splats off a person past the cap (dirt_evict()). Then it draws each carrier a landing changed and the person once, so the masks asked for are the ones kept. The person is drawn whole if a part's share changed, as mess_changed() draws a part, and else only their limbs.
/proc/dirt_evictTakes a person's oldest splats off until they carry DIRT_MAX_SPLATS at most, mess and general deposits together. It counts their skin, their underclothes, their parts and their worn items. It draws nothing. The caller draws each carrier it returns, and each part.
/proc/dirt_carriersReturns the carriers of a person's splats: their own, then each worn item's, suit storage's included.
/proc/dirt_mess_zonesReturns the zones a shot can be aimed at on a person, in the order they're offered: face, chest, stomach, back, groin, thighs, butt. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_mess_regionsReturns the zones a shot on a person can aim at, in dirt_mess_zones() order.
/proc/dirt_mess_entryReturns a zone's entry in a shot's list of where to aim: "On their chest".
/proc/dirt_landPuts a cause's dirt in a material on a person. Each carrier that shows on a limb the cause reaches (dirt_cause_reach(), dirt_shown_carriers()) takes the deposit for those limbs. The carriers are worn items, underclothes and the skin, which takes only the limbs it draws. They share one variant and the person's dirtier side, so the deposit's lines meet across them. The sweat cause and a wet material never land on bare skin or underwear. A taur's lower half takes what reaches the legs it stands in place of. If it wears a taur suit, the suit takes that, else its taur body does. The suit takes it on every limb the cause reaches, so a biped wearing it draws them all. On a taur it draws as the hem band (ready_taur_cloth_masks()). The suit also takes what reaches the rider alone, as any garment does. The person's tail, wings, ears, horns and other drawn parts take what their own rules take, each while it's drawn (dirt_shape_slots()). Their hair takes what reaches the hair while it shows (dirt_hair_takes()), and a hat that hides it takes that as a worn item. A carrier holding the same cause and material raises that deposit instead (/datum/component/dirtiness/proc/add_dirt()). Then it takes the oldest splats off past DIRT_MAX_SPLATS and draws what changed, once (dirt_draw_landing()).
/proc/dirt_drawn_limbsReturns the limbs a person draws, DIRT_LIMB_BIT() of each: those with a sprite of their own (drawn_sheet()), so a missing arm or a taur's hidden legs take no skin dirt.
/proc/dirt_cause_reachReturns the limbs a general cause's dirt in a material can lie on, DIRT_LIMB_BIT() of each, or NONE for a cause that isn't one. They're the union of what the reference generator's deposits of the cause draw on the plain human template. The union takes in every general material of the material's shape class (/datum/dirt_material/proc/shape_class()). It covers every level, variant and side, on cloth, skin and a bare body's painted designs. A material with a halo reaches further, and wear and sweat reach the head only in one.
