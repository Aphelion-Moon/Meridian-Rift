/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/rasterise.dm

/datum/dirt_pixelsA raster's or a mask's pixels: a tone role and a thickness for each. Lists are indexed y * width + x + 1 in the reference generator's coordinates, as a /datum/dirt_surface's are: x from the left and y from the top, both from 0.
/proc/dirt_index_rangeReturns list(1, 2, ... count), made once for each count. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_stepped_up_rolesReturns each role a step darker, indexed by its DIRT_ROLE_* define: haze to film and film to body, the rest as they are. A material that its cause draws darker takes it, and so does a painted design at level 4. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_lighter_rolesReturns each role a step lighter, indexed by its DIRT_ROLE_* define: haze to nothing, film to haze, body to film and core to body. Fleck and glint stay as they are. Cloth's rim takes it, for every role of a coat but its core, and for a speck. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_role_ranksReturns each role's rank, indexed by its DIRT_ROLE_* define: where rasters or levels meet, the higher rank keeps the pixel. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_neighbour_tableReturns each pixel's neighbours on a canvas, in the order the reference generator visits them: right, left, below, above, those off the canvas left out. Built once per canvas size. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_surface_rowsReturns a surface's pixels grouped by limb and row, so a feature is weighed only on the rows it can reach: alist(limb = list(list(row, lowest h, highest h, indexes), ...)), rows from the top. Built once for each surface. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_rasterise_aimedReturns mess aimed at a zone, drawn on one direction of a body. It holds levels 1 to level, each from the deposit's same draws. They're merged by role rank, the later level winning a tie (dirt_stack_level()), and the result is despeckled. The top level then breaks its flat edges. It keeps every pixel of level 3 as it was drawn, at its role or a darker one.
/proc/dirt_rasteriseReturns a deposit drawn on one direction of a body. It's the one entry point for every cause. Mess aimed at a zone ("aimed_") goes to dirt_rasterise_aimed(). Any other cause draws levels 1 to level, each from the deposit's same random draws (dirt_cause_features()). They're merged by role rank, the later level winning a tie. If its cause draws the material darker (/datum/dirt_material/var/darker_causes), it then goes a step darker, haze to film and film to body. On a bare body's skin, a deposit with a hand-painted design (dirt_painted_rasterise()) wears it below the head in place of its own marks, which the head keeps. Then the whole is despeckled (dirt_rasterise_deposit()). A plunge, a blast or a douse is drawn by its body field (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/body), its bands back to back. A general deposit is drawn while drafting (dirt_drafted()), so its noise samples the draft's hash.
/proc/dirt_rasterise_depositReturns a general deposit drawn on one direction of a body, for dirt_rasterise(): levels 1 to level, then its finish (dirt_finish_deposit()). A plunge, a blast or a douse is its body field's bands drawn back to back (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/body), as a mask job draws them a piece each.
/proc/dirt_finish_depositReturns a general deposit on a body finished, from its levels stacked (dirt_stack_level()). It's made darker if its cause draws its material darker. It wears its painted design on a bare body's skin, and then it's despeckled.
/datum/dirt_canvas_field/bodyA plunge, a blast or a douse on one direction of a body, as dirt_rasterise_deposit() draws it, drawn some rows at a time (draw_rows()). A mask job draws it a band a piece (dirt_canvas_field_band()) and keeps it among its fields (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/field_for()). Each level's whole-body coat is bound a band of rows at a time (/datum/dirt_feature/coverage/proc/bind_rows()).
/proc/dirt_stack_levelLays one level of a deposit over the levels under it, and changes them. Each pixel takes the level's role where that role ranks as high or higher. Only a glint, once drawn, stays, because a later run beside it would fold it in. A fleck is taken like any other role.
/proc/dirt_cause_featuresReturns a general deposit's features at one level, from its ":" stream (dirt_general_stream()), drawn by its cause's proc (dirt_cause_procs()). A cause without a proc gets none.
/proc/dirt_level_featuresReturns a general deposit's features at one level for one direction. They're made afresh (dirt_cause_features()), or they're a mask job's, kept by level across the deposit's directions. They depend on the cause, level, variant and side alone. Their stream starts at its seed text on every call, and none of them reads it once made. So the first direction to ask makes them, and each later one takes them unbound (/datum/dirt_feature/proc/unbind()) to bind on its own view. A job draws one deposit's directions back to back, so the level keys them. The job's /datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/rasterise_next() stays on the same splat until it's made in every direction, then drops the features. Each of a job's features samples its noise through the job's table (/datum/dirt_feature/proc/noise()). This proc hands that table to the features, when a caller passes one. A body field passes none, so noise() samples afresh for its features. You should NEVER modify them.
/proc/dirt_cause_procsReturns each general cause's proc: alist(DIRT_CAUSE_* define = proc path). Each proc takes (level, /datum/dirt_random, side) and returns the cause's features at that level. For slime where it touched, the entry is list(proc path, the zone it touched), and the zone is passed after the side. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_cause_max_levelReturns the top level a general cause reaches. Machine work and smoke have a harder fourth, as mess has its covering one. Every other cause stops at DIRT_DEPOSIT_MAX_LEVEL.
/proc/dirt_runs_apartReturns a level's features with the runs and drops this view leaves out taken out, and its run plan with them. Runs and drops are tried in the plan's order. Each stays only if it keeps DIRT_RUN_GAP px of skin from level 1's shot and from every one kept before it. Side by side, they fill into a sheet. The two hung from level 1's shot and drop always stay. Then each kept run's carry-on stays where it keeps as clear. Level 2's carry-on from the first drop stays where level 2 shows nothing new in the zone's main facing. The plan holds the top run level's set, so every level keeps the same ones.
/proc/dirt_level_showsReturns whether a level shows anything new on the body over level 1's shot: a pixel the kept runs and drops draw that level 1's doesn't, beside either. A lone one would go.
/proc/dirt_footprintReturns the pixels features draw on in one view before the raster's cleanups, as alist(index = TRUE): where any of them lies thick enough for film. A feature hidden in profile is left out there.
/proc/dirt_footprint_touchesReturns whether any of a footprint's pixels lies within DIRT_RUN_GAP px of another's, in both axes: no skin between.
/proc/dirt_rasterise_levelReturns one level of a deposit drawn on one direction of a body. The first feature, in list order, to lie thickest on a pixel picks its role. A feature for cloth only or for skin only is left out of the other. A feature for profile only is left out facing south and north, and one hidden in profile is left out facing east and west. Nothing goes on an eye, the pixels beside it or a human mouth. A veil keeps only to dirt_face_zone()'s limits, and an eye's diagonals take film at most. On skin, a feature with a skin role draws in it. Cloth draws its rim a role lighter, so a garment keeps its outline, though a contact point keeps its tone.
/proc/dirt_covering_thicknessReturns how thick a covering feature lies on a pixel: film at most on the outline, and in profile broken by noise on the outline and beside it, to nothing, haze or film. A glint isn't broken.
/proc/dirt_face_zoneReturns the thickest a veil may lie on a pixel of a face. That's nothing across the eyes' top row and a pixel either side of them, because rings round each eye read as spectacles. Round a lone eye in profile it's haze there, because two clean pixels read as a chip. On the row under the eyes it's haze. Returns null elsewhere, where the veil decides.
/proc/dirt_skin_streaksStreaks bare legs in a raster rather than coating them, and returns it. A solid coat up a bare shin reads as boots. The body tone more than 2.5 px up a leg mostly thins to film, in clumps about a limb wide. Each pixel is judged on its own, so the raster is changed where it is.
/proc/dirt_cleanupReturns a raster cleaned up: a lone film pixel goes, a lone core softens to body, and a pixel whose role none of its neighbours share takes the role three of them share. Flecks stay.
/proc/dirt_skin_rulesMakes a raster as skin takes it, and returns it. No pixel without a neighbour stays, since a lone dot reads as a mole and nothing smaller than 2 px stays. Skin gets wiped, so its core softens to body. Goo keeps its core, because flattened it reads as body paint.
/proc/dirt_thin_lightThins a light material's coat on the legs, and returns the raster. Pale dirt lifts dark cloth, so a full coat reads as other trousers. The body tone stays on a leg's bottom two pixels and turns to film above them. The sole's row is film, with every third column clean. From 4 px up, pixels drop out: 14% more for each pixel up, 55% at most. Each pixel is judged on its own, so the raster is changed where it is.
/proc/dirt_ringsRings what ringed features drew with broken film, and returns the raster. Abrasion's light core marks dark cloth, and its ring marks light cloth. The ring takes about half of the bare pixels of the surface beside them, off the face's guards.
/proc/dirt_haloRings thick patches with film, and returns the raster. It's oil's wicked halo, or wet mud's damp edge. A body or core pixel with two or more such neighbours puts film on the bare pixels of the surface beside it, off its rim and the face's guards.
/proc/dirt_tideDarkens a drying coat's top edge, and returns the raster. About half of its film pixels with nothing over them take the body tone, in row-major order, two side by side at most, following the jagged top. It never makes core or a long run, because that reads as trim.
/proc/dirt_sheenPuts one wet fleck on a coat, and returns the raster. It's 1x2, on the leg's most convex lit spot, the boot's toe or the shin's front, 1 to 4.5 px up a leg. It goes on body or core with body or core under it, both inside the silhouette. The leftmost such pixel takes it, or the upper one on a tie.
/proc/dirt_cover_sheenPuts a whole-body wet coat's glints on a raster, and returns it. Each is a pale glint fading into a film pixel under it, because a lone pale pixel reads as a harsh speck. One goes on the pixel of the body tone nearest each shine point, within 3 px of it. The first in row-major order wins a tie. Glints go only on the torso and the legs, because on a hand one reads as a speck. They go inside the silhouette, on body with body under it and off the face's guards. They're 4 px apart counted along rows and columns, and there are most of them at most. Where they may go is read before any is put, so the raster is changed where it is.
/proc/dirt_ash_specksScatters ash's light flecks through a coat, and returns the raster. There are four at most, on the body tone inside the silhouette, each at least 3 px from the others, counted along rows and columns. They're placed in the order a noise hash puts the pixels, row-major on a tie. Pixels are bucketed by their hash, because the draft's hash has 65536 values, too many to sort a hash and an index as one number.
/proc/dirt_speck_shapeReturns a soft speck's shape, each pixel list(columns right, rows down, DIRT_ROLE_* define): a drip's film running down to haze, or a dot's film centre in a haze ring. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_scatterPlaces specks after the coats, and returns the raster. Each goes where it lies first in row-major order, on its own limb's pixels off the face's guards, and only where it's clear of every pixel drawn so far. A speck is soft: film with a haze ring, or a drip fading from film to haze. A strict speck also keeps off the rows and columns of the strict ones before it. On bare skin only drips are placed, because a dot there reads as a mole however soft. Cloth draws a speck's rim a role lighter.
/proc/dirt_overlay_stampsLays stamps over a raster, and changes it. A pixel they wipe goes clean, and a highlight they force turns film whatever is there. Every other mark draws where it ranks over what's there, never on an eye's or a mouth's guard, and film at most on an eye's diagonal.
/proc/dirt_despeckleReturns a raster without lone pixels, which read harshly: a lone film or haze pixel goes, and a lone body or core pixel becomes film with a haze ring where the surface reaches. A film or haze hole with three dark neighbours fills with the body tone, because it reads as a chip or a scar. Glints and flecks stay.
/proc/dirt_ragged_edgesBreaks a covering raster's flat edges, and returns it. No top or bottom edge runs flat along one row for more than 2 px. Cut level, a sheet reads as clipped at a region: an apron, a back patch, a bib. At irregular spots along a flat stretch, film runs on 1 to 3 px below a bottom edge, or rises 1 px over a top edge. It only adds film, on bare skin or haze of the chest and the legs, so what's under it stays, glints and flecks too. What's solid is read before anything is added, so the raster is changed where it is.
/proc/dirt_ragged_freeReturns whether a covering raster's flat edge may run on over a pixel: bare skin or haze on the surface, where the chest or a leg shows.
/proc/dirt_mergeReturns rasters merged into a mask: on each pixel the thickest wins, by role rank and then thickness, and the first raster keeps a full tie. The pixel takes the winner's material. All the splats on one surface make one mask this way, oldest first. A mess splat below the top level leaves out each of its pieces that comes within DIRT_RUN_GAP px of an earlier mess splat's. Splats piled on each other fill into one sheet. A top-level splat keeps everything, because it covers. Other materials neither keep apart from earlier splats nor make later ones keep apart. A splat's packed raster is read run by run as it merges, and never unpacked. The exception is mess that leaves pieces out, whose pieces are read on its whole raster (dirt_pixels_from_drawn()).
/proc/dirt_merge_with_partsReturns a surface's splats in one direction merged with the parts drawn under and over it. Each splat leaves out the pixels of the rounded parts on its zone (dirt_part_rounded()), because straight streaks carried onto a breast read as blocks. Then each part drawn under the surface takes each splat on its zone in its own pixels (dirt_part_field()). These merge in after the splats and keep every piece, as a top-level splat does. A part drawn over the surface takes its share on itself, so its field isn't drawn here.
/proc/dirt_pieces_nearReturns TRUE on each pixel of a raster's pieces, 8-connected, that come within DIRT_RUN_GAP px of a taken pixel.
