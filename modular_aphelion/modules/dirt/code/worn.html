modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/worn.dm

DIRT_COVERAGE_READSHow many sprites' coverage of a zone dirt_sprite_zone_pixels() keeps.
/proc/dirt_mess_garmentReturns the worn item a shot at a zone lands on, or null for none: the outermost of the head, mask, suit and uniform slots whose body_parts_covered includes the zone's flag (dirt_zone_cover_flags()). The face is covered when the mouth is, by the item covering it. Underclothes never count, as they aren't items. Through a wall portal only the uniform counts, while it's drawn, as the relay copies it and no suit (lower_body_only()). So a coat over the lower body leaves the shot to what the relay shows under it. A skirt the relay leaves out (wall_relay_skips_uniform()) leaves it to the underclothes or the skin.
/proc/dirt_zone_cover_flagsReturns the body_parts_covered flag each zone below the face lies under: list(zone = flag). You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_mess_surfaceReturns the carrier a shot at a zone lands on, and the underclothes it lands on: list(/datum/component/dirtiness, a DIRT_UNDERCLOTHES_* define or null). The outermost garment over the zone takes it (dirt_mess_garment()), else the underclothes that cover it, held by the person, else the skin.
/proc/dirt_mess_inset_imageReturns an image drawing cloth's baked dirt over a garment. BLEND_INSET_OVERLAY keeps it to what's drawn before it inside the worn KEEP_TOGETHER wrapper that build_worn_icon() makes. That's the garment's own pixels, and every image added to the wrapper earlier, such as a blood stain the dirt draws over. It floats, and on a garment drawn on the body's own tile it isn't offset. So the bulge redraws it over its cloth too (bulge_over_cloth()).
/proc/dirt_glow_underReturns whether anything drawn before a garment's dirt on the emissive plane could glow, so that the dirt's blockers dim it. A blocker is black and inset, and an inset keeps the alpha of what it's drawn over. Over the garment's own blocker, or over nothing, a blocker changes no pixel, and it never makes the garment's emissive mirror bigger. Any blood counts, as a stain can glow and dirt_order_worn() can move it under the dirt.
/proc/dirt_holds_glowReturns whether any image in a list, or under one, glows. A glow is anything on the emissive plane that isn't black.
/proc/dirt_blacks_outReturns whether a colour matrix draws every pixel black and keeps its alpha, as an emissive blocker's does (EM_BLOCK_COLOR).
/proc/dirt_worn_shiftReturns where a worn garment's wrapper has its wearer's own tile, as list(pixels right, pixels up). build_worn_icon() centres the wrapper on the garment's worn dimensions, then moves it by its worn offsets. So masks drawn on the body go back by both. A 32x32 garment without offsets gives list(0, 0), and a 64x32 taur suit, moved 16 px left, gives list(16, 0).
/proc/dirt_worn_read_shiftReturns where a worn sprite is read over its wearer's tile (dirt_worn_shift()). Returns null for a 32x32 sprite its wrapper doesn't move, which is read on the tile, where it lies.
/proc/dirt_taur_fittedReturns whether a garment is drawn on its wearer's taur body as a taur suit, as its slot draws it now (dirt_taur_suit_sprite()). A taur suit takes the taur body's dirt.
/proc/dirt_taur_suit_spriteReturns whether a worn sprite draws a taur suit. It does if its wearer has a taur body and the sprite spans the 64x32 taur canvas, with the body's own tile in its middle. A 64x64 hat or back item doesn't.
/proc/dirt_cover_layersReturns the overlays_standing slots whose sprites cover skin dirt, so it fades out beside them: the underclothes and the garments. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_cover_spritesReturns the sprites a body draws over its skin, from what it wears: list(list(icon, icon state), ...), in dirt_cover_layers() order. A garment's sprite its wrapper moves off the tile, or one that isn't 32x32, adds where the wrapper has the body's tile (dirt_worn_read_shift()). So a taur suit, a 64x64 hat or a hat drawn higher covers where it lies. dirt_shown_carriers() reads worn sprites the same way.
/proc/dirt_sprites_keyReturns sprites as one line of text, to key masks by and to tell outfits apart.
/proc/dirt_underclothes_wornReturns the underclothes a body draws, each its own surface: alist(DIRT_UNDERCLOTHES_* define = its /datum/sprite_accessory/clothing), as get_underwear_overlays() picks them.
/proc/dirt_underclothes_spritesReturns a body's underclothes as drawn, lowest first: list(list(DIRT_UNDERCLOTHES_* define, icon, icon state, accessory name), ...). Each is drawn as get_underwear_overlays() draws it, fitted to the body. A revealing loincloth has its flap cut away while the bulge cuts it (bulge_cut_worn()). They stack by their accessories' layers, then in that proc's order.
/proc/cmp_dirt_underclothes_drawnSorts underclothes from dirt_underclothes_sprites() lowest first: the higher layer number draws under.
/proc/dirt_underclothes_colourReturns the colour a body's preferences give a piece of underclothes, as get_underwear_overlays() passes it to make_appearance(), which draws a static accessory without it. dirt_underclothes_sprites() draws the piece in this colour, and sweat's multiplier reads the piece in it too.
/proc/dirt_underclothes_keyReturns the key a person's dirt on one piece of underclothes is kept under: the slot and the accessory, so it stays with that piece while it's off.
/proc/dirt_underclothes_pieceReturns the DIRT_UNDERCLOTHES_* define of the piece an underclothes key (dirt_underclothes_key()) names.
/proc/dirt_underclothes_onReturns which underclothes a shot at a zone lands on, or null for the skin. Each piece is judged by its sprite against the zone's pixels on the plain human template (dirt_human_zone_pixels()). So every body is judged on the human shape, and nothing waits on its own template.
/proc/dirt_underclothes_by_rulesReturns which underclothes a shot at a zone lands on before the human template is built: the topmost piece covering it by the rules underclothes follow elsewhere, or null. Underwear covers the groin and butt, an undershirt or bra the chest, and an undershirt the stomach and back too. A piece also covers what it hides (dirt_underclothes_hides()).
/proc/dirt_underclothes_hidesReturns whether the underclothes rules say a piece hides a zone outright. An undershirt that hides the groin (a corset, a leotard) hides the groin and butt. Underwear that hides the breasts (a one-piece swimsuit) hides the chest.
/proc/dirt_zone_underclothesReturns the kinds of underclothes that belong on each zone, in the order they take a tied shot there: list(zone = list of DIRT_UNDERCLOTHES_* defines). You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_sprite_zone_pixelsReturns which of a zone's pixels a sprite covers, read at those pixels only. It keeps a result for each sprite, zone, direction and placement, and drops the oldest past DIRT_COVERAGE_READS. A sprite given a shift is read where the shift puts it over the body's tile, whatever its size. One given none covers nothing unless it's 32x32. You should NEVER modify the list it returns.
/proc/dirt_zone_facingReturns the direction a zone (DIRT_ZONE_*) is judged from, the one that shows it.
/proc/dirt_human_zone_pixelsReturns a zone's pixels on the plain human template, facing the way that shows it (dirt_zone_facing()): the body pixels whose limb, angle and height lie in one of the zone's aim areas (dirt_aim_zones()). Null until SSdirt has built that direction of the template in the background (/datum/controller/subsystem/dirt/proc/human_surface()). Each zone is read once a round. You should NEVER modify the list it returns.
/proc/dirt_human_body_pixelsReturns the plain human template's body pixels in one direction, as indexes in row-major order. It returns every one, or only those one zone's image shows (dirt_pixel_zones()). Returns null until SSdirt has built that direction (/datum/controller/subsystem/dirt/proc/human_surface()). Each list is read once a round. You should NEVER modify the list it returns.
/proc/dirt_carrier_slotsReturns the slots whose worn items take general dirt (dirt_shown_carriers()). You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_shown_carriersReturns what shows on each limb of a person, as general dirt lands on it: list(carrier = the limbs it shows on, DIRT_LIMB_BIT() of each). Each carrier is a worn item (dirt_carrier_slots()), an underclothes key (dirt_underclothes_key()) or DIRT_CARRIER_SKIN. Each is judged as dirt_underclothes_on() judges a piece, on the plain human template's body facing south and north. Every carrier that shows counts, not only the one that shows most. A carrier shows on a limb where its sprite covers that limb's pixels and nothing drawn over it does.
/proc/cmp_dirt_drawn_topmostSorts what a body draws from dirt_shown_carriers() topmost first. The lower layer number draws over, so it's first.
/proc/dirt_bare_bodiedReturns whether a body is bare, so its skin and underwear take the painted designs where a cause has one. A body is bare when more than DIRT_BARE_SHARE of the chest's pixels on the plain human template, facing south, show skin. A pixel shows skin when none of its garments (dirt_cover_layers()), undershirt, bra or socks covers it. Underwear takes the skin's dirt, so it covers nothing here. This judges by what the skin's masks fade beside (dirt_cover_sprites()), so a change to those sprites has the masks made again (/datum/component/dirtiness/proc/check_cover()). Returns FALSE until SSdirt has built the template, and while the body is hidden (dirt_body_hidden()).
/proc/dirt_body_hiddenReturns whether a body is hidden under a worn sprite larger than its tile, as a sleeping bag is. It is when its uniform's or suit's sprite as drawn isn't 32x32 and isn't a taur suit (dirt_taur_suit_sprite()). Its skin then draws no dirt, and keeps it. Putting such a sprite on or off changes what the skin fades beside, so check_cover() redraws the body.
/proc/dirt_zone_layerReturns the layer of the surface a shot at a zone would land on: the garment's over it, DIRT_UNDERCLOTHES_LAYER where underclothes take it, else DIRT_SKIN_LAYER.
/proc/dirt_slot_layerReturns the layer a worn slot's garment draws at, which its dirt lies on, against which parts draw over or under it.
/datum/dirt_drawingWhat one surface's masks are made from and drawn with: a garment's cloth, one of a person's underclothes, or a part. A request is made when the surface's splats or what it's drawn on change, never on a draw.
/proc/dirt_slot_redrawReturns what to draw again on a body for an item in a slot (ITEM_SLOT_): a DIRT_REDRAW_ flag, or NONE.
