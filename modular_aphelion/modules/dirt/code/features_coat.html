/datum/dirt_feature/coatlineA coat up from a limb's lower end. It's contact at the bottom and the body tone up to a jagged line. The line tilts from front to back, and at levels 2 and 3 it rises in a tongue with a film tip. Film runs along under the line, so the coat's edge follows the line and is never one level row. One or two 1x2 film windows break the body tone.
/datum/dirt_feature/blobAn irregular patch whose outline wobbles round it, with a film ring past it.
/datum/dirt_feature/patchA worn-in patch that breaks up toward its edge with noise. Below full strength it draws film only.
/datum/dirt_feature/regionAn even tone over a whole limb. Its subtypes keep to part of it.
/datum/dirt_feature/region/bandA tone in a band round a limb, between two heights: a cuff, a hem.
/datum/dirt_feature/region/collarA tone over a limb's top, on its front, and on its back too where it reaches there: a collar.
/datum/dirt_feature/mantleWhat settles from above: a coat down from a limb's top. It's deepest on the side it faces, and the back takes back of its depth. It reaches a pixel and a half deeper on the dirtier side, and large noise roughens its lower edge. It's body over the top body_fraction of it, film under that, then a haze fringe: a gradient, never a bib with a hard hem. Bare skin takes it broken: a step lighter, with about half the skin left clean.
/datum/dirt_feature/dampWhere cloth soaks through with sweat. It's wettest in the middle with a soft rim. Large noise makes its outline wander, since sweat wicks out unevenly and is never round or level. Its shape (/datum/dirt_shape) gives each point's distance from the middle, 1 at the edge.
/datum/dirt_shapeA damp patch's shape. It gives each point's distance from the shape's middle, 1 at its edge. A point is given in pixels round the limb and up it from where the patch is anchored.
/datum/dirt_shape/ellipseAn ellipse about the anchor, longer below it where sweat runs down a side seam.
/datum/dirt_shape/ellipse_atAn ellipse whose middle sits off the anchor: a wing of a butterfly.
/datum/dirt_shape/hangingA shape hanging from its anchor, such as the V under a collar or a sweaty spine. It's width px either side at the top, and narrows by taper toward its rounded end hang_length px down. It's rounded over the anchor too.
/datum/dirt_shape/unionSeveral shapes as one.
/datum/dirt_shape/skewA shape widened on one side and narrowed on the other. A positive skew widens it round the limb's way. Mirrored halves of a back read as a printed butterfly, so a back's shape is skewed.
/datum/dirt_feature/fadeHaze up a limb from its lower end, over the pixels a stamp leaves (a dark hand). The stamp then fades into the forearm rather than ending at a line.
/datum/dirt_feature/contactA contact point, where the full darkness belongs. It's an upright 1x2 (a kneecap), so the cleanup can't fold it.
/datum/dirt_feature/speckOne speck: a pixel in the body tone, or a 1x2 film drip. Specks are drawn after the coats and kept clear of them and of each other (dirt_scatter()). A strict speck (kicked up) never shares a row or a column with another. A loose one (splashed) only keeps a pixel clear.
