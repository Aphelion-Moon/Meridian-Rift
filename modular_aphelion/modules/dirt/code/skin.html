modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/skin.dm

/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirtDirt a person's own carrier draws on their limbs: the skin's on each limb it lies on (dirt_skin), and the underclothes' on the chest (dirt_underclothes). Each draws its baked images' emissive blockers itself.
/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_skinA limb's skin dirt: one container image at DIRT_SKIN_LAYER holding the limb's baked image, with that image's emissive blocker beside it. So a limb adds one of each to its person whatever the splats, and an arm adds one more of each for its hand (/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_skin/arm). It lies under the underclothes, the genitals and everything worn. The masks are its carrier's, clipped to the pixels the limb shows, fading out beside what's worn over them.
/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_skin/armAn arm's skin dirt. It draws the arm's baked image at DIRT_SKIN_LAYER, as every limb does, and the hand's at DIRT_HAND_SKIN_LAYER. That layer lies over the hand and under gloves, and the hand draws over the uniform. Each layer draws only while its baked image has something to draw.
/proc/dirt_skin_overlayReturns a new skin overlay for the limb in a body zone. An arm's overlay draws its hand too.
/datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_underclothesA person's dirt on their underclothes: one container image on the chest at DIRT_UNDERCLOTHES_LAYER, over the underclothes and under the uniform. It holds the baked image of each piece worn and shown, already kept to the pixels the piece shows. Each baked image's emissive blocker draws beside the container. A piece holding sweat also draws its multiplier there (get_all_overlays()), and no blocker for it.
/proc/dirt_skin_zone_limbsReturns the limbs each skin zone's pixels lie on, as alist(body zone = DIRT_LIMB_BIT() of each). Its keys are the body zones of the limbs skin dirt draws on: the head, the chest, the arms with their hands and the legs. Mess draws on the head, the chest and the legs, where aimed mess lands. A zone's overlay draws while a splat lies on one of its limbs. The chest's rows from DIRT_BELT down lie on the legs, and an arm takes its hand. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_skin_besidesReturns the body zones whose sprites ready_skin_masks() checks besides a limb's own, for a limb that skin dirt draws on. For the chest those are the arms, which bring their hands, and whose own overlays draw only while general dirt lies on them. The chest checks the eyes too. For every other limb it returns none. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_eye_readsReturns what a body's eyes and its human head are drawn from as dirt_zone_sources_match() reads them against zone sources: list(the head, its limb_id, whether it draws eyes, the eyes, their sheet, their state).
/proc/dirt_eyes_unchangedReturns whether a body's eyes and its human head are drawn from what they were when dirt_eye_reads() read them. It reads them as dirt_eye_reads() does, without making a list.
/proc/dirt_emissive_blockerReturns an emissive blocker in a baked image's shape and at its alpha, to draw just after an image of it. The dirt then dims what glows under it as much as it covers it. It takes that image's layer, alpha, offsets and blend.
