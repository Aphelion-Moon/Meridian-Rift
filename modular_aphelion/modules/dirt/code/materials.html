/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/materials.dm

/datum/dirt_materialWhat dirt is made of: its tone roles' colours and the flags that change how it's drawn, as the reference generator's materials. One of each is made and shared (dirt_material()). Slime's and blood's are made per colour.
/datum/dirt_material/sweatIts tints say how wet the cloth is. They're white, because the cloth's own colour darkens under it.
/datum/dirt_material/bloodBlood run down a taur's body from its garment's stain (dirt_taur_blood_inputs()), in the stain's own colour.
/proc/dirt_materialReturns a shared material. One is made once a round for each id, and slime and blood get one for each colour. SSdirt.slime_materials keeps the newest DIRT_SLIME_COLOURS, and SSdirt.blood_materials the newest DIRT_BLOOD_COLOURS.
/proc/dirt_made_materialsMakes every material but slime and blood, and returns them as alist(DIRT_MATERIAL_* define = /datum/dirt_material).
/proc/dirt_slime_tintsReturns slime's tints from its colour, indexed by DIRT_ROLE_* define, as the reference generator's ramp makes them from tg's own slimes. Shadows turn toward blue or magenta, and a warm colour turns less, because its underside reads as dried blood if it turns far. The light tones turn toward yellow. Saturation is full at the base and gone only in the glint. Value runs from about a quarter to nine tenths, never black or white.
/proc/dirt_slime_toneReturns one tone of slime's ramp as "#RRGGBB", upper case: the colour's hue turned toward another, at a value and saturation each kept from 0 to 1. Each channel rounds halves up.
/proc/dirt_blood_tintsReturns blood's tints from its colour, indexed by DIRT_ROLE_* define, in only the tones tg's own stain shows. The stain's sheets are grey and drawn in the blood's colour, so each role is the colour times a grey, at an alpha. Each role's grey and alpha come from the stain's pixels beside the taur waist crop, on tg's worn blood sheets. Each channel rounds halves up, and each alpha rounds to three decimals.
/proc/dirt_hue_towardReturns a hue moved toward another the short way round, by amount at most, from 0 up to 1. Hues are in turns, where 1 is the whole colour wheel. A target half a turn away turns the hue down, as in the reference generator, where tg's closer_angle_difference() turns up.
/proc/dirt_rgb_to_hsvReturns list(hue, saturation, value) of a colour, each from 0 to 1, the hue from 0 up to 1, as Python's colorsys.
/proc/dirt_hsv_to_rgbReturns list(red, green, blue) of a colour, each from 0 to 1. This and dirt_rgb_to_hsv() follow Python's colorsys, as the reference generator does, rather than BYOND's HSV (tg's rgb2hsv()).
