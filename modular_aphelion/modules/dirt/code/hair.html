/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/hair.dm

/proc/dirt_hair_levelsReturns the causes that reach the hair, each with the first level at which it reaches it, as the reference generator's HAIR_CAUSES. A deposit of another cause, or under its level, draws nothing on the hair. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_hair_takesReturns whether a person's hair takes a general cause's dirt in a material. It takes a cause that reaches the hair at some level (dirt_hair_levels()) while the hair shows (dirt_hair_source()). It never takes dirt in a material that wets cloth. A deposit under the cause's first hair level is taken all the same and draws nothing. Once another deposit raises it, it draws as the body's does.
/proc/dirt_hair_outer_statesReturns the states of a hairstyle's pieces drawn over hats that show, in its order, as get_base_hair_overlays() draws them, or null for none. A piece doesn't show in a zone that a hair mask of what's worn strictly covers.
/proc/dirt_hair_dirtReturns the hair's dirt in one direction, as the reference generator draws it on a hairstyle. The deposits that reach the hair are merged (dirt_merge()) on its pixels less its darkest quarter (dirt_hair_kept()). They're then despeckled on those pixels (dirt_despeckle()). A pixel the despeckle adds takes the material of the first deposit with a pixel on them. A glint goes on the top three rows of those pixels, where a material with a sheen (DIRT_MATERIAL_SHEEN) lies as the body tone or core. It goes on the brightest such pixel of the hair, the first in row-major order on a tie. Every pixel names its material.
/proc/dirt_hair_keptReturns the hair's pixels less its darkest quarter, as alist(index = TRUE) in row-major order. The darkest quarter stays clean, so dirty hair keeps its strands. It's rounded down and ranked by brightness, and on a tie the first in row-major order is the darker.
/proc/dirt_hair_brightnessReturns a hairstyle's own pixels in one direction, uncoloured, with any opacity counting. Each pixel has its brightness, its red, green and blue added together. The result is alist(index = brightness) in row-major order on a canvas width across and height tall. It holds none for a sprite of another size. The hair's colour is never read, so no colour changes which strands are darkest or where the glint goes. It's kept with the sprites dirt reads (SSdirt.sprite_reads). You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_hair_layers_brightnessReturns the pixels of a hairstyle and its pieces drawn over hats in one direction, each with its brightness, as dirt_hair_brightness() reads one sprite's: alist(index = brightness) in row-major order. Where a piece draws, the pixel is the piece's, and the last piece listed is over the others, as they're drawn over the hair. With no pieces, it's the hair's own alone.
/datum/dirt_hair_viewThe hair's pixels less its darkest strands, as a surface that dirt_despeckle() reads. The haze the despeckle rings a lone pixel with keeps to those pixels, as the reference generator's despeckle keeps to the hair.
