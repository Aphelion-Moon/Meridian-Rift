/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/causes_ground.dm

/proc/dirt_up_hReturns a distance in pixels up a limb as h, the height along it: 0 at its lowest row, 1 at its highest.
/proc/dirt_limb_sideReturns which side of the body a limb is on: 1 for the right, -1 for the left.
/proc/dirt_legs_by_sideReturns both legs, the one on the dirtier side first.
/proc/dirt_hands_levelsReturns the hands' stamp levels: alist(hand zone = level), 3 at most. The dominant hand, on the dirtier side, is a level ahead of the other until both reach 3.
/proc/dirt_coatReturns a coat up a leg from its sole, and the height its line sits at: list(/datum/dirt_feature/coatline, pixels). The line varies by variant and tilts 1.6 to 2.2 px between front and back. On the dirtier leg it rises 0.5, 1.5 and 2 px by level, so level 1 stays contact only. It carries a tongue at levels 2 and 3. It stops 2.5 px under the leg's top, so the belt never cuts it into a level row. The proc makes every random draw whatever the level.
/proc/dirt_kickupReturns specks kicked up round a calf, a little more at the back. Each sits 1.5 to 3.5 px over its coat's line, after a clean pixel, and a few of them are drips. Five are drawn from the stream whatever the count.
/proc/dirt_settledReturns what settles from above: film clusters on the shoulder tops, none at level 1, two at level 2, four at 3.
/proc/dirt_cause_walkReturns walking's features: a coat up from the soles with a jagged, tilted top and a tongue, and film along its line. Spaced specks are kicked up over it. The dirtier leg's line sits higher.
/proc/dirt_cause_dustReturns dust's features: a thin coat from the shoes, which a light material lifts (dirt_thin_light()). From level 2 dust settles on the knees and the shoulders, and at level 3 on the lap too.
/proc/dirt_cause_kneelReturns kneeling's features. The kneeling knee, on the dirtier side, takes a patch from the kneecap down the shin and film up the thigh. Its kneecap takes a small packed core. The other knee takes a level less, with film down its shin. The kneeling boot's toe cap and the fingertips take dirt too.
/proc/dirt_cause_crawlReturns crawling's features: kneeling's, then a broad, broken patch on the belly, off its middle because a streak down the middle reads as a necktie. From level 2 the forearms' fronts take patches too.
/proc/dirt_cause_wearReturns everyday wear's features, as costume departments age clothes. Soiling darkens the collar, the cuffs, the trouser hems over the boots and the pocket edges, on cloth only. Abrasion lightens the knees, the seat and the elbows in the fleck tone, ringed in film so it marks light cloth too. At level 3 the dirtier thigh's front wears.
/proc/dirt_cause_fallReturns a fall's features, in mud or dirt. The side that hit the ground, mostly the front, takes broad patches across the torso, the thighs and the knees. From level 2 so do the forearms. The hands that broke the fall take dirt too. Drips run down from the torso's patch, with drops thrown round it. A fall forwards smudges the face from level 2.
/proc/dirt_cause_splashReturns a splash's features (a kicked puddle, a passing cart, a burst pipe), from the front or from 60 degrees toward the dirtier side. The drops lie on the side facing it, mostly on the upper body and arms and about one in six on the legs. Some run as drips, and level 3 adds two bigger splats.
