DIRT_PAINTED_REACHHow near a body pixel's place must lie to a painted point to take it, in pixels round and along the limb.
/proc/dirt_painted_cellsReturns the design each deposit wears on bare skin. The designs painted in their materials' own tones are keyed alist(":" = DIRT_PAINTED_* define), and grease wears oil's, in its own colour. The designs any material wears are keyed alist(cause = DIRT_PAINTED_* define). You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_painted_sideReturns the dirtier side of the body a design was painted on: 1 for the right, -1 for the left. A body dirtier on the other side wears it mirrored.
/proc/dirt_painted_pointsReturns a design's points: list(limbs, thetas, heights, materials, roles). Each list has an entry for each point, in the order its pixels were painted, row by row. materials is null for a design any material wears. The points are made once for every design, from the plain human template facing south they were painted on. That's SSdirt's template, or one built on the spot if SSdirt hasn't built that direction yet. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_painted_points_onReturns every design's points on a surface, as dirt_painted_points() keeps them: alist(DIRT_PAINTED_* define = list of each version's points). A point is a painted pixel where the surface has a limb other than the head, which keeps the deposit's own marks. Each point holds its place on that limb, its material and its role.
/proc/dirt_painted_designReturns the hand-painted design a deposit wears on a bare body's skin, a DIRT_PAINTED_* define, or null for a deposit without one. It's its cause's cell in its material, or else its cause's cell in any material (dirt_painted_cells()). A deposit without one draws on a bare body as on a dressed one.
/proc/dirt_painted_rasteriseReturns a deposit's hand-painted design on a bare body's skin below the head, or null for a deposit without one (dirt_painted_design()). A design is painted on the plain human template facing south. Each of its pixels becomes a point on that body's surface (dirt_painted_points()), so the design wraps round any body and turns with it. The deposit's own "painted:" stream (dirt_general_stream()) picks one of the design's two versions, a jitter round and along the limbs, and the seed of its noise. The points are mirrored for a body dirtier on the other side, and copied round the body where dirt goes all round (dirt_painted_around()). A level shows some of them (dirt_painted_shown()), the same ones in every direction (dirt_painted_near()). Each pixel of the body then takes the role of the nearest point on its limb within DIRT_PAINTED_REACH. The point painted first wins a tie. At level 4 the role is a step darker: haze goes to film and film to body. Every pixel drawn has a thickness of 1.
/proc/dirt_painted_nearReturns the points of a deposit's design that its level shows, by limb, for dirt_painted_rasterise() to place: alist(limb = list(thetas, heights, roles, alist(row = indexes), degrees per pixel, h per pixel)). Each limb's entry holds the points shown on it, each point's copies in turn, and their indexes by the row up the limb each lies in. A design painted in materials' own tones keeps the points painted in the deposit's material. Grease takes oil's, because the hands' design is lettered in oil and soot alone. It reads no direction's surface. Each point is copied and shown by its own limb, theta and h (dirt_painted_around(), dirt_painted_shown()), and the limbs' sizes come from dirt_metric(). It keeps the last deposit's points, so the four directions a mask job draws it in, one after another, build them once. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_painted_aroundReturns the thetas round its limb that a design's point is copied to. A design painted from the front goes all round where the dirt would. A plunge coats all round, and walking splashes all round the lower legs, so those go to the back and both sides. A knee's film and haze wrap round to its sides. Greasy hands are greasy front and back, and soot settles on the shoulders' tops behind too. Each copy is shown or not where it ends up.
/proc/dirt_painted_shownReturns whether a design's point shows at a level. At 3 every point does. Grease and soot build from the hands, and a blast from its core out. Walking climbs the legs from the feet behind a ragged line, because a level line reads as boots. Kneeling dirties the kneeling knee first, then both legs and the hands. Mud comes in patches that grow, a little ahead on the legs, because mud built from the feet reads as boots.
/proc/dirt_painted_gridsReturns the designs as painted, on the plain human template facing south: alist(DIRT_PAINTED_* define = list of its two versions, each 32 rows of 32 glyphs from the top, "." for none). It's read once a round, when the points are made (dirt_painted_points()), so it isn't kept.
