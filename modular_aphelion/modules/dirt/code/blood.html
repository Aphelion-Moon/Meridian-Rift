/proc/dirt_blood_last_kept_rowReturns the last stain row a taur's waist crop keeps in a direction, read from the crop itself (taur_waist_crop()). That's the last of the rows from the top that the crop keeps whole. It's read once a direction.
/proc/dirt_blood_seedsReturns where a worn blood stain meets a taur's waist crop in a direction, from the stain's last two kept rows of dirt_blood_sheet_rows(). It's a list of two lists, both indexed by tile column + 1. The first holds the alpha of each column's stain pixel on the crop's last kept row, out of 255, or null. The second holds TRUE in each column the stain reaches in its last two kept rows, or null.
/proc/dirt_blood_sheet_rowsReturns a worn blood stain's own pixels from tg's sheet, on the rows its mass is read from: DIRT_BLOOD_MASS_ROWS of them, down to the crop's last kept row. They're read once a state and direction, as list(list(tile column, row from the top, alpha out of 255), ...).
/proc/dirt_blood_massReturns what of a stain a taur's blood may hang from, as it's drawn on the taur canvas: the stain's alpha out of 255 on each such pixel, else null. That's its mass: the largest 8-connected piece of its pixels at least DIRT_BLOOD_MASS_ALPHA dense on its own rows (dirt_blood_sheet_rows()). A tie goes to the piece reaching lowest, then to the leftmost. The mass also takes every other such piece of 2 px or more that touches the rider's hands drawn over it. That's the stain going on behind a hand. At a height that stretches the rider, its pieces are found on the sheet's own rows, as an Average rider wears it. They're then drawn where the height draws each row. A row drawn twice or not at all changes no piece's size, so the same columns run at every height.
/proc/dirt_blood_pieceReturns the 8-connected piece of the taur canvas that a pixel starts, as its indexes in the order they're found, and marks each as taken.
/proc/dirt_blood_touchesReturns whether pixels holds a value at a pixel of the taur canvas, or at one of the eight round it.
/proc/dirt_blood_seam_rowReturns the row of the 32 px tile, from the top, that a taur's blood starts on below its garment's stain, at the rider's height. It's one under the stain's last drawn row: the last row showing one of the rows the crop keeps. The stain is cut at the waist before the height filter stretches the rider. The stretch moves that row, while the taur body under it stays put. The crop keeps the same rows facing every way. A stain moved for a species-shaped sheet (/obj/item/var/worn_effect_offset) isn't counted, because no such species is a taur by default.
/proc/dirt_height_rowsReturns which row of a 32 px tall image each of its rows shows once a person's height stretches it. That's how their rider and a taur-fitted garment are drawn (apply_height(), ENTIRE_BODY). The list is by row + 1, from the top, and holds the row each row shows, or null where it shows none. Returns null at a height that stretches nothing. Each filter is a displacement map whose green moves a row by up to its size (128 none, 0 a size up, 255 a size down). The maps are read at the nearest row, in the filters' order. Each height's list is read once, from the game's own maps.
/proc/dirt_blood_material_keyReturns a bloodied garment's blood as a material key, "blood#RRGGBB", in the colour its stain is drawn in.
/proc/dirt_blood_variantReturns the variant a garment's blood draws in, 0 to DIRT_VARIANTS - 1. It's a hash of the garment's first blood DNA, so two taurs bloodied by different people differ, and one taur's blood never flickers.
/proc/dirt_taur_blood_inputsReturns what a taur's blood below the waist is drawn from now, or null for none: list(the bloodied garments' stains, the taur-fitted garment's sprite or null, whether the taur body draws, that garment's slot or null, the outermost bloodied garment's slot). Each stain is list(its state in icons/effects/blood.dmi, its material key, its variant, and for a uniform under a suit the suit's sprite and where its wrapper has the tile (dirt_worn_shift(), null where it's on the tile), or null), with the suit's first. A garment counts while it's drawn and its stain is cut at the waist (/obj/item/clothing/proc/cuts_effects_at_taur_waist()), on a taur that takes dirt (dirt_read_source()). A garment's rips draw nothing. The outermost taur-fitted garment (dirt_taur_suit_sprite()) carries what lies on its cloth, and the taur body, while it draws, carries the rest. It's read from the garments and the sprites drawn, so it's never stored, and nothing has to change when the blood goes.
/proc/dirt_cloth_run_overReturns whether the blood run down below a taur's waist onto a taur-fitted garment's cloth draws over that garment's dirt. It does while the blood landed after the garment's newest dirt. On the outermost bloodied garment, that's its own stain's side (dirt_stain_over()), so the run keeps that side across the hem. Blood run from another garment landed when the person's carrier last saw blood land at the waist (waist_blood_landed). The taur body's share takes its own side, against the body's own dirt (/datum/component/dirtiness/proc/waist_blood_over()).
/proc/dirt_waist_blood_landedReturns whether a worn garment holds blood landed since note_waist_blood() last saw its slot. It does if it's another garment than the one seen there, or its blood DNA list is new. A merge makes a new list. The merge counts when it added blood that draws a stain on items, or added no DNA at all, as the same blood landing again. A merge that added only blood that draws no stain counts nothing.
/proc/dirt_waist_bloodReturns a worn garment's blood DNA as its forensics hold it, while the garment shows a stain cut at its taur wearer's waist (/obj/item/clothing/proc/cuts_effects_at_taur_waist()). So its blood runs below the waist, as dirt_taur_blood_inputs() reads it. Returns null otherwise, as for a garment whose blood draws no stain on items, or one drawn with no sprite.
/proc/dirt_taur_blood_dataReturns what a person's blood masks are made from, as a request holds it (/datum/dirt_mask_request/var/blood): list(the stains, the taur-fitted garment's sprite or null, whether the taur body draws, the seam row, the rows the rider and a taur-fitted garment show at the rider's height (dirt_height_rows()) or null). It's worked out as the game draws it at that height. The taur body stays put, the rider and the garment stretch, and the blood starts where the height puts the stain's end (dirt_blood_seam_row()).
/proc/dirt_taur_blood_keyReturns blood masks' data as one line of text, to key the masks by and to tell when the data changes. Given the taur body, it starts with the body's shape, as a drawing of the blood checks it (/datum/dirt_drawing/var/parts_key).
/proc/dirt_blood_carriersReturns what the taur canvas holds for blood in one direction, as the game draws it at the rider's height. It's a list of five. The first, its owners, is what each pixel shows, as dirt_taur_owners() stacks the game's own draw order (dirt_taur_drawn_stack()). The second is the carriers' pixels: the taur body's (while it draws) and the taur-fitted garment's. The third is those of them that show, and the fourth the rider's pixels that show off them. The fifth is the rider's hands, drawn over a uniform, in the rider's own rows (dirt_blood_mass()). The last four each hold TRUE or null per pixel. The rider and the garment are drawn stretched by the height (the data's rows), and the taur body where it is. Its owners are a list of its own. Facing north, where blood draws nothing (dirt_blood_continuation()), the owners are empty and the other four null.
/proc/dirt_blood_stretchedReturns a sprite's pixels on the taur canvas as its height filter draws them. Each row shows the row of the sprite it's drawn from.
/proc/dirt_blood_rows_drawnReturns what a list on the taur canvas holds once its height filter draws it. Each row holds what the row it shows holds. A row that shows none holds nothing.
/proc/dirt_blood_unstretchedReturns blood worked out in the rows the game draws a taur-fitted garment at its wearer's height, put back in the garment's own rows. The garment's height filter stretches them again as it draws. Each pixel goes on the row its row shows. Where the stretch draws one row twice, the pixels of both go on it, so no run breaks.
/proc/dirt_blood_hash_byteReturns the lattice hash of a point as a whole number from 0 to 255: dirt_noise_hash() times 255. Blood is never drawn while drafting, so it's the permutation table's entry.
/proc/dirt_blood_nearest_roleReturns the role whose alpha is nearest a stain pixel's, out of haze, film, body and core. A run's first pixel continues that stain pixel.
/proc/dirt_blood_lighterReturns a role a tone lighter: core to body, body to film, film and haze to haze.
/proc/dirt_blood_edgeReturns whether a pixel lies on the carrier's outline, where the pixel left or right of it isn't the carrier's.
/proc/dirt_blood_depthReturns how many carrier pixels lie between a pixel and the outline, to its left or right, whichever is nearer. The pixel itself counts, and the most is 6.
/proc/dirt_blood_last_rowReturns the lowest row a run may reach in a column. That's DIRT_BLOOD_FEET rows above the ground where the column reaches the ground, else one row above the column's lowest pixel (a belly's underside).
/proc/dirt_blood_startReturns where a run starts in a canvas column: list(the first row at or under the seam that the carrier is on, the rider's own pixels between, which it draws over, at most DIRT_BLOOD_DROP), or null where anything else comes first.
/proc/dirt_blood_walkReturns a run's pixels, one a row, as indexes. The run goes straight down the carrier. It steps a column aside where its hash byte is under DIRT_BLOOD_WOBBLE, though never on two rows running or in its first two steps. It also steps aside where the carrier's edge turns under it. A step onto the outline goes a column in, if that column isn't on the outline, else the run ends. Within inset of the outline a step goes a column further in, inset + 1 columns in all. The run stops where what it may take ends, at its column's last row (dirt_blood_last_row()), or beside another run. Facing east or west, a run stopped on its last row where the body turns into a coil behind it (dirt_blood_turns()) rounds the bend. Its last two rows give way to a curve back along the coil, each pixel on what the run may take. The curve is at least 2 px and at most DIRT_BLOOD_BEND.
/proc/dirt_blood_turnsReturns whether the carrier turns at a pixel. It does if, from that pixel toward back, the carrier goes on for DIRT_BLOOD_TURN_REACH px or more on that row. Every one of those columns must reach the ground too. A coil lying behind its front does that, and a leg or a belly doesn't.
/proc/dirt_blood_putPuts a role on a pixel unless a role that outranks it is there.
/proc/dirt_blood_holdMarks pixels and every pixel round them as taken, so runs never touch.
/proc/dirt_blood_neighbour_rolesReturns the 4-neighbours of a pixel that hold a role, as their roles.
/proc/dirt_blood_continuationReturns the blood a taur's garment stain runs down onto the carrier below its seam, in one direction, on the taur canvas: the reference's soaked band. Each stain pixel on the crop's last row (dirt_blood_seeds()) can seed a run. A run starts where dirt_blood_start() puts it. There are up to DIRT_BLOOD_RUNS runs, kept apart, the densest first, and the variant shifts which. The first run leads, and it's darkest under the hem. The rest are at most half its length and a tone lighter, and the second is short. Each walks down what shows of the carrier (dirt_blood_walk()), and ends in a 2 px bulb above the ground unless it rounds a bend. Under every seed column the carrier takes, a ragged band lies a few rows deep. No dark pixel stands alone. No row runs level, and nothing floats off the seam (dirt_blood_break_level()). North draws nothing, because lines up a taur's back onto the rider defy logic. Below another garment's blood, its runs keep off that blood and the pixels round it, and start nowhere it holds.
/proc/dirt_blood_sorts_beforeReturns whether one sort key comes before another, comparing the lists item by item.
/proc/dirt_blood_ends_levelReturns whether a run's last pixel ends on the row of another run's end within 4 columns of it.
/proc/dirt_blood_break_levelBreaks every level row of blood. While a row holds 3 px or more side by side, the one nearest their middle that isn't a run's own pixel goes. Ties go by its hash byte, then to the leftmost. Three or more of a run's own pixels side by side stay. Then any piece (8-connected) without a pixel on the seam row goes, so nothing floats off the stain. So does any single pixel left alone, which reads as a dot. So does any piece touching none of its stain's mass, so nothing hangs from nothing. Dropping a whole piece leaves every other piece as it was, so one walk tests all three.
/proc/dirt_blood_rasteriseReturns one carrier's blood in one direction of a mask job: each stain's continuation (dirt_blood_continuation()) on what the canvas holds there (dirt_blood_carriers()), kept to that carrier's own pixels. A uniform's stain is left out where the suit drawn over it reaches the crop's last row. On the taur body, the bridge layer draws the pixels a run crosses over the rider. That's the body's lowest layer that the game draws over the rider. On a taur-fitted garment, only the pixels on its cloth are kept, back in its own rows (dirt_blood_unstretched()).
/proc/dirt_note_taur_stainNotes a taur whose uniform or suit effect was just cut at the waist (/obj/item/clothing/proc/fit_worn_effect()). If it's a blood stain and the taur has no carrier of dirt yet, SSdirt makes one at its next fire, outside the render. Then the blood below the waist draws. A taur with a carrier follows its garments already (/datum/component/dirtiness/proc/on_overlay_changed()). Rips draw nothing below the waist.
