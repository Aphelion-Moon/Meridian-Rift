/datum/dirt_aimA mess aimed at a zone: one deposit's random draws, made once, and the features each level draws from them, as the reference generator's aimed cause draws them.
/proc/dirt_aim_zonesReturns each aim zone's areas, where its shots land: list(zone = list(list(limb, lowest theta, highest theta, lowest h, highest h), ...)). You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_aim_boundsReturns how far past its zone's heights a streak may end, for the zones whose streaks stay in them: list(zone = h). Off target they read as a U, bars or socks. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_aim_insideReturns a streak's end pulled back along the streak until its height lies between two bounds: list(theta, h). The streak keeps its own taper.
/proc/dirt_aim_insetReturns how many pixels a clamped streak end's or a capped drip's bound moves in: odd on one side of a pair, 0 or 2 on the other, so the sides keep to different rows. It stays under the room the streak or drip starts with inside its bound, so a streak never shrinks to nothing and a drip never runs up. Without the room an odd one moves 1 px out where that's allowed (the knee); elsewhere it stays on the bound.
/proc/dirt_mess_role_tintsReturns the mess's tints, indexed by DIRT_ROLE_* define, each list(colour, alpha from 0 to 1), as dirt_baked_icon() takes them. Pearl white, translucent where thin and near opaque where thick, a light grey underside that sets drops apart on pale skin, and a white highlight. Haze is the film's colour at half its alpha, drawn as film at half coverage. You should NEVER modify it.
