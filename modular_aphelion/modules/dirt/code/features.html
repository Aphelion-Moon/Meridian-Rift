modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/features.dm

/datum/dirt_featureOne mark a cause leaves on a body's surface: how thick it lies at each point of one limb, from 0, nothing, to 1. Only a contact point or a wet coat's underside passes DIRT_CONTACT, so the darkest role lands there and nowhere else.
/datum/dirt_feature/strokeA streak between two surface points, tapering toward its end. A fringe draws a film edge round it. A fluid's drip goes without, since a fringe would dither a 1 px streak. A fringe on one side only keeps two streaks from merging into one blob.
/datum/dirt_feature/globA rounded lump of goo where it landed: thickest underneath, a thin film round it, its outline wobbling a little with noise. A glint (/datum/dirt_feature/glint) goes on top.
/datum/dirt_feature/glintA lump's glint, one pixel on its upper side.
/datum/dirt_feature/glint/highlightA bead's glint on one pixel only, the nearest to its theta of those a glint lights. On a narrow limb a glint lights two side by side, which reads as a white dash.
/datum/dirt_feature/runA run straight down a limb, one pixel a row: in each row, the limb's pixel nearest its theta, where that pixel covers it. It isn't a streak along one theta, because that breaks up wherever the view foreshortens the limb.
/proc/dirt_pixel_spanReturns how many degrees a pixel of a row spans. It's the widest turn to a pixel beside it in the row, but never less than the limb's own.
/datum/dirt_feature/glazeA translucent tongue hanging from where it landed. It's film in its middle, thinning to broken haze toward its edge, because a sheet even to its edge reads as a garment. Its top and tip move up and down across it with noise.
/datum/dirt_feature/spillA run or a tongue carried on past the torso's last row onto the leg below, straight down the column it left the torso by. A coat that stops at the waist reads as an apron or a tube top's hem. A run ends in its grey underside, a tongue in haze, with film beside its upper half.
/datum/dirt_feature/run_planA mess's runs and drops as its top run level has them, in the order a view keeps them (dirt_runs_apart()). So every level keeps the same ones. It draws nothing itself.
