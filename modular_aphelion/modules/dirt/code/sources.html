modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/sources.dm

/datum/dirt_sourceA game event that lands general dirt through the gate, dirt_source_land(). It's never made. The gate reads its cause, material, level, chance, cooldown and deferred off the type, and keys its cooldown by the type. The event's own procs read the extra vars on subtypes, and pass the gate what the event works out, such as a slime's colour.
/datum/dirt_source/slimeSlime, in the colour that the call passes.
/datum/dirt_source/slime/hitA slime's hit. It lands where it hit, and every zone shares the one cooldown.
/datum/dirt_source/slime/touchA slime person's touch. Each kind of touch keeps a cooldown for each place on the body it lands, by slime cause.
/datum/dirt_source/tool_workTool work on a machine, a vehicle, a mech or a cyborg.
/datum/dirt_source/floor_decalWalking or crawling over a dirty decal. It has no cooldown, but floor_check_interval limits how often it looks.
/datum/dirt_source/soot/blastAn explosion, which lands at a level equal to its severity.
/datum/dirt_source/soot/fireCatching fire, and the fire going out.
/datum/dirt_source/fallA fall from standing, which lands what's underfoot.
/datum/dirt_source/restingLying down by choice on a dirty spot.
/datum/dirt_source/rollingEach turn of rolling on a dirty spot, as stop, drop and roll puts out a fire.
/datum/dirt_source/reagentsReagents touching someone. A splash and wading each keep a cooldown for each material.
/datum/dirt_source/reagents/wadingStanding in liquid. It lands as walking at the ankles, and deeper as a plunge, one level for each depth past them.
/datum/dirt_source/sweatSweat, which lands on cloth only.
/datum/dirt_source/sweat/worked_upWorking out, or an overheating core.
/datum/component/dirt_sourcesThe listener for the events on a person that land general dirt in a round. Every human but a preview dummy gets one as it's made, and keeps it. It stores the cooldowns that the gate (dirt_source_land()) holds those events to.
/proc/dirt_source_landLands a game event's general dirt on a person through dirt_land(), as often as the event allows. Every source in a round lands its dirt here, never through dirt_land() itself.
/datum/dirt_job/landingA landing that waits for a later SSdirt fire (dirt_source_land()). What it lands is fixed when the event happens.
/proc/dirt_touches_floorReturns whether a person touches the floor they're on.
/proc/dirt_decal_materialsReturns what each dirty floor decal leaves on someone moving over it, by its type and each of its subtypes: list(decal type = DIRT_MATERIAL_* define). Glitter, vomit and every blood but oil leave nothing. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_underfoot_materialReturns what the first dirty decal on a floor leaves (dirt_decal_materials()), or null. Blood is left out, because a wet oil pool lands its oil as it's stepped in (/datum/component/dirt_sources/proc/on_stepped_in_pool()).
/proc/dirt_slime_causeReturns the slime cause for a BODY_ZONE_* define, precise or not, folded onto its base zone (check_zone()).
/proc/dirt_hand_slime_causeReturns the slime cause for the arm of the hand a person is using.
/proc/dirt_slime_colourReturns the colour a slime's slime lands in, its type's rgb_code. A rainbow slime's own colour is grey, so it lands a random slime type's.
/proc/dirt_slime_hitLands a slime's hit as slime in its colour on the zone it aimed at. A chest hit from behind lands on the back. For a slime with no player, /datum/element/attack_zone_randomiser picks that zone.
/proc/dirt_jelly_colourReturns a slime person's colour, their mutant colour, or null for anyone else and for a colour that isn't "#RRGGBB".
/proc/dirt_slime_touchLands slime where a slime person touched someone, in the slime person's colour. It works both ways. If the one touched is the slime person, the one touching takes theirs, and two slime people each take the other's. Each kind of touch keeps a cooldown for each slime cause it lands on. So a grab doesn't hold off the bear hug that follows it.
/proc/dirt_tool_workLands machine work's dirt on someone who finished a tool job, by chance. A machine leaves grease, and a vehicle, a mech or a cyborg leaves oil. Only a job with a tool in dirty_tools counts, so a scan or a multitool link leaves nothing. Engineers roll a lower chance. Someone with no mind or job isn't one.
/proc/dirt_ground_materialsReturns what falling on the ground itself leaves, by turf type: list(type = DIRT_MATERIAL_* define, or null for nothing). You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_fall_materialReturns what a fall onto a floor lands: a dirty decal's, else a wet oil pool's, else the ground's own, or null.
/proc/dirt_lie_on_spotLands the dirt of the spot someone lies on (dirt_fall_material()) on them, as the source passed in, a /datum/dirt_source subtype. If the spot is clean or they don't touch the floor, nothing lands.
/proc/dirt_reagent_materialsReturns what each reagent that dirties leaves on someone it touches, by its type and each of its subtypes: list(reagent type = DIRT_MATERIAL_* define). You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_reagent_colourReturns a reagent's colour as "#RRGGBB", its alpha left off, or null if it isn't one.
/proc/dirt_wading_depthReturns how deep a person stands in liquid, a LIQUID_STATE_* define, or 0 under the ankles. Lying in it counts deeper, as the liquid's own exposure counts it.
/proc/dirt_reagent_landLands a material from reagents on someone they touched, as wading in the liquid they stand in or as a splash.
/proc/dirt_work_up_sweatLands the sweat of working out or overheating, unless they're in water. The two share one cooldown.
/proc/dirt_wears_sweatReturns whether a person's underclothes or anything they wear holds sweat. Bare skin never does.
