modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/taur.dm

DIRT_POOL_KEYA pool pixel as a key, from its column and row on the taur canvas: a pool's drip can run a few pixels off it.
DIRT_MESS_TAUR_SEEDA fixed seed value, the reference generator's: changing it reshapes every taur splat.
/datum/dirt_shape_sourceA drawn shape's sprite as dirt reads it: the states of each of its layers, as it draws them on its canvas (dirt_canvases()). A layer's colour layers are taken together (a matrixed part's channel images). The shape is a taur body, a tail, wings, ears, horns or another drawn part. It's made once and never changed.
/datum/dirt_taur_sourceA taur body's sprite as dirt reads it, on the 64x32 taur canvas, with its layers stacked round its rider (dirt_taur_stack()). Mess aimed at the back puts pools along the back that these pixels show (dirt_taur_pools()).
/proc/dirt_taur_stackReturns what a taur canvas shows, lowest first, as the reference stacks a taur under its rider: the taur body's behind and adjacent layers, the rider, then its front layers. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_taur_sourceReturns a person's taur body as dirt reads it, drawn or hidden (a taur suit hides it), or null without one.
/proc/dirt_taur_drawn_stackReturns what a taur canvas shows, lowest first, as the game draws it over a taur's own pixels: the taur body's behind layer, the rider's limbs, the taur's adjacent and front-under layers, a taur-fitted garment's cloth, then the taur's front layers, which are drawn over that garment. Blood below the waist reads this stack (dirt_blood_carriers()), while mess keeps dirt_taur_stack(). You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_taur_ownersReturns what each pixel of a shape's canvas shows in one direction. That's the EXTERNAL_* layer of the shape drawn last over it, in its stack's order. On a taur body it's DIRT_TAUR_RIDER where the rider (the body, 16 px in) is drawn over it. It's DIRT_TAUR_CLOTH where a taur-fitted garment is, and null where nothing is.
/proc/dirt_shape_maskReturns a shape's own pixels in one direction, as alist(index = TRUE) in row-major order: every pixel its layers show, leaving out the rider's.
/proc/dirt_taur_poolsReturns a back shot's pools on a taur body in one direction. dirt_splat_rasterise() draws them as mess on the pixels the taur body shows, keeping clear of the rider's mess there.
/proc/dirt_taur_height_fillCarries a taur body's merged dirt onto the pixels its rider's height uncovers. The dirt is made on the canvas as an Average rider shows it. Pools, slime and a douse lie on what the taur body shows then, so a rider stretched taller leaves bare pixels under its waist. A canvas field or a coat is drawn on the whole canvas, under the rider too. So a pixel the merge draws there keeps its own dirt. Each other uncovered pixel looks at the pixel just below its run of uncovered pixels in the column. If the taur body shows that pixel at Average height, the uncovered pixel takes its dirt. A glint is carried as the body tone, so it isn't doubled. A carried pixel is shown on the layer it shows at the rider's height, whichever layer the pixel below is on. The pixels a shorter rider covers keep their dirt here, and layers_shown() leaves them out.
/proc/dirt_splat_rasteriseReturns a splat drawn in a shape's own pixels in one direction. It's the one entry point for slime and mess on taur bodies, wings and tails. Mess draws only as a back shot's pools on a taur body (dirt_mess_splat_rasterise()), drawn the portable way, and any other mess draws nothing. Every other material lands as slime's lumps (dirt_slime_splat_rasterise()), drawn while drafting.
/proc/dirt_taur_back_regionReturns the pixels of a taur body mess aimed at its back reaches in one direction: a band 3 px deep under the back line the view shows. Facing south it's none, as the band there is the rider's waist, which reads as underwear. Facing north it's the columns whose top lies within 2 px below the back's middle height. In profile it's the columns whose top lies within 1 px of it. A profile leaves out the 30% at the far end (a white tuft at the tail) and the 2 beside the rider's waist (the groin).
/proc/dirt_mess_splat_rasteriseReturns mess aimed at a taur body's back in one direction, from the "splat:aimed_back:taur:" and DIRT_MESS_TAUR_SEED streams. Broken pools lie along the back line (dirt_taur_back_region()), ragged underneath, with drips of uneven length off them. They're opaque at the core, with a glint on each drop and a translucent fringe, all on the taur body's pixels. Level 1 is one drop, and levels 2 and 3 two pools, level 3's heavier. A drop or pool that comes beside the rider's mess, or under it where a line would carry on down, is left out whole. A pool beside it reads as one straight line across both bodies. Its levels don't nest, as each is drawn from the streams on its own.
/proc/dirt_pool_putLays a solid role on a pool pixel, noting it as the drop or pool's own if nothing was laid there before.
/proc/dirt_pool_clearReturns whether a pool's columns keep more than 2 px from every column of the pools kept before it.
/proc/dirt_pool_drawDraws a role on a pool pixel of the taur body unless the role drawn there outranks it. It never draws off the taur body's pixels.
/proc/dirt_slime_splat_rasteriseReturns slime landed in a shape's own pixels in one direction, from the "splat:::" stream. It draws lumps in the region its cause reaches on that kind of shape (dirt_splat_region()), darkest underneath with a glint on top. Drips run down from them and end in a bulb, and spatter lies beside them. Nothing lands off the shape. It's drawn inside the drafting that dirt_splat_rasterise() sets up (SSdirt.drafting). So the lumps' starts are picked from the region column by column, as the draft picks them.
/proc/dirt_shape_putDraws a role on a pixel of a shape unless the role drawn there outranks it. It never draws off the shape's pixels.
/proc/dirt_round_coordinateReturns a coordinate rounded to a whole pixel, with halves to even, as the draft's round() takes them.
/proc/dirt_taur_takes_poolsReturns whether a back shot on a person also lands along their taur body's back. It does when their taur body has a back (dirt_read_source(): not Big Legs) and draws now. It doesn't when the shot lands on a garment drawn over the whole taur canvas (a taur suit), which takes the pools itself (/datum/component/dirtiness/proc/ready_taur_cloth_masks()).
/proc/dirt_taur_takesReturns whether a general cause's dirt in a material lands on a taur's lower half: its taur body, or the taur suit over it. It lands if it reaches the legs the taur body stands in place of. Slime on the back, the front or the legs lands too (dirt_splat_region()). A wet material never does, because neither a coat nor a hem band takes sweat.
/proc/dirt_taur_suitReturns the outermost taur suit a taur wears over its taur body (dirt_taur_fitted()), or null. The suit takes the general dirt the taur body would take, as its hem band.
/proc/dirt_canvas_blankReturns a blank icon the size of a canvas (dirt_canvases()), for masks on it. You should NEVER modify it.
