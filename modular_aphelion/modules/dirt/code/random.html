DIRT_DRAFT_WORDSHow many twister words the draft stream keeps its state in, and how far ahead the twist reaches (MT19937's N and M).
DIRT_LATTICE_POINTSThe most of the draft's noise lattice points that SSdirt.lattice keeps before it's emptied (dirt_draft_noise()).
DIRT_DRAFT_PRODUCT_LOWThe low half of the 32-bit word (high, low) times the constant (by_high, by_low), mod 2^32: low * by_low, taken through low's bytes so no product passes 2^24. Pass it variables, because it reads them more than once.
DIRT_DRAFT_PRODUCT_HIGHThe high half of that product: the low half's carry, then low * by_high and high * by_low mod 2^16, through bytes.
/datum/dirt_randomA stream of random draws seeded by text, the same on every server and in the reference generator. So a mask drawn here matches the reference's pixel for pixel. The nth draw, counting from 0, is md5("[seed]:[n]") cut to its first six hex digits: a 24-bit integer, which a DM number holds exactly. Mess takes its random draws from it, and general deposits take theirs from the draft stream (/datum/dirt_random/draft).
/proc/dirt_general_streamReturns the draft stream a general deposit draws from, for its seed text as the reference spells it ("walk:3").
/datum/dirt_random/draftThe original draft's stream: Python's random.Random(seed text), word for word, so a general deposit draws as the draft drew it. A Mersenne twister (MT19937) seeded from text as CPython seeds it, on 16-bit halves, every product through bytes so nothing passes 2^24. random() is CPython's 53-bit fraction rounded to float32, as the reference rounds it. randrange(), randint(), choose() and shuffle() take whole draws as CPython's _randbelow() does, rejecting those past the count. gauss() keeps the second of each pair for its next call. choices() walks the weights' running sums to the first past random() times their total. That picks what CPython's bisect picks. draw() and sample() are the portable stream's alone, and crash on this one.
/datum/dirt_draft_wordsA seed text's draft-stream words, as every stream of it reads them. It holds the tempered words made so far. While it's SSdirt.draft_open, it also holds the twister's state to make more. When it's closed and a stream needs more, it's seeded again and twisted on to where it stopped.
/proc/dirt_draft_seededReturns the twister's state for a seed text as CPython's random.Random(text) leaves it before its first twist: init_genrand(19650218), then init_by_array() of its key. The key is the text's bytes then their SHA-512 digest, read as one big-endian number and cut into 32-bit words from the lowest. The state is DIRT_DRAFT_WORDS words, high and low halves in turn.
/proc/dirt_fraction_belowReturns whether fraction * count is under bound, taken exactly, because float32 can round the product up onto a whole number it's under. The fraction is a mantissa over a power of 2, and mantissa * count is taken in 12-bit pieces.
/proc/dirt_fraction_indexReturns int(fraction * count), taken exactly, as the reference takes the draft's draw_index().
/proc/dirt_draft_hash_bitsReturns the draft's noise hash of a lattice point as its 16 bits, 0 to 65535: (x * 374761393 + y * 668265263 + seed * 2147483647) mod 2^32, then h ^ (h >> 13), times 1274126177, and the low half of h ^ (h >> 16). Every 32-bit word is two 16-bit halves, and a negative number is its two's complement. A point within 512 of the origin reads its column's and row's terms from tables, and a point further out works them out. The last multiply is read a byte at a time from tables (dirt_draft_hash_tables()). Each table entry is the product worked out once, so the bits are the same.
/proc/dirt_draft_hash_tablesReturns the tables the draft's noise hash reads (dirt_draft_hash_bits()), each 32-bit product as its high half then its low. From 1 are the column terms (x * 374761393) of x from -512 up to 512, at (x + 512) * 2 + 1. From 2049 are the row terms (y * 668265263), laid out the same way. From 4097 is each byte b in byte place t of a word (from the lowest) times 1274126177, at (t * 256 + b) * 2 + 4097. That's 6,144 numbers, built on the first call. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_draft_noiseReturns the draft's noise value of a lattice point, from 0 to 1 in steps of 1/65535. SSdirt.lattice keeps it while the general deposit sampling it is drawn (dirt_drafted()).
/proc/dirt_perm_tableReturns the table the noise hashes lattice points through: 0 to 255, shuffled by the "dirt:perm" stream.
/proc/dirt_noise_hashReturns a lattice point's noise value, from 0 to 1 in steps of 1/255. While a general deposit is drawn (SSdirt.drafting), it's the draft's value (dirt_draft_noise()), read in place where SSdirt.lattice keeps it.
/proc/dirt_value_noiseReturns smooth value noise from 0 to 1: the four lattice points round a sample, blended with a smoothstep. Each point is dirt_noise_hash()'s, with its chain's seed steps taken once for all four and each column's step once. While a general deposit is drawn (SSdirt.drafting), each point is dirt_draft_noise()'s instead. It's read in place where SSdirt.lattice keeps all four, and asked of dirt_draft_noise() for the rest.
/proc/dirt_spread_noiseReturns two octaves of value noise, stretched back out to cover 0 to 1 (summed noise bunches round 0.5).
