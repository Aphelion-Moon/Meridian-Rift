modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/subsystem.dm

DIRT_MAX_FINISH_ROUNDSThe most rounds finish_all() goes: the bodies a round draws again can ask for masks, which the next round makes.
DIRT_TEMPLATE_CACHE_SIZEHow many templates SSdirt keeps built, besides SSdirt.human_template. One a queued job holds lives until that job is done.
DIRT_MASK_CACHE_SIZEHow many masks SSdirt keeps. Masks dropped and made again reuse their cached rasters and their icons' resource cache entries.
DIRT_RASTER_CACHE_SIZEHow many splats' rasters SSdirt keeps, each one text under its key (/datum/dirt_pixels/proc/drawn()). A splat dropped is rasterised again when its body's masks are next made.
DIRT_SPRITE_READSHow many whole sprite directions dirt_sprite_pixels() keeps, the ones read most lately. Only SSdirt's pieces read them.
DIRT_SPRITE_FACINGSHow many sprites' facings dirt_sprite_facings() keeps. SSdirt.sprite_one_way keeps as many sprites.
DIRT_RASTER_SEPARATORWhat a cached raster's directions are joined by: drawn() writes only "0" to "o" (ASCII 48 to 111).
DIRT_DRAFT_TEXTSHow many seed texts' draft-stream words SSdirt keeps (/datum/dirt_draft_words). It's more than the general causes use between them, so each text is seeded only once.
DIRT_WET_CACHE_SIZEHow many sweat multipliers SSdirt keeps (/datum/dirt_mask_request/wet), the ones asked for most lately. Each is one baked icon under its key, one for each garment look and the masks sweat lies on.
DIRT_WAG_SPRITE_CACHE_SIZEHow many wags' sprites SSdirt keeps (/datum/dirt_mask_job/var/sprite_frames), the ones baked most lately. Each holds every frame's pixels and where each pixel takes the still tail's dirt from.
/datum/dirt_jobWork SSdirt does a piece at a time (next_piece()), each piece in its own try/catch (make_piece()), so a piece that runtimes drops its job and can't hold up the jobs after it. A mask or template job is then cached as failed.
/var/SSdirtMakes the masks dirt is drawn with, a piece at a time, in the tick time other subsystems leave over. It keeps the newest of them.
/datum/dirt_template_job/metricThe plain human template dirt_metric() is measured from and underclothes coverage is judged on, built facing south, north and east and kept as SSdirt.human_template.
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_recentKeeps the entries asked for most lately. One asked for again goes to the back of the line, so what's most in play stays. It builds on bulge's cache, as tg has no bounded cache to build on.
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_recent/templatesKeeps the DIRT_TEMPLATE_CACHE_SIZE templates asked for most lately, so the bodies most in play keep theirs.
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_recent/sprite_readsKeeps the DIRT_SPRITE_READS sprite directions read most lately: the sprites of the bodies and clothes most in play.
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_facingsKeeps the newest DIRT_SPRITE_FACINGS sprites' facings, or whether each has one direction (sprite_one_way).
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_masksKeeps the newest DIRT_MASK_CACHE_SIZE masks.
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_recent/wet_iconsKeeps the DIRT_WET_CACHE_SIZE sweat multipliers asked for most lately, so the garments most in play keep theirs.
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_recent/draft_wordsKeeps the DIRT_DRAFT_TEXTS seed texts' draft-stream words used most lately.
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_recent/wag_spritesKeeps the DIRT_WAG_SPRITE_CACHE_SIZE wags' sprites baked most lately, so the tails most in play keep theirs. One dropped is read again at its next bake.
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_slimesKeeps the newest DIRT_SLIME_COLOURS slime materials. One dropped is made again from its colour when next asked for.
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_bloodsKeeps the newest DIRT_BLOOD_COLOURS blood materials. One dropped is made again from its colour when next asked for.
/datum/bulge_bounded_cache/dirt_rastersKeeps the newest DIRT_RASTER_CACHE_SIZE splats' rasters, each one text: its drawn() in each direction, in dirt_mask_directions() order, joined by DIRT_RASTER_SEPARATOR, empty where it isn't rasterised yet.
