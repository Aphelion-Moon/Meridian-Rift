/tg/ Station 13 - Modules - Types

modular_aphelion/modules/dirt/code/taur_coat.dm

/datum/dirt_canvas_view/taurA taur body in one direction as its contact coats read it: its silhouette, with every layer's states together, and each pixel's distance through it to the floor. It's also a surface as dirt_halo() reads one: the body's pixels lie on it, and those with a side off the body are its rim.
/proc/dirt_heap_pushAdds an entry to a binary heap kept least first: by key, then by value. It's hand-rolled for speed, as tg's /datum/heap calls a compare proc for each comparison and would need a list for each entry.
/proc/dirt_heap_popTakes the least entry off a binary heap (dirt_heap_push()) and returns its value. Its key was keys[1].
/datum/dirt_taur_drawsEvery random draw a taur coat makes. They don't depend on its level, so its levels nest. A speck's or a tongue's pick is kept as its random(), and turned into a place once the list it picks from is known (dirt_taur_picked()).
/proc/dirt_taur_drawsReturns a taur coat's random draws, from its stream. It takes the random draws for flecks too, though the coat doesn't use them, so the tongues keep their places in the stream.
/proc/dirt_taur_pickedReturns the item a taur coat's pick lands on: the draft's int(random() * (count - 1)).
/proc/dirt_taur_tonguesReturns how much higher a coat's line climbs at each column, indexed by column + 1, or null where it doesn't climb. These tongues are narrow places where the mud drags up the body, one column each, because a wider one reads as a seam. Level 2 has one, and each level after it one more. The first tongue on a column holds it.
/proc/dirt_taur_coat_thicknessReturns a coat's thickness on some of a taur body's pixels at one level, alist(index = thickness). The coat is solid under the feet. Up to a noise-roughened line, measured through the body, it goes from the body tone near the ground through a wide film band to haze. The line's noise is stretched along it, because round noise reads as camo. Extra kick-up film lies along the belly. One spray field breaks the coat up: film blots in the haze, and mud thrown up past the line in soft blots that thin out. A light material reaches less, keeps the body tone only in clumps by the contact, and breaks its film up.
/proc/dirt_taur_specksPuts soft specks beyond a taur coat's line. Each goes on the pixel its pick lands on, among those within 0.8 px either side of its distance past the line. It's kept clear of the coat and of every speck's rows and columns (dirt_place_speck()). The pixels are picked from column by column, as the draft picks them.
/proc/dirt_taur_coatReturns a contact coat on a taur body in one direction, on the 64x32 taur canvas. It holds levels 1 to level, each from the same random draws of the "taur::" stream (dirt_taur_draws()). They're merged by role rank, and the later level wins a tie. Each level's coat (dirt_taur_coat_thickness()) is cleaned up (dirt_cleanup()) and takes its material's edge, halo or tide. For walking (not dust) it takes its specks too (dirt_taur_specks()). A light material draws film at the contact, because core there would paint a light outline. A material's sheen draws nothing here, because it looks for a leg. It's drawn while drafting, by its field (/datum/dirt_canvas_field/taur_coat).
/proc/dirt_taur_coat_causeReturns the contact coat a general cause draws on a taur body (dirt_taur_coat()): walking's for walking and crawling, and dust's for dust. It draws on the fur, and on a taur suit's cloth as the coat of the body it hides. Returns null for any other cause.
/datum/dirt_canvas_field/taur_coatA taur body's contact coat in one direction, as dirt_taur_coat() draws it. It's drawn some of its body's pixels at a time (draw_rows()). A mask job draws it a band a piece (dirt_canvas_field_band()) and keeps it among its fields (/datum/dirt_mask_job/proc/field_for()).
