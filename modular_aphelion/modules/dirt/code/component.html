/datum/component/dirtinessThe dirt a person or an item carries, as splats (/datum/dirt_splat). SSdirt makes the masks they draw with. A person's skin splats draw through one /datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_skin on each limb they lie on, under what the person wears, and fade out beside it. The splats on their underclothes are kept with each piece. While the piece is worn and shows, they draw over it through one /datum/bodypart_overlay/dirt_underclothes on the chest. The splats on a person's parts draw with each part's own front layer. A taur body's pools and general deposits draw with its own images. The splats of tails, wings, ears, horns and other drawn parts draw with each part's images (appendages.dm). The hair's draw over the hair while it shows (hair.dm). A garment's splats draw inset over it while a person wears it. A taur suit's back splats and general deposits draw on the taur canvas round it too. Washing clears it, as it clears the floor's decals.
/proc/dirt_fresh_variantReturns a variant of a zone's mess none of some splats on that zone has, at random, or any once they have them all.
/datum/dirt_hidden_readWhat skin_hidden() last read a body's hiding from, and what it found.
/proc/dirt_body_readsReturns what every zone's limb and the eyes are drawn from now: alist(body zone = /obj/item/bodypart/proc/dirt_sprite_reads(), or null with no limb, "eyes" = dirt_eye_reads()).
/proc/dirt_worn_sourcesReturns the zone sources a garment asks for its masks with on its wearer (dirt_zone_sources()). While every limb and the eyes are drawn from what the wearer's carrier noted (worn_reads), each garment gets the same list. Its draws then match by comparing a few vars (dirt_worn_sources_match()). A wearer without a carrier gets a new list each time.
/proc/dirt_worn_sources_matchReturns whether a garment's wearer draws the sprites its masks were asked for with, as dirt_zone_sources_match() reads them. Masks asked for with the wearer's carrier's worn_sources match while worn_reads hold. Others are read in full. If a full read matches, the carrier notes the masks' zone sources and what the body is drawn from now, as rider_unchanged() notes again. It keeps its notes if they're still true. Later draws then compare against them.
/proc/cmp_dirt_splats_landedOrders splats by when they first landed, oldest first (/datum/dirt_splat/var/serial).
/proc/dirt_wearer_sideReturns the side a worn item's general deposits draw on, its wearer's dirtier side. A wearer without a carrier gives 1, as none is made mid-render. A carrier made later with another side has the item's masks made again.
/proc/dirt_stain_overReturns whether a garment's blood stain draws over its dirt (/datum/component/dirtiness/proc/stain_over()). A garment without a carrier holds no dirt, so its stain does.
/proc/dirt_examine_lineReturns the line examining a person or an item shows for its general dirt, or null with none. It names the heaviest material on the carriers by the sum of its deposits' levels. Its thickest deposit's level picks the words ("It is caked in mud."). Mess shows none.
