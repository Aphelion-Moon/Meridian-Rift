/datum/dirt_feature/stampsMarks put on the screen rather than on a limb, because a face and hands are too small to wrap dirt round. The face takes them on a human head only. A cause picks each cheek's and each hand's level, which needn't be the deposit's. dirt_overlay_stamps() lays them over the deposit's other marks, never on an eye or a mouth.
/proc/dirt_face_stampsReturns the face's smudges by direction, for a body whose right side is the dirtier: alist(direction = one list per level, 1 to 3, of list(x, y, DIRT_ROLE_* define)). They keep off the face's edge column (sideburns), the forehead (the hairline) and the mouth. Each body pixel has its film on the nose's side. Film may touch an eye's diagonal, but body never does. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_cover_faceReturns a covered face's darker parts by direction, for a body whose right side is the dirtier: alist(direction = list of list(x, y, DIRT_ROLE_* define)). The dirtier cheek's smudge runs on down to the jaw, off the eyes' rows and the hair's edge. It stays small because more dark under clean eyes reads as a beard. You should NEVER modify it.
/proc/dirt_face_stampReturns a dirty face's smudges in one direction at a level: alist(index = DIRT_ROLE_* define), on a 32x32 canvas.
/proc/dirt_cover_face_stampReturns a covered face's darker parts in one direction: alist(index = DIRT_ROLE_* define), on a 32x32 canvas.
/proc/dirt_mirror_directionReturns the direction a face's marks are mirrored from for the body's left side.
/proc/dirt_stamp_marksReturns face marks as alist(index = DIRT_ROLE_* define) on a 32x32 canvas, mirrored for the body's left side. Facing south they're mirrored about the face's middle, and in profile about the canvas's middle.
