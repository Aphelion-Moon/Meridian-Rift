/proc/dirt_cause_machineReturns machine work's features, in oil or grease. The fingertips take dirt, and below level 3 the dominant hand takes a level more. Parallel wipes run from the hip down the outer thigh, on cloth. From level 2 it adds a belly wipe and a cheek smudge. At level 3 both hands go dark past the wrist, with smears up the forearms. The dirtier thigh takes wider wipes over a worn patch, and the trouser fronts grey over. The belly and chest smears reach bare skin as film. Level 4 turns solid, black past the wrist. Its random draws are made after all the others.
/proc/dirt_cause_smokeReturns smoke's features, in soot or ash: the face and fingertips, and what settles on the shoulder tops, which bare skin takes as film. From level 2 the collar front and back darken, and bare skin there takes it as film. On cloth the cuffs at the sleeve ends also darken from level 2, and a diagonal wipe runs off the placket, two from level 3. At level 3 soot settles from above, down the chest from the collar and shoulders (coal miners, firefighters). The whole head greys, the dirtier shoulder blade and shoulder take patches, and sometimes both cheeks smudge. Level 4 goes harder, from draws made after every other. Bare skin then takes the whole coat from above, unbroken.
/proc/dirt_cause_sweatReturns sweat's features, on cloth only, because on bare skin sweat is a sheen, not a stain. Cloth soaks through where it holds sweat, in soft-edged rounded masses that grow with level rather than darken. Level 1 wets the pits. Level 2 makes them bigger, running down the side seams, and adds a patch under the collar and one down the spine. Level 3 wets the back from the shoulder blades to the small of the back. Each variant sweats its own way: down the back or on the chest, tall along the spine or wide between the shoulder blades. One side of the back is wetter, and the dirtier side's pit is heavier.
/proc/dirt_cause_slimedReturns a slime's attack from above. It latches on, so ooze sits where it clung, from the shoulders down. A big lump sits on the side it grabbed from, with a narrow one on that arm and one on the upper back. Smaller ones sit on the other shoulder and the chest. Each is darkest underneath with a glint on top. Drips run down from them in strands that end in bulbs, and spatter lies round them, with bare cloth between. It's never a coat, because an even one reads as a dyed shirt and a band over both shoulders as a shawl.
/proc/dirt_slime_lumpReturns one of a slime's lumps from above: list(limb, theta, h, radius round, radius up, seed). The caller draws its theta, and this proc draws the rest from the stream, in that order.
/proc/dirt_slime_areasReturns where slime touches for a contact on a zone, the struck spot first: each list(limb, theta, h, radius round, radius up). The radii are in pixels. The chest takes a whole front, as for a fall on your face, and the back a whole back, as for lying on your back. The head takes the face's sides and the neck. An arm takes its front and its outer side, with the torso beside it. A leg takes its front and back. The areas never go round a whole arm (a sleeve, a glove) or a whole leg at the boot (socks, gaiters). The chest's and the back's never go down the legs (overalls).
/proc/dirt_cause_slime_contactReturns slime on one part of the body, for a hit or contact there, or a fall or lying down in it. Level 1 leaves a lump where it struck, level 2 a bigger lumpy patch, and level 3 the part's whole contact surface (dirt_slime_areas()). Each area is lumps with gaps between, darkest underneath with a glint on top. It's never an even coat, which reads as dye. Drips run down off the lumps, and spatter lies round the struck spot. Drips from the head run off the jaw and down the neck.
