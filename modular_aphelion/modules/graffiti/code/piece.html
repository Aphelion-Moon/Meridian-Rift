GRAFFITI_FLAT_MAX_COLORSMost colours a flattened art may hold: as many as two grid characters can index.
GRAFFITI_ALPHA_FLOORThe least alpha paint keeps, 20%, so translucent paint still shows: a lower one is raised to it, and 0 is refused.
/proc/graffiti_colorReturns a colour in its canonical graffiti form: lowercase #rrggbb when its alpha reaches GRAFFITI_ALPHA_CEILING, the alpha placed graffiti draws opaque paint at, and #rrggbbaa below it, raised to GRAFFITI_ALPHA_FLOOR when lower. So a stored colour's real alpha is the ceiling when it has seven characters, and its own alpha when it has nine.
/proc/graffiti_palette_packPacks canonical colours into one string of nine-character #rrggbbaa entries, the form placed art keeps its palette in.
/proc/graffiti_palette_unpackUnpacks a palette graffiti_palette_pack() wrote. Only its lowercase spelling is read, so an art has one packed form and one hash.
/proc/graffiti_encode_flatEncodes palette indices as a flat art grid.
/proc/graffiti_decode_flatDecodes a flat art grid that graffiti_encode_flat() wrote. Hostile input is refused before anything is expanded, and decoding stops at pixel_count whatever the runs claim.
/proc/graffiti_merge_fingerprintReturns a fingerprint of what merging gives: the alpha ceiling and the hash of one fixed merge of opaque and translucent paint, glowing and not, made once. The map file keeps it beside its stack cache, so a cache written before merges changed is set aside rather than show stacks under keys today's merges wouldn't give.
/proc/graffiti_flat_codesReturns the characters a flat grid writes, built once, so encoding looks each run's characters up instead of cutting text and calling custom_sprite_canvas_code() for every run, and the glow of art that glows nowhere at each width.
/proc/graffiti_art_hashReturns an art's content identity, which tiles showing the same art share.
/proc/graffiti_art_pixelsCounts an art's painted pixels.
/proc/graffiti_art_decodeDecodes an art, such as one read from a map file, when it can be drawn: a one- or two-tile width, a palette of 1 to GRAFFITI_FLAT_MAX_COLORS colours, and a grid and glow mask that both cover every pixel.
/proc/graffiti_unlit_glowReturns the glow of art that glows nowhere, as graffiti_encode_flat() writes it and decoded, which graffiti_flat_codes() makes once for each width, so decoded arts that glow nowhere share one list.
/proc/graffiti_blendBlends a colour over another as placed graffiti shows them, in their real alphas: GRAFFITI_ALPHA_CEILING for opaque paint and its own for translucent paint. The standard "over" operator gives the alpha and the colour, and an alpha that reaches the ceiling is stored opaque.
/proc/graffiti_merge_artsMerges arts of one width into one, bottom to top.
/proc/graffiti_flattenFlattens a piece's layers into one art, or only as much of them as a paint job has revealed.
/proc/graffiti_layer_recordsDecodes a piece's layers for graffiti_merge_arts(), each against the piece's whole palette, glowing on every pixel or on none. A paint job keeps its piece's records, so its steps never decode the layers again.
/proc/graffiti_flatten_recordsFlattens decoded layers into one art, or only as much of them as a paint job has revealed. Nothing is encoded or decoded on the way, so it is graffiti_flatten() for layers already decoded.
/proc/graffiti_split_wideSplits a wide art into the arts of its two tiles.
/proc/graffiti_trim_wideNarrows a two-tile piece to one tile when no layer paints its right tile, as a wide canvas left blank there saves.
/proc/graffiti_validate_pieceStrictly validates a library piece, as Export writes it and Import reads it. Nothing is salvaged, and messages never repeat uploaded text.
/proc/graffiti_clean_textCleans player text for a piece's name, description or layer names, so that cleaning it again changes nothing.
/proc/graffiti_encode_pieceReturns a canonical piece as the text Export writes, format and version first.
