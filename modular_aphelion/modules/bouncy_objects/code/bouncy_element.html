Define Details

#define
BOUNCE_MAX_DISTANCE

Furthest, in tiles, one bounce travels while it has gravity. Mostly trims mass drivers and explosions, which throw far further than hands do.

#define
BOUNCE_MAX_HOP_HEIGHT

Tallest hop arc, in pixels.

#define
BOUNCE_MAX_PER_TICK

Most bounces (rebounds, ejection hops and tosses) that may start in one tick, shared by every bouncy object.

Keeps mass throws, such as an explosion scattering a pile of casings and rods, from flooding SSthrowing, the timer subsystem, clients and the sound system. Objects past the budget settle where they are.

#define
BOUNCE_MINIMUM_DISTANCE

Bounces shorter than this many tiles are dropped rather than animated as an imperceptible twitch.

#define
BOUNCE_REBOUND_DELAY

Delay between an impact and the rebound it causes.

Must be nonzero. Impacts are handled inside [/datum/thrownthing/proc/finalize], and that throw's Destroy() clears throwing and the SSthrowing entry of its movable. A rebound started synchronously would be cancelled by that cleanup.

#define
BOUNCE_SLIDE_PIXEL_LIMIT

Furthest, in pixels, a sub-tile slide may move an object from its base offset, so it stays over its own tile.
