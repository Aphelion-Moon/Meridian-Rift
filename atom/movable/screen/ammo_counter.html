proc
clear_oth
()

Clears the OTH backing, digits and indicator, leaving only the icon_state behind.

Always go through this rather than cut_overlays(): that empties overlays behind update_icon()'s back, so managed_overlays still lists the digits that were just wiped. The next paint of the same gun then builds an identical list, update_icon() takes it for already drawn and skips it, and the counter stays blank until something happens to paint a different set.
