/proc/derive_body_marking_speciesGives every marking in a marking set the species of its sets: the union of their recommended_species, or any species when one of them names none. A marking in no set keeps the species it declares. Runs once both lists are built, so a set and its markings never disagree about who may wear them. Only choices and validation read the result, never loading or drawing.
/proc/remove_erp_thingsIf the "Remove ERP Interaction" config is disabled, remove ERP things from various lists
