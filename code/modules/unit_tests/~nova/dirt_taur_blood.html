/datum/unit_test/dirt_taur_bloodBlood below a taur's waist starts one row under its garment's stain, at every height. The stain is cut at the waist. The rider's height filter then moves the stain's last row while the taur body under it stays put, so the start row follows the height (dirt_blood_seam_row()). This test works out the stain's last row from the crop and the height maps the game puts on it. So a change to either, or to the start rows, fails. Drawing the blood must also settle. What it's drawn from is read on the render path, and checked again as worn sprites change. If the two disagree, the taur redraws forever. That's most likely where the most is read: a bloodied uniform under a bloodied suit, and a taur-fitted apron that carries the blood on its cloth and on the body under its hem. A taur-fitted suit draws its share through a carrier of its own, made outside the render. A carrier made for the blood alone goes once the blood is washed off or its garment is taken off. The newest landing draws on top (check_newest_on_top()), and a garment holding the blood list of the one it replaced counts as blood landing (check_shared_blood_list()).
