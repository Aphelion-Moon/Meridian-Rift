/datum/unit_test/dirt_zone_sourcesThe game's limbs feed the dirt template the sprites the corpus names, so the parity manifest's surfaces are the ones the game builds: a consistent human gives the corpus's human_m sources, and the same body at the Muscular size gives human_muscular's, its size sheet included.
/mob/living/carbon/human/consistent/dirt_redraw_fixtureA body that notes what SSdirt draws again on it.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_mask_queueSSdirt makes mess masks in the background and caches them: the first ask queues them, finish_all() makes them, and the body that asked is drawn again. The masks are made from the splats as they were when asked for, whatever happens to the splats meanwhile. Skin's masks are clipped to each limb, and cloth's are made too. If a request's limb names a missing state, the request is dropped, cached as failed and never queued again. The queue goes on past it. Masks asked for on more body types than the template cache holds are all made, though templates drop while they wait.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_skin_messThe mess on bare skin draws through the limbs' own images. A landing gives each limb it reaches one container image at DIRT_SKIN_LAYER, holding the baked image SSdirt makes, and gives the other limbs nothing. Washing takes the mess off, even while its masks are still being made. Mess on every zone at once keeps the chest's splats in its one container. The body's images share one holder per layer, and their blockers one group (prepare_bodypart_overlays()). So skin dirt adds a holder and a group, and underclothes dirt the same, four at most. Past the top level, a shot starts another splat, of a variant the zone doesn't have yet. A steady draw only compares what each is drawn from. Even so, if a limb is drawn from other sprites with no limb swapped, the masks are made again for what the body draws. The cases are the chest at another physique, a restyled arm, which the chest checks, and eyes drawn from another state.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_worn_messThe mess on clothes. A shot at a covered region lands on the outermost garment over it. The garment draws it inset in its own worn wrapper, where the bulge can copy it. Washing the garment takes it off. If only underclothes cover a region, the shot lands on the piece that shows the most of it. It's drawn over that piece, kept to its pixels, and kept with the piece while it's hidden or swapped. Skin mess lies under the underclothes, and fades out beside them.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_mess_regionsA shot on someone offers the zones mess draws on them (dirt_mess_regions()): all seven on a human, none below the waist on a taur, the groin and butt through a wall-stuck portal and the penis alone through a one-part portal, while it draws. Odd bodies get only what draws on them: a monkey nothing, a kobold everything, a human without legs no thighs. A full amount from size 4 testicles, as climax() spends it, lands at the top level.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_part_messParts take their own share of the mess aimed at them and draw it with their own images. The checks below follow a share through portals, relays, state and layer changes, bulges, underclothes put on, washes and the part's removal. A part hidden while underclothes carry mess, or drawn by a gloryhole's relay alone, doesn't shape the skin's mess.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_taur_messA back shot on a taur lands on the rider's back and along the taur body's: the rider's skin and the taur body's slot each take a splat of the same variant. The game reads the rider and the taur body as the parity corpus names them. Once the masks are made, the taur body's images carry the pools beside them. A taur suit takes the pools itself, inset over its 64x32 sprite. The rider's mess on the suit lands on the body's tile, not 16 px off. Removing the taur body takes its pools with it. A taur's penis drawn only behind the body takes no share.
