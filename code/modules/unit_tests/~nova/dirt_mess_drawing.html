/datum/unit_test/dirt_zone_sourcesThe game's limbs feed the dirt template the sprites the corpus names, so the parity manifest's surfaces are the ones the game builds: a consistent human gives the corpus's human_m sources, and the same body at the Muscular size gives human_muscular's, its size sheet included.
/mob/living/carbon/human/consistent/dirt_redraw_fixtureA body that notes what SSdirt draws again on it.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_mask_queueSSdirt makes mess masks in the background and caches them: the first ask queues them, finish_all() makes them, and the body that asked is drawn again. They're made from the splats as they were when asked for, whatever happens to them meanwhile. Skin's masks are clipped to each limb and cloth's cover the canvas, and both match the parity manifest's cases, one splat or two merged, the second drawn over a first cached from an earlier ask. A request whose limb names a missing state is dropped, cached as failed, and never queued again, and the queue goes on past it. Masks asked for on more body types than the template cache holds are all made, though templates drop while they wait.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_skin_messThe mess on bare skin draws through the limbs' own images: a landing gives each limb it reaches one container image at DIRT_SKIN_LAYER, holding the baked image SSdirt makes, and the other limbs nothing. Washing takes it off, even while its masks are still being made: they finish without bringing it back. Every zone at once adds one overlay per limb that carries mess, the head, the chest and the legs, so four at most, the chest's splats in its one container. A shot's amount adds levels to the zone's newest splat, and past the top one what's left starts another, of a variant the zone doesn't have yet. A limb drawn from other sprites with none swapped has the masks made again for what the body draws, though a steady draw only compares what each is drawn from: the chest at another physique, a restyled arm, which the chest checks, and eyes drawn from another state.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_worn_messThe mess on clothes. A shot at a covered region lands on the outermost garment over it, which draws it inset in its own worn wrapper, where the bulge can copy it, from its wearer's cloth masks: moved to a digitigrade lizard, from the lizard's. Washing the garment takes it off. A region covered only by underclothes takes it on the piece showing the most of it, drawn over that piece and kept to its pixels, and kept with it while it's hidden or swapped. Skin mess lies under the underclothes, and fades out beside them.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_genital_testThe dirt tests whose bodies need genitals, built from DNA as a real character's.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_mess_regionsA shot on someone offers the zones mess draws on them (dirt_mess_regions()): all seven on a human, none below the waist on a taur, the groin and butt through a wall-stuck portal and the penis alone through a one-part portal, while it draws. Odd bodies get only what draws on them: a monkey nothing, a kobold everything, a human without legs no thighs. A full amount from size 4 testicles, as climax() spends it, lands at the top level.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_part_messParts take their own share of the mess aimed at them. Breasts drawn above all clothing take theirs over a jumpsuit, which takes the rest, and draw it with their own front layer. Through a one-part portal a shot lands on the penis alone, and the relay draws it; a wall-stuck relay draws no legs, and draws the mess on the hips and butt, on the skin and on a jumpsuit, under a winter coat too, but not a skirt, nor ever a bulge: a shot lands on what the relay shows under the skirt. A share follows the part: an erection draws it again for the erect sprite, a butt set to draw above its underwear is read again at its new layer, and a part removed takes its splats with it. A bulge's cloth over boxers draws their mess over itself, a bulge's in a jumpsuit draws the jumpsuit's again once another splat lands, and testicles a bulge look draws lose theirs to a wash. A butt shot takes the butt's share facing north, where its front image draws over the skin, and in profile, where its back image draws under it, shapes the skin's mess past the body's edge. Boxers put on over a butt drawn below them leave its share nothing to draw; put on under a vulva drawn above them, they have its share made again for cloth. A part hidden while underclothes carry mess no longer shapes the skin's.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_taur_messA back shot on a taur lands on the rider's back and along the taur body's: the rider's skin and the taur body's slot each take a splat of the same variant. The game reads the rider and the taur body as the parity corpus names them. Once the masks are made the taur body's images carry the pools beside them. A Big Legs body, its legs under the rider and no taur back, takes the shot on the rider alone. A taur suit takes the pools itself, inset over its 64x32 sprite, and the rider's mess on it lands on the body's tile, not 16 px off. Removing the taur body takes its pools with it. A taur's penis drawn only behind the body takes no share.
/datum/unit_test/dirt_mess_capA person carries DIRT_MESS_MAX_SPLATS splats at most, counted across their skin, their parts and what they wear, and past it the oldest go first. A human in a jumpsuit and a labcoat, breasts drawn above all clothing, takes DIRT_MESS_MAX_SPLATS + 1 huge shots: on the face's skin, on the labcoat with the breasts' share, and on the jumpsuit. At the cap a landing takes the oldest splat off before anything is drawn, so a face landing that evicts a face splat asks for one mask at most, and a heavy shot that tops the face's newest splat and starts another asks for exactly one, counted with the mask cache at its limit. Drawn, it all adds four overlays at most, and nothing to tg's shared limb icon cache: a dirty limb's images are kept on its mob, which a wash drops, mess landed again draws afresh, and a limb severed or swapped takes with it, the new limb drawn afresh, even every limb at once as a species change swaps them.
