#define
GARMENT_FITS_DEFERRED

Whether a garment's first fit to a new fit waits for SSgarment_fitting, the garment drawn as it is until it's done, rather than being made as it's drawn. A dummy, drawn for snapshots such as record photos and character previews, is fitted as it's drawn either way. Comment it out to make every fit as it's drawn.
